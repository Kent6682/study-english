## 課文

**¶1** Have you ever wanted a video game that could help you stay active? *Pikmin Bloom* is a free-to-download app that **rewards** players for walking **outdoors**. As you move, you raise Pikmin—tiny, plant-like creatures—collect flower petals, and leave **colorful** **trails** on a real-world map.

**¶2** Each Pikmin starts as a seedling and grows as you walk. After enough steps, you can pull it out from the ground to add it to your **squad**. Your Pikmin find in-game fruit that you can *turn into* nectar to *make flowers bloom* on their heads. Then, you can collect the petals and plant them as you keep walking.

**¶3** As your **bond** with a Pikmin grows, it may **earn** a small item *based on* where it was found—these Pikmin are then called Decor Pikmin. For example, one from a café might wear a coffee cup. Many players join special events to find new ones. Pikmin may also bring postcards back from **nearby** landmarks—a small surprise that's waiting at the end of every walk.

**¶4** Taiwan has one of the most active *Pikmin Bloom* **communities**. Local players showed strong interest from the start, and that energy has *resulted in* a calendar that's full of events. In October 2025, Kaohsiung hosted a Pikmin Bloom Tour. This past May, fans gathered in Chiayi for a Mini Walk that was part of the Chiayi Arts Festival. Players explored the city and stopped at eight **locations** to earn Gold Seedlings. Every seedling can grow into a one-of-a-kind Decor Pikmin.

**¶5** *Pikmin Bloom*'s social features *keep players connected* beyond local events. Teams can battle giant **mushrooms** at real-world locations and earn shared rewards. With the Party Walk feature, players walk together and share step counts in real time. Players can also send postcards to friends anywhere in the world—each one makes a 12-hour trip with a Pikmin before it lands in a friend's **virtual** garden.

**¶6** *Pikmin Bloom* turns everyday steps into shared **adventures** that connect people across borders. For **casual** walkers and **dedicated** Pikmin collectors *alike*, every **journey** can be something fun and exciting.

## 關鍵句型

1. **turn A into B**：把 A 變成 B（A turn into B：A 變成 B）
2. **make + O + 原形 V**：使／讓……；強迫……
3. **based on + N／名詞子句**：根據、依照
4. **result in（＋結果）vs. result from（＋原因）**
5. **keep + O + Adj.／V-ing／p.p.**：使維持某狀態
6. **A and B alike**：A 和 B 都
