# Unit 9｜PinkPantheress: Queen of the Board and the Beat
## 粉紅豹女孩：棋盤高手，樂壇新女王

> 焦點人物 ★★★｜—Eric Chen

## 一、課文與翻譯

### 第一部分

**¶1** In July 2025, a photo went **viral** online. A young pop star had just won a **chess** **tournament** in Kent, and her fans were **amazed**—few knew that she played chess **at all**. That star was PinkPantheress.

> 2025 年 7 月，一張照片在網路上爆紅。一位年輕的流行歌手剛在肯特郡贏得一場西洋棋錦標賽，她的粉絲都大吃一驚——幾乎沒有人知道她會下西洋棋。那位明星就是 PinkPantheress。

**¶2** So who is PinkPantheress? Her real name is Victoria Beverley Walker. She was born in 2001, in Bath, England, and **grew up** in Kent. Her **unique** stage name came from a question on the British quiz show *The Chase* and is also **a nod to** *The Pink Panther* film **series**.

> 那麼 PinkPantheress 是誰？她的本名是維多莉亞・貝芙莉・沃克。她 2001 年出生於英格蘭的巴斯，在肯特郡長大。她獨特的藝名來自英國益智節目《The Chase》裡的一道題目，同時也是在向《粉紅豹》系列電影致敬。

**¶3** Chess **runs in her family**—her aunt, Susan Lalic, is a five-time British Women's Chess **Champion** and an International Master. PinkPantheress has said that she was going to **pursue** a **career** in chess—but music came first. In 2021, she began posting her own songs on SoundCloud and TikTok when she was a university student in London, and fans around the world quickly **took notice**. Her first mixtape, *To Hell with It* (2021), reached the UK charts and earned her the BBC Sound of 2022 title.

> 下西洋棋是她的家族傳統——她的阿姨蘇珊・拉利奇曾五度獲得英國女子西洋棋冠軍，也是國際大師。PinkPantheress 曾說過，她原本打算從事西洋棋的職業——但音樂先找上了她。2021 年，她還在倫敦念大學時，開始在 SoundCloud 和 TikTok 上發布自己的歌曲，世界各地的粉絲很快就注意到了她。她的第一張混音專輯《To Hell with It》（2021）登上英國排行榜，並為她贏得「BBC 2022 年度新聲」的頭銜。

### 第二部分

**¶4** PinkPantheress **produces** much of her own music, and her sound **blends** pop, drum and bass, and UK garage, with samples from 1990s and 2000s songs. Her music is **catchy**, hard to forget, and has received strong praise. Her second mixtape, *Fancy That* (2025), is **proof** of this. It earned two Grammy **nominations** and reached number three on the UK Albums Chart. Singles like "Stateside" and "Illegal" **found audiences far beyond the UK**, and she now has **hundreds of millions of** streams.

> PinkPantheress 自己製作大部分的音樂，她的曲風融合了流行樂、鼓打貝斯和英式車庫，並取樣自 1990 年代和 2000 年代的歌曲。她的音樂琅琅上口、令人難忘，也獲得高度讚揚。她的第二張混音專輯《Fancy That》（2025）就是最好的證明。這張專輯獲得兩項葛萊美獎提名，並在英國專輯排行榜上拿下第三名。〈Stateside〉和〈Illegal〉等單曲在英國以外的地區也找到了廣大聽眾，她現在的串流播放次數已達數億次。

**¶5** **Yet** success did **not** come **without** challenges along the way. Years of loud music gradually damaged PinkPantheress's hearing. She has now lost 80 percent of the hearing in her right ear, so she must check the **volume** very carefully at every live show.

> 然而，成功的路上並非沒有挑戰。多年的大音量音樂逐漸損害了 PinkPantheress 的聽力。她的右耳現在已經喪失了八成聽力，所以每場現場演出她都必須非常小心地確認音量。

**¶6** In February 2026, at the BRIT Awards, PinkPantheress **was named** British Producer of the Year. She was **the youngest person and the first woman ever to** receive this **honor**. Like in chess, PinkPantheress has shown that success in music comes from **talent**, hard work, and making the right moves at the right time.

> 2026 年 2 月，在全英音樂獎上，PinkPantheress 獲選為英國年度製作人。她是史上最年輕、也是第一位獲得這項殊榮的女性。就像在西洋棋裡一樣，PinkPantheress 證明了音樂上的成功來自天賦、努力，以及在對的時間走對的棋步。

## 三、必考句型

1. **grow up** + 地點／Adj.／V-ing（不及物）
2. **a nod to** sth（向……致敬）
3. sth **runs in the/sb's family**（家族遺傳）
4. **few** ... **at all**（幾乎沒有人……）
5. **Yet** + S + V（然而，正式書面轉折）
6. **not ... without** + N/V-ing（雙重否定＝必然伴隨）
7. **be named** + N（被選為）
8. **the first / the youngest + N + ever to V**（史上第一位／最年輕……）
9. **find** + audiences + 地點（作品打入某市場）
10. **earn** + 人 + 物（為某人贏得）
