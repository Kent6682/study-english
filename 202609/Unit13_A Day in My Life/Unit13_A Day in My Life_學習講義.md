# Unit 13 ★★★ A Day in My Life 我生活中的一天
> 寫作素養專欄 Learning to Write｜範文：An Unexpected Day｜—Karl Buckley

## 🎯 寫作任務
寫一篇至少 120 字的英文短文，描述你某一天的生活，內容需包括：
1. 日常作息與活動
2. 當天發生的一件有趣或特別的事件
3. 你對這件事的反應或感受

**三大寫作提醒**
| 提醒 | 做法 | 範文示範 |
|---|---|---|
| ① 時態 | 當天事件用過去式；例行習慣加 as usual／normally | I went to cram school **as usual** |
| ② 時間順序 | 用時間副詞、片語、子句串起事件 | at first → In the morning → Next → After school → On my way home → Tomorrow → For now |
| ③ 連接詞 | 用連接詞與轉折語讓句子銜接 | because, but luckily, which, since, so, when, In fact |

## 📖 範文 An Unexpected Day（起承轉合）
| 段落 | 英文 | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| 起（平凡早晨） | Today was a **normal** day at first. I woke up at 6:30 a.m. and had some breakfast. I almost missed my stop on the bus to school because I fell **asleep**, but luckily, I woke up just in time. In the morning, I had math class, which was quite **dull**. Next, history class was a lot more interesting since the teacher told great stories. | 今天一開始是很普通的一天。我早上六點半起床，吃了點早餐。我在搭公車上學的途中睡著了，差點坐過站，幸好及時醒來。早上我上了數學課，內容相當沉悶。接著上的歷史課有趣多了，因為老師講了很精彩的故事。 |
| 承＋轉（意外的小貓） | After school, I went to cram school as usual for a couple of hours. On my way home, I noticed a small cat sitting under a car near my house. I didn't recognize the cat, so I was a little surprised and **curious**. It didn't run away when I got closer. In fact, it followed me. | 放學後，我照常去補習班上了幾個小時的課。回家的路上，我注意到一隻小貓坐在我家附近的一輛車子底下。我沒見過那隻貓，所以有點驚訝，也很好奇。我走近時，牠並沒有跑走。事實上，牠還跟著我走。 |
| 合（決定與收尾） | I didn't really know what to do. It looked hungry, so I brought it home. I gave it some food and asked my mom what she thought. Tomorrow, we'll decide on the best course of action. For now, I have to finish my homework and get ready for bed—it's another early start tomorrow. | 我實在不知道該怎麼辦。牠看起來很餓，所以我把牠帶回家。我餵了牠一些食物，並問媽媽有什麼想法。明天，我們會決定最好的處理方式。現在，我得先寫完作業、準備上床睡覺——明天又要一大早出門了。 |

## 🧠 核心單字
| 單字 | 音標 | 詞性・中義 | 例句 | 搭配 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| normal | [ˋnɔrml] | adj. 普通的、正常的 | A little rain in spring is completely normal. | back to normal 恢復正常 |
| asleep | [əˋslip] | adj. 睡著的 | The baby fell asleep right after dinner. | fall asleep／be fast asleep |
| dull | [dʌl] | adj. 沉悶的、無聊的 | The movie was so dull that I kept checking the clock. | a dull class／a dull pain |
| curious | [ˋkjʊrɪəs] | adj. 好奇的 | The little girl was curious about everything she saw at the zoo. | be curious about／curiosity |
| cram | [kræm] | n., v. 臨時死記硬背 | I went to cram school as usual. | cram school 補習班 |

## 🔗 必背片語（Info Box）
- **wake up**（不及物）醒來；wake–woke–woken
- **in time** 及時、來得及 ≠ on time 準時
- **a couple of** 幾個、兩三個
- **on one's way home** 在回家路上（home 為副詞，不加 to）
- **notice sb/sth + V／V-ing** 注意到某人／某物在做……
- **decide on sth** 決定、選定；**a course of action** 行動方針、做法

## ⚡ Language Notes：wh- 名詞子句 vs. wh- + to V
1. **wh- 名詞子句**（直述句語序 wh- + S + V）可作主詞、受詞、補語
   - How the accident happened **is** still unclear.（主詞，單數動詞）
   - We all wondered why the class was canceled.（受詞）
   - My question is who is responsible for this.（補語）
   - 課文：I asked my mom **what she thought**.（ask＋人＋wh- 子句）
   - ✗ I don't know where is he. ✓ I don't know where he is.
2. **wh- + to V**（主詞相同時簡化）
   - I didn't really know what I should do. ➔ I didn't really know **what to do**.
   - How to say no is a skill everyone needs.／I haven't decided when to tell her the news.／The real challenge is how to keep everyone happy.
