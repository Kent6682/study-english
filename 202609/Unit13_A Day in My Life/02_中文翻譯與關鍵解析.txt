202609 Unit 13｜A Day in My Life 我生活中的一天
範文 An Unexpected Day（意料之外的一天）中文翻譯與關鍵解析

==================================================
一、全文中文翻譯
==================================================
[第一段]
今天一開始是很普通的一天。我早上六點半起床，吃了點早餐。我在搭公車上學的途中睡著了，差點坐過站，幸好及時醒來。早上我上了數學課，內容相當沉悶。接著上的歷史課有趣多了，因為老師講了很精彩的故事。

[第二段]
放學後，我照常去補習班上了幾個小時的課。回家的路上，我注意到一隻小貓坐在我家附近的一輛車子底下。我沒見過那隻貓，所以有點驚訝，也很好奇。我走近時，牠並沒有跑走。事實上，牠還跟著我走。

[第三段]
我實在不知道該怎麼辦。牠看起來很餓，所以我把牠帶回家。我餵了牠一些食物，並問媽媽有什麼想法。明天，我們會決定最好的處理方式。現在，我得先寫完作業、準備上床睡覺——明天又要一大早出門了。

==================================================
二、逐句關鍵解析
==================================================
S01 Today was a normal day at first.
  • at first「一開始」暗示後面會出現轉折（意外事件），是記敘文開頭埋伏筆的好用片語。
  • normal（普通的）＝ ordinary／usual；反義 unusual、unexpected。
S02 I woke up at 6:30 a.m. and had some breakfast.
  • wake 三態 wake–woke–woken；wake up 不及物「醒來」，wake sb up 及物「叫醒某人」。
  • have breakfast 三餐前不加冠詞；加 some 表「吃了一點」。
S03 I almost missed my stop on the bus to school because I fell asleep, but luckily, I woke up just in time.
  • miss one's stop 坐過站；almost＋過去式＝差一點就……（實際上沒發生）。
  • fall asleep 睡著（asleep 是敘述用形容詞，不能放名詞前：an asleep baby ✗ → a sleeping baby ✓）。
  • in time 及時、來得及 ≠ on time 準時；just 加強「剛好」。
S04 In the morning, I had math class, which was quite dull.
  • , which 非限定關代，補充說明 math class；逗號後不能用 that。
  • dull 沉悶的＝boring。
S05 Next, history class was a lot more interesting since the teacher told great stories.
  • a lot／much／far／even＋比較級，加強「……得多」；very 不能修飾比較級。
  • since 在此是「因為」（＝because），不是「自從」。
S06 After school, I went to cram school as usual for a couple of hours.
  • as usual 照常、像往常一樣；a couple of＝兩三個、幾個。
S07 On my way home, I noticed a small cat sitting under a car near my house.
  • on one's way home：home 是副詞，不加 to（on my way to school 則要 to）。
  • notice sb/sth＋V-ing（看到正在進行）／＋原形 V（看到整個過程）。
S08 I didn't recognize the cat, so I was a little surprised and curious.
  • recognize 認出（以前見過的）；so 連接因果。
  • surprised／curious 描述人的感受；be curious about sth。
S09 It didn't run away when I got closer.
  • run away 逃跑；get closer 走近；when 時間副詞子句。
S10 In fact, it followed me.
  • In fact 事實上：補充更出乎意料的事實（遞進），不是轉折。
S11 I didn't really know what to do.
  • what to do＝what I should do（wh- + to V 名詞片語作受詞）。
  • didn't really know 語氣比 didn't know 委婉，表示「真的拿不定主意」。
S12 It looked hungry, so I brought it home.
  • look＋形容詞（連綴動詞）；bring sth home：home 是副詞，不加 to。
S13 I gave it some food and asked my mom what she thought.
  • ask＋人＋wh- 名詞子句，子句用直述句語序（what she thought，不是 what did she think）。
  • give sb sth 雙受詞。
S14 Tomorrow, we'll decide on the best course of action.
  • decide on sth（仔細考慮後）決定、選定；decide to V 決定做某事。
  • a course of action 行動方針、做法。
S15 For now, I have to finish my homework and get ready for bed—it's another early start tomorrow.
  • for now 目前、暫時；get ready for sth 為……做準備。
  • 破折號補充原因；現在式 it's 搭配 tomorrow 表已排定的行程。
  • 時態轉換：全文敘述用過去式，最後回到「現在」（have to）與「未來」（we'll），是日記型記敘文的收尾公式。

==================================================
三、Language Notes：wh- 名詞子句 vs. wh- + to V 名詞片語
==================================================
1. wh- 名詞子句可作主詞、受詞或補語：
   How the accident happened is still unclear.（主詞，動詞用單數）
   We all wondered why the class was canceled.（受詞）
   My question is who is responsible for this.（主詞補語）
   本課：I ... asked my mom what she thought.
2. 名詞子句不倒裝：I don't know where he is.（✗ where is he）
3. 主詞相同時可簡化為 wh- + to V：
   I didn't really know what I should do. ➔ I didn't really know what to do.
   How to say no is a skill everyone needs.／I haven't decided when to tell her the news.／The real challenge is how to keep everyone happy.
   ⚠ why 通常不接 to V（why to do ✗）。

==================================================
四、延伸補充詞彙
==================================================
unexpected adj. 出乎意料的｜ordinary adj. 普通的｜routine n. 例行公事｜stray cat 流浪貓
oversleep v. 睡過頭｜on time 準時｜in time 及時｜at last 最後｜later that evening 那天晚上稍後
in fact 事實上｜for now 目前｜get ready for 為……做準備｜decide on 決定

==================================================
五、課後練習
==================================================
Step 1 參考答案（範例）：
1. This morning, I woke up at 6:30 a.m. and had some breakfast.
2. In the morning, I had math class, which was quite dull. Next, history class was a lot more interesting since the teacher told great stories.
3. After school, I went to cram school as usual.
4. On my way home, I noticed a small cat sitting under a car near my house.
5. I have to finish my homework and then get ready for bed.

Practice A 參考答案：C → A → F → D → B → E
（I woke up early → On my way to school → In the morning → after school → Next, I went home → Later that evening）

Practice B 寫作檢核清單：
□ 至少 120 字、分段　□ 日常作息＋一件意外事件＋自己的感受
□ 敘述以過去式為主，例行習慣用 as usual／normally
□ 時間順序詞：at first, in the morning, next, after school, on my way home, later that evening, for now
□ 連接詞：because, so, but, since, when, which；轉折語 luckily, in fact
