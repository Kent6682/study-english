202609 Unit 13 ★★★｜寫作素養專欄 Learning to Write：英文寫作課
A Day in My Life 我生活中的一天 —Karl Buckley

【寫作提示】
每個人的一天通常由固定的作息與日常活動組成，但生活中偶爾也會出現意想不到的小插曲，使原本平凡的一天變得特別。
請寫一篇英文短文來描述你某一天的生活，文長至少 120 字，可分段書寫。內容需包括：
  ☑ 日常作息與活動
  ☑ 當天發生的一件有趣或特別的事件
  ☑ 你對這件事的反應或感受

【Step 1・發想與單句練習】
1. When did you wake up this morning?
   This morning, I woke up ________ and ________.
2. How did you start your day at school?
   In the morning, I had ________ class, which was ________. Next, ________ was a lot more ________.
3. What did you do after school today?
   After school, I went to ________ as usual.
4. What was something interesting that happened today?
   On my way home, I noticed ________.
5. What do you usually do before going to bed?
   I have to ________ and then ________.

寫作小提醒：
① 時態使用：描述當天發生的事件時，以過去式為主；若描述平時的習慣或例行活動，則可搭配 as usual、normally 等詞語。
② 時間與順序：適當運用表示時間或順序的副詞、片語或子句，使敘述更有條理。
③ 連接詞使用：善用連接詞，讓句子之間的銜接更加自然流暢。

【Step 2・範文】An Unexpected Day

[第一段]
(L1) Today was a normal day at first. I woke up at 6:30 a.m. and
(L2) had some breakfast. I almost missed my stop on the bus to
(L3) school because I fell asleep, but luckily, I woke up just in time.
(L4) In the morning, I had math class, which was quite dull. Next,
(L5) history class was a lot more interesting since the teacher told
(L6) great stories.

[第二段]
(L7) After school, I went to cram school as usual for a couple
(L8) of hours. On my way home, I noticed a small cat sitting under
(L9) a car near my house. I didn't recognize the cat, so I was a little
(L10) surprised and curious. It didn't run away when I got closer. In
(L11) fact, it followed me.

[第三段]
(L12) I didn't really know what to do. It looked hungry, so I brought
(L13) it home. I gave it some food and asked my mom what she
(L14) thought. Tomorrow, we'll decide on the best course of action.
(L15) For now, I have to finish my homework and get ready for bed—
(L16) it's another early start tomorrow.

範文旁註：
• 善用連接詞與轉折語，使句子之間的銜接更自然流暢。（because, but luckily, which, since, so, when, In fact）
• 搭配時間副詞或片語，依先後順序敘述事件。（at first, In the morning, Next, After school, On my way home, Tomorrow, For now）
• 適時融入個人感受或反應，讓內容更加生動。（a little surprised and curious, I didn't really know what to do）

【字彙 Vocabulary】（* 為大考出現過的字彙）
*1. normal [ˋnɔrml] adj. 普通的、正常的
    A little rain in spring is completely normal.
*2. asleep [əˋslip] adj. 睡著的
    The baby fell asleep right after dinner.
*3. dull [dʌl] adj. 沉悶的、無聊的
    The movie was so dull that I kept checking the clock.
*4. curious [ˋkjʊrɪəs] adj. 好奇的
    The little girl was curious about everything she saw at the zoo.
◆ 字彙小筆記（僅供參考）
**cram [kræm] n., v.（為應考）臨時死記硬背（文中 cram school 指「補習班」）

【Info Box】
• 第 1 行 woke 為 wake 的過去式；wake up 在此作不及物動詞，表示「醒來」。
• 第 3 行 in time 表示「及時；來得及」。
• 第 7 行 a couple of 表示「幾個；兩三個」。
• 第 8 行 on one's way home 表示「在某人回家的路上」；notice 在此意為「看到、注意到」，可用句型 notice sb/sth + V./V-ing 表示。
• 第 14 行 decide on sth 表示「（仔細考慮後）決定、選定……」，a course of action 指「行動方針、做法」。

【關鍵解析 Language Notes】
1. wh- 名詞子句 vs. wh- + to V. 名詞片語
 ⓐ 由 wh- 疑問詞（what、where、why、when、who、which、how）引導的名詞子句可作主詞、受詞或補語。
   • How the accident happened is still unclear.（作主詞用；動詞須用單數形）
   • We all wondered why the class was canceled.（作受詞用）
   • My question is who is responsible for this.（作主詞補語用）
   ➔ 第 13 行 what she thought 為授與動詞 ask 的間接受詞（my mom 為直接受詞）。
   ※ wh- 引導的名詞子句不需倒裝，寫作 wh- + S. + V.
     ✗ I don't know where is he.   ✓ I don't know where he is.
 ⓑ wh- 子句的主詞與主要子句主詞相同時，可將 wh- 子句簡化為名詞片語 wh- + to V.，意思與「wh- + S. + 助動詞 + V.」相近。
   • I didn't really know what I should do. ➔ I didn't really know what to do.
   名詞片語亦可作為句子的主詞、受詞或補語：
   • How to say no is a skill everyone needs.（作主詞用）
   • I haven't decided when to tell her the news.（作受詞用）
   • The real challenge is how to keep everyone happy.（作主詞補語用）

（註：課本原文 ask 的受詞說明為「what she thought 為間接受詞、my mom 為直接受詞」；一般文法書多把 ask 後的「人」稱為間接受詞、「事」稱為直接受詞，兩種稱呼都會見到，考試重點是 ask + 人 + wh- 子句的語序。）

【Step 3・引導練習】
Practice A 句子排序：請根據時間副詞與轉折詞的提示，將下列六個句子重新排列成合理順序（1–6）。
 ___ A On my way to school, I saw a dog running across the street.
 ___ B Next, I went home and started my homework.
 ___ C I woke up early, and then I got ready for school.
 ___ D I went to basketball practice for two hours after school.
 ___ E Later that evening, I watched TV with my family.
 ___ F In the morning, I had two lessons of English.
 參考答案：C → A → F → D → B → E（A=2, B=5, C=1, D=4, E=6, F=3）

Practice B 動手寫寫看：請以某一天的生活為主題，寫一篇至少 120 字的英文短文。文中需描述你的日常作息，並加入一件出乎意料的事件。文章可分段書寫。請注意事件的先後順序、時態的正確使用，以及連接詞的適當搭配。
