## 課文

**¶1** *Imagine making* music with the vegetables in your kitchen. The Vienna Vegetable Orchestra *has done* exactly that! This musical group from Austria started in 1998 as a joke. A group of friends asked themselves a silly question: Could vegetables become instruments? They decided to try it, and **amazingly**, it worked.

**¶2** Now, the musicians buy fresh vegetables at the market on the day of each **concert**. Since cut vegetables *dry out* quickly, they have to make new instruments for every single show. With simple kitchen tools, they *turn* carrots *into* flutes, pumpkins into drums, and leeks into violins. Their music is surprisingly modern and fun—a **mix** of styles such as electronic and jazz.

**¶3** After more than 340 concerts around the world, the group officially *holds a Guinness World Record* for most concerts by a vegetable orchestra. But perhaps the biggest surprise comes at the end of each show: The musicians use all the leftover vegetables to cook soup for the **audience**! The Vienna Vegetable Orchestra **proves** that music can come from anywhere—even your dinner table.
