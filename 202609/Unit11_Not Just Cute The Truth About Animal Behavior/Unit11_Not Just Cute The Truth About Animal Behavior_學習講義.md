## 課文

**¶1** People love sharing videos of animals online, especially when they *appear to* be doing something sweet or funny. However, **scientists** who study animal behavior *point out* that we often *mistake* the reasons for these actions. An animal might look happy or friendly, even when it is *actually* feeling something different.

**¶2** When red pandas feel **threatened** by a predator, they stand up on their back legs and raise their front paws high in the air. To humans, this looks like the red panda wants a **hug** or is waving hello, and *as a result*, videos and memes that show this behavior have become widely popular online. Most people *find* the pose *cute* *without realizing* what it actually means.

**¶3** *In fact*, red pandas do this only when they *sense* danger nearby. *By standing* tall and spreading their sharp claws, they try to *appear* much bigger and more **threatening** than they really are. This helps them *scare away* possible dangers. *Even though* it looks **charming** on the Internet, for the red panda, it is simply a survival **strategy**.

**¶4** Penguins are another animal *whose* behavior is easily *mistaken for* something else. In winter, thousands of emperor penguins in Antarctica stand close together in a giant circle that's known as a huddle. *While* this may look like a group hug, it is actually a clever way to survive the **freezing** cold. The penguins on the outside slowly move inward, and those in the center move outward, *so that* no single bird stays in the coldest spot for too long.

**¶5** The actions of other types of penguin may also be misunderstood. Gentoo and Adélie penguins collect small stones and place them at a partner's feet, and many people believe this is a **romantic** gift. *In reality*, the stones *serve a* **practical** *purpose*—penguins use them to build **nests** that keep their eggs safe and dry above the **icy** ground.

**¶6** These examples *remind us to* be careful when we think we understand what animals are doing. Something that looks simply cute or entertaining from a human *point of view* may have an important purpose for the animal in the wild.
