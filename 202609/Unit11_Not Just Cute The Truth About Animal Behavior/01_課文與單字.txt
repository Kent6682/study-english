Unit 11｜Not Just Cute: The Truth About Animal Behavior
不是在賣萌：動物行為背後的真相

一、完整課文（中英對照，依段落）
================================================================

【第一部分：紅貓熊篇】

¶1
People love sharing videos of animals online, especially when they appear to be doing something sweet or funny. However, scientists who study animal behavior point out that we often mistake the reasons for these actions. An animal might look happy or friendly, even when it is actually feeling something different.
人們喜歡在網路上分享動物影片，尤其是當牠們看起來正在做些可愛或好笑的事情時。然而，研究動物行為的科學家指出，我們常常誤解這些舉動背後的原因。一隻動物可能看起來很開心或友善，即使牠其實正感受著截然不同的情緒。

¶2
When red pandas feel threatened by a predator, they stand up on their back legs and raise their front paws high in the air. To humans, this looks like the red panda wants a hug or is waving hello, and as a result, videos and memes that show this behavior have become widely popular online. Most people find the pose cute without realizing what it actually means.
當紅貓熊感受到掠食者的威脅時，牠們會用後腳站起來，並把前掌高高舉向空中。在人類看來，這就像紅貓熊想要一個擁抱或在揮手打招呼，因此，呈現這種行為的影片和迷因在網路上廣為流行。大多數人覺得這個姿勢很可愛，卻沒有意識到它真正的意義。

¶3
In fact, red pandas do this only when they sense danger nearby. By standing tall and spreading their sharp claws, they try to appear much bigger and more threatening than they really are. This helps them scare away possible dangers. Even though it looks charming on the Internet, for the red panda, it is simply a survival strategy.
事實上，紅貓熊只有在察覺附近有危險時才會這麼做。藉由挺直站立並張開銳利的爪子，牠們試圖讓自己看起來比實際上更龐大、更具威脅性。這有助於牠們嚇跑潛在的威脅。雖然這在網路上看起來很討喜，但對紅貓熊來說，這純粹是一種求生策略。

【第二部分：企鵝篇】

¶4
Penguins are another animal whose behavior is easily mistaken for something else. In winter, thousands of emperor penguins in Antarctica stand close together in a giant circle that's known as a huddle. While this may look like a group hug, it is actually a clever way to survive the freezing cold. The penguins on the outside slowly move inward, and those in the center move outward, so that no single bird stays in the coldest spot for too long.
企鵝是另一種行為很容易被誤認為別的意思的動物。冬天時，南極洲成千上萬隻皇帝企鵝會緊靠在一起，圍成一個被稱為「抱團」的巨大圓圈。雖然這看起來可能像是集體擁抱，但其實是熬過酷寒的聰明方法。外圍的企鵝會慢慢往內移動，而中間的企鵝則往外移動，好讓沒有任何一隻企鵝在最冷的位置待太久。

¶5
The actions of other types of penguin may also be misunderstood. Gentoo and Adélie penguins collect small stones and place them at a partner's feet, and many people believe this is a romantic gift. In reality, the stones serve a practical purpose—penguins use them to build nests that keep their eggs safe and dry above the icy ground.
其他種類企鵝的舉動也可能被誤解。巴布亞企鵝和阿德利企鵝會收集小石頭，並把它們放在伴侶的腳邊，許多人認為這是一份浪漫的禮物。實際上，這些石頭有實際的用途——企鵝用它們築巢，讓蛋在結冰的地面上方保持安全與乾燥。

¶6
These examples remind us to be careful when we think we understand what animals are doing. Something that looks simply cute or entertaining from a human point of view may have an important purpose for the animal in the wild.
這些例子提醒我們，當我們自認為了解動物在做什麼時，要格外謹慎。從人類的角度看來單純可愛或有趣的事物，對野生動物來說可能有著重要的目的。

三、Language Notes 關鍵解析
================================================================

【紅貓熊篇】
1. appear 作連綴動詞的用法：表示「看起來、顯得；似乎」
   appear + to V.（¶1）／appear + (to be) Adj./N.（¶3）
   • The stars appear to shine more brightly tonight. 今晚的星星似乎變得更加明亮。
   • The man appears to be nervous onstage. 那名男子在台上看起來很緊張。
2. mistake 作動詞的用法：三態 mistake–mistook–mistaken
   mistake the reasons for N.＝誤解……的原因；mistake sb/sth for sb/sth＝把……誤認為……；被動 be mistaken for
   • I mistook the meaning of his email. 我誤解了他電子郵件的意思。
   • The clerk mistook Nicholas for a local student. 那位店員把尼可拉斯誤認為一名本地學生。
3. actually vs. in fact vs. in reality
   (a) actually「其實、實際上」：最口語、最廣泛，用於糾正錯誤、補充說明或轉折。
       • Although this fruit smells terrible, it is actually quite tasty.
   (b) in fact「事實上、其實」：補充說明或加強真實情況，常帶出出乎意料或對比資訊。
       • Doris moved to Japan. In fact, she has been there for almost two years now.
   (c) in reality「實際上、事實上」：著重對比「表面印象與真實情況」，語氣較強。
       • People say I look like I'm in my 20s, but in reality, I'm 32.

Info Box
  sweet 在此指「可愛的、討喜的、溫馨的」。
  point out 表示「指出」，之後可接 sth 或是 that 子句。
  as a result 為副詞片語，表示「因此；結果」；meme [mim] 迷因（網路爆紅圖片、影片；梗圖）。
  sense 在此作及物動詞，指「察覺、感覺到」。
  scare away 表示「嚇跑、驅趕」；possible dangers 指「可能的危險來源、潛在威脅」，包括掠食者、天敵等。
  survival 名詞「求生、生存」，常置於另一名詞前，如 survival strategy。

【企鵝篇】
1. whose 的用法：所有格關係代名詞，表示「……的」，之後須接名詞（如 behavior），先行詞為 another animal。
   • Ricky is looking after a dog whose leg was hurt. 瑞奇正在照顧一隻腿受傷的狗。
2. serve a + (Adj.) + purpose「有……用途」：purpose 可數名詞，前可加形容詞（如 practical）。
   • These boxes may look old, but they still serve a purpose. 這些盒子看起來或許很舊，但它們仍有用處。
3. remind 的用法：「提醒；使想起」
   remind sb to V.（文中用法）／remind sb not to V.／remind sb of sth／remind sb that S + V.
   • Can you remind Tom to bring his notebook tomorrow?
   • The warm soup reminds me of winter evenings.
   • Our teacher reminded us that we have a test on Friday.
補充：misunderstand（mis-「錯、不」＋understand）三態 misunderstand–misunderstood–misunderstood；
      point of view「觀點、見解、角度」，複數 points of view。

Info Box
  Antarctica [͵æntˋɑrktɪkə] 南極洲。
  while 在此指「雖然、儘管」。
  those 在此代指 those penguins。
  so that 引導表目的的副詞子句，表示「以便……；為了讓……」。

Reading Comprehension
1. Why do penguins stand closely together in a huddle?
   (A) To protect themselves from the cold  (B) To look after the youngest penguins
   (C) To share food with other penguins    (D) To keep their nests and eggs safe
   答案：(A)
2. What is the main idea of paragraph 2?（企鵝篇第二段）
   (A) Gentoo and Adélie penguins share romantic gifts.
   (B) We still don't know why penguins collect small stones.
   (C) Many types of penguins build their nests on the ground.
   (D) Penguins have a specific reason for sharing small stones.
   答案：(D)

Integrated Question（108 課綱混合題型練習）
Choose a word from the third paragraph of the red panda part that best fits in the passage below. Write the correct form of the word.
Many people enjoy watching cute videos of red pandas online. However, behavior we think is cute may actually be a way to protect themselves and ________.
答案：survive（取自 survival，依語法改為原形動詞，與 protect 對等）
