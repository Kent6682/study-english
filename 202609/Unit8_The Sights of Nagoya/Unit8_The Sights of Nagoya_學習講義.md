## 課文

**¶1** Nagoya is Japan's fourth-largest city—a **lively** center of industry, culture, and delicious local food. It *plays a major role in* the country's **economy** and *is set to* host the 2026 Asian Games. Yet many travelers still *pass it by* when they plan their trips to Japan. But there are many reasons to consider making a visit to Nagoya.

**¶2** History lovers will find **plenty** to enjoy at the Tokugawa Art Museum. Its impressive **collection** of samurai **armor**, **weapons**, and paintings *brings* Nagoya's Edo period *to life*. Visitors can explore the world of Japan's powerful samurai **clans** and gain a deeper understanding of the time *in which* they lived. Nagoya Castle, the city's most famous **landmark**, was also built during this period.

**¶3** Although much of the castle has been rebuilt over the years, it is still one of the city's most interesting sights. On its rooftop stands a pair of golden *shachihoko*—sea creatures from Japanese **folklore**. *According to* **legend**, they *were believed to* protect the castle from fire. Today, these golden figures **remain** among the castle's most special symbols.

**¶4** Nagoya offers far *more than just* its history. For fans of **Studio** Ghibli, the nearby Ghibli Park is a **magical** experience unlike any other. **Beloved** films such as *My Neighbor Totoro* and *Spirited Away* **inspired** the park's carefully **designed** spaces. There are no **thrilling** rides here. *Instead*, visitors can walk through these areas and feel *as if* they have stepped into the films themselves.

**¶5** *In addition to* visiting its **attractions**, *no* trip to Nagoya *is complete without* trying its famous local dishes. *Kishimen* is a popular noodle dish. The flat noodles can be *served hot* in a fish broth or cold with a **dipping** sauce. Another *must-try* is *hitsumabushi*—grilled eel on rice. It can be eaten on its own, with **various** toppings, or in a light broth.

**¶6** Whether visitors are interested in Japanese history, Ghibli films, or traditional foods, there is something here for every kind of traveler. As the 2026 Asian Games *bring more attention to* the city, now is the perfect time to go and discover why Nagoya is *worth the trip*.

## 必考句型

1. **介系詞 + 關係代名詞**：the time *in which* they lived ＝ the time which they lived in（介系詞在前時不可用 that、不可省略關代）
2. **S. + be believed to V.** ＝ It is believed that + S. + V.
3. **instead（adv.）vs. instead of（prep. + N./V-ing）**
4. **as if + S. + V.**：直說法（可能）vs. 假設語氣（與事實相反：V-ed/were、had p.p.）
5. **no ... be complete without N./V-ing**：沒有……就不完整
6. 字尾 **-er／-or**：traveler、lover、visitor
