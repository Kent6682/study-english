一、完整課文（中英對照，依段落）
================================================================

【第一部分】

¶1（L1–8）
Nagoya is Japan's fourth-largest city—a lively center of industry, culture, and delicious local food. It plays a major role in the country's economy and is set to host the 2026 Asian Games. Yet many travelers still pass it by when they plan their trips to Japan. But there are many reasons to consider making a visit to Nagoya.
名古屋是日本第四大城市——一個充滿活力的工業、文化與在地美食中心。它在日本的經濟中扮演重要角色，並將主辦 2026 年亞運。然而，許多旅客在規劃日本行程時，仍然會略過這座城市。但其實有很多理由值得你考慮造訪名古屋。

¶2（L9–19）
History lovers will find plenty to enjoy at the Tokugawa Art Museum. Its impressive collection of samurai armor, weapons, and paintings brings Nagoya's Edo period to life. Visitors can explore the world of Japan's powerful samurai clans and gain a deeper understanding of the time in which they lived. Nagoya Castle, the city's most famous landmark, was also built during this period.
歷史愛好者在德川美術館會找到許多值得欣賞的事物。館內令人驚豔的武士盔甲、兵器與畫作收藏，讓名古屋的江戶時代栩栩如生。參觀者可以探索日本強大武士家族的世界，並更深入了解他們所生活的那個時代。名古屋城這座城市最著名的地標，也是在這個時期建造的。

¶3（L20–29）
Although much of the castle has been rebuilt over the years, it is still one of the city's most interesting sights. On its rooftop stands a pair of golden shachihoko—sea creatures from Japanese folklore. According to legend, they were believed to protect the castle from fire. Today, these golden figures remain among the castle's most special symbols.
雖然名古屋城的大部分建築多年來已經重建過，它仍然是這座城市最有趣的景點之一。城頂上矗立著一對金色的鯱——日本民間傳說中的海中生物。根據傳說，人們相信它們能保護城堡免於火災。如今，這對金色雕像依然是名古屋城最特別的象徵之一。

【第二部分】

¶4（L1–10）
Nagoya offers far more than just its history. For fans of Studio Ghibli, the nearby Ghibli Park is a magical experience unlike any other. Beloved films such as My Neighbor Totoro and Spirited Away inspired the park's carefully designed spaces. There are no thrilling rides here. Instead, visitors can walk through these areas and feel as if they have stepped into the films themselves.
名古屋能提供的遠不只是它的歷史。對吉卜力工作室的粉絲來說，鄰近的吉卜力公園是一場獨一無二的奇幻體驗。《龍貓》和《神隱少女》等深受喜愛的電影，啟發了園區裡精心設計的空間。這裡沒有刺激的遊樂設施。取而代之的是，遊客可以漫步穿越這些區域，感覺彷彿自己走進了電影之中。

¶5（L11–20）
In addition to visiting its attractions, no trip to Nagoya is complete without trying its famous local dishes. Kishimen is a popular noodle dish. The flat noodles can be served hot in a fish broth or cold with a dipping sauce. Another must-try is hitsumabushi—grilled eel on rice. It can be eaten on its own, with various toppings, or in a light broth.
除了參觀當地景點，沒有品嚐名古屋的知名在地料理，名古屋之旅就不算完整。棊子麵是一道很受歡迎的麵食。這種扁平的麵條可以熱熱地泡在魚高湯裡吃，也可以冷的搭配沾醬吃。另一道必吃美食是鰻魚飯三吃——鋪在白飯上的烤鰻魚。它可以單吃、搭配各種配料吃，或是泡在清淡的高湯裡吃。

¶6（L21–28）
Whether visitors are interested in Japanese history, Ghibli films, or traditional foods, there is something here for every kind of traveler. As the 2026 Asian Games bring more attention to the city, now is the perfect time to go and discover why Nagoya is worth the trip.
無論遊客對日本歷史、吉卜力電影還是傳統美食感興趣，這裡都有適合每一種旅客的東西。隨著 2026 年亞運讓這座城市受到更多關注，現在正是前往名古屋、發掘它為何值得一遊的最佳時機。

四、Language Notes 關鍵解析（摘要）
================================================================
【第一部分】
1. 「介系詞 + 關係代名詞」的用法
   當受格關係代名詞（which 或 whom）在形容詞子句中作介系詞的受詞時，可將介系詞移到關係代名詞前面。
   此時關係代名詞不可省略，且不能用 that 取代 which 或 whom。
   課文：... gain a deeper understanding of the time in which they lived.
       = ... gain a deeper understanding of the time which they lived in.
   • This is the book about which I told you. = This is the book which I told you about.
   • The man for whom Ivy works is a famous lawyer. = The man whom Ivy works for is a famous lawyer.

2. S. + be believed to V.  據信……；一般認為……
   此句型的主詞是被評價的人或事物，以被動語態呈現，重點在「被認為的內容」，而非評價者（通常泛指一般人），
   因此不明確指出看法的來源。此外，此句型可與以 it 作虛主詞的句型改寫，兩者意思相同：
   S. + be believed to V. = It is believed that + S. + V.
   改寫時，原句的主詞移作 that 子句的主詞，而不定詞則對應 that 子句的動詞。
   • In some countries, drinking goat milk is believed to be good for your health.
   = In some countries, it's believed that drinking goat milk is good for your health.

Word Focus｜字尾 -er, -or（常接在動詞後面，表示「從事某事種行為或職業的人」）
   travel + -er = traveler 旅人、旅客
   love + -er = lover 愛好者；情人（love 去 e 加 -er）
   visit + -or = visitor 參觀者；訪客；遊客

【第二部分】
1. instead 與 instead of 的比較
   instead：副詞，指「作為替代、取而代之；反而」，可置於句首（文中用法）、句中或句尾。
   instead of：片語介系詞，指「代替……；而不是……」，之後接名詞或 V-ing，表示被取代的對象或行為。
   • Carter didn't buy coffee. He bought tea instead.
   • Lillian walked instead of taking the bus.

2. as if + S. + V.  好像……、彷彿……
   as if 與 as though 意思相同，引導的子句有兩種情況用法：
   (a) 描述事實或可能發生的情況時，子句用直說法，時式依實際的時間而定，如文中用法。
       • Joshua acts as if he will win the game.
   (b) 描述與事實相反或不太可能發生的情況時，子句須用假設語氣：
       as if/though + S. + V-ed/were（與現在事實相反）
       as if/though + S. + had + p.p.（與過去事實相反）
       • Victoria acts as if she were a queen.
       • He talked to me as if he had known me for years.

3. no ... be complete without N./V-ing  沒有……就不完整；一定要有……才算完整
   此句型用兩個否定詞 no 和 without 來加強肯定語氣，表示「一定要有……才完整」。
   • No picnic is complete without good food.
   • No weekend would be complete without relaxing.
