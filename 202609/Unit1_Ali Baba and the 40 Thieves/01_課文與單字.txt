Unit 1｜Ali Baba and the 40 Thieves《阿里巴巴與四十大盜》
大師名作選｜Synopsis by Ryan Miller
課文朗讀：第一部分 正常 Track 4／慢速 Track 52；第二部分 正常 Track 6／慢速 Track 53

【導讀】
《阿里巴巴與四十大盜》原是一則流傳於敘利亞的民間故事。十八世紀時，法國譯者安托萬・加朗（Antoine Galland）在翻譯《一千零一夜》（The Thousand and One Nights）時將這篇故事收入其中，使其成為聞名世界的經典童話。故事中的咒語 Open, sesame!（芝麻開門）後來更被引申為「獲得成功或達成目標的關鍵方法與祕訣」。

================================================================
一、完整課文（中英對照，依段落）
================================================================

【第一部分】

¶1（L1–10）
Ali Baba was a hardworking but poor woodcutter. One day, while he was gathering wood in the forest, he spotted a group of 40 thieves approaching in the distance. He quickly climbed a tree to hide and watch. The leader stepped up to a large rock and called out, "Open, sesame!" A door appeared in the rock, the men filed inside, and the door closed behind them.
阿里巴巴是一個勤奮卻貧窮的樵夫。有一天，他在森林裡撿柴時，看見遠處有一群四十個盜賊正在走近。他迅速爬上一棵樹，躲起來觀察。首領走到一塊大岩石前，喊道：「芝麻，開門！」岩石上出現一扇門，那群人魚貫而入，門隨即在他們身後關上。

¶2（L11–16）
When the coast was clear, Ali Baba climbed down and tried the words himself. The door opened, and inside the cave, he found gold, jewels, and silk. He took only a modest amount and quietly made his way home.
等到四下無人、安全了，阿里巴巴爬下樹，自己試著念出那句咒語。門打開了，他在洞穴裡發現了黃金、珠寶和絲綢。他只拿了少量財寶，便悄悄地回家了。

¶3（L17–25）
His brother Cassim soon found out and insisted on going to the cave himself. Ali Baba warned him to be careful, but Cassim was too greedy to listen. He entered the cave without trouble—yet in his excitement, he completely forgot the magic words to get back out. When the thieves returned and saw him inside, they killed him as a warning to anyone else who might dare enter their secret spot.
他的哥哥卡辛很快就發現了這件事，堅持要自己去那個洞穴。阿里巴巴警告他要小心，但卡辛太貪心，根本聽不進去。他毫無阻礙地進了洞穴，卻在興奮之下完全忘了出去的咒語。盜賊們回來看到他在裡面，就殺了他，藉此警告任何膽敢闖入他們秘密基地的人。

【第二部分】

¶4（L26–34）
Ali Baba recovered his brother's body, but this helped the thieves realize that someone else knew their secret. Their leader came up with a plan. He disguised himself as an oil seller and asked to stay the night at Ali Baba's home. He also hid his men, who were ready to attack on his signal, inside large oil jars.
阿里巴巴找回了哥哥的屍體，但這反而讓盜賊們發現還有別人知道他們的秘密。他們的首領想出了一個計畫。他把自己偽裝成賣油的商人，請求在阿里巴巴家過夜。他還把手下藏在大油甕裡，這些人隨時準備在他發出暗號時發動攻擊。

¶5（L35–41）
That night, Morgiana, a clever slave girl, went to get some oil from the jars and found the hiding men. Before anyone could make a move, she poured boiling oil into each jar and killed them all. When their leader gave his signal and got no answer, he ran away in fear.
那天晚上，聰明的女奴摩吉娜去甕裡取油，發現了躲藏的人。在任何人有所行動之前，她把滾燙的油倒進每個甕裡，把他們全都殺了。首領發出暗號卻沒有回應，便嚇得逃走了。

¶6（L42–51）
He came back once more and pretended to be a guest at a feast. However, Morgiana knew who he was. As she danced for the guests, she drew a hidden dagger and struck him down before he could do anything. To thank her for her courage and quick thinking, Ali Baba granted Morgiana her freedom. He shared the treasure wisely and spent the rest of his days in peace.
他又回來了一次，假裝是宴會上的賓客。然而，摩吉娜認出了他是誰。她為賓客跳舞時，拔出一把暗藏的短劍，在他還來不及做任何事之前就將他擊倒。為了感謝她的勇氣和機智，阿里巴巴給了摩吉娜自由。他明智地分享寶藏，從此平靜地度過餘生。

三、Language Notes 關鍵解析
================================================================
1. spot + sb/sth + V-ing：看見……正在……
   課文：he spotted a group of 40 thieves approaching in the distance.
   • I spotted my neighbor watering his plants this morning.
2. too + Adj./Adv. + to V.：太……而不能……（to V. 前可加 for sb/sth）
   課文：Cassim was too greedy to listen.
   • The box was too heavy for me to carry by myself.
   • She left too early to catch the last song.
3. 關係子句的限定與非限定用法（who）
   限定：先行詞不明確或泛指（anyone else who might dare...），who 前不加逗號，省略後句意不完整
   非限定：先行詞為專有名詞或已明確指稱（his men, who were ready...），who 前加逗號，省略後句意仍完整
   • The boy who is playing the piano is my brother.
   • Ms. Lee, who teaches us English, is very kind.
4. dare 表示「敢、膽敢」
   (a) 助動詞（過去式 dared）＋原形動詞：多用於否定句、疑問句、固定用法（How dare you!）及情態助動詞之後（課文：who might dare enter）
       • I dared not open the window during the storm.　• Dare you tell your mother the truth?
       ※ 現代口語中疑問句多用一般動詞：Do you dare to tell your mother the truth?
   (b) 一般動詞＋to V.：可用於肯定、否定、疑問句
       • Nobody dared to disagree with the strict coach.　• Did you dare to ask the teacher for permission?
       • Simon didn't dare (to) look down from the top of the building.（現代英語中常省略 to）
5. that 引導名詞子句
   (a) 作受詞，常接在 know、say、think、believe、find、realize（課文）等動詞後
       • Owen said that the machine was broken.
   (b) 作形容詞補語，接在 happy、surprised、worried、sure、angry 等形容詞後
       • I'm surprised that she finished so quickly.
Info Box：in the distance 在遠處｜step up to N. 走上前去｜file inside 魚貫而入｜the coast is clear 情況安全了｜make one's way home 回家｜find out 發現；insist on V-ing 堅持做某事｜in one's excitement 在興奮之下；get back out 出去｜recover 找回｜come up with 想出｜stay the night 過夜｜hid 為 hide 的過去式｜make a move 採取行動｜run away in fear 嚇得落荒而逃｜struck 為 strike 的過去式，strike sb down 擊倒｜thank sb for N./V-ing；quick thinking 機智｜in peace 平靜地

四、課本練習
================================================================
Reading Comprehension
 1. What mistake did Cassim make?
    (A) He got lost deep in the cave. (B) He forgot the magic words.
    (C) He told Morgiana about the cave. (D) He tried to take too much treasure.
 2. How do Morgiana's actions change the end of the story?
    (A) She warns Ali Baba before the thieves get to his home.
    (B) She helps Ali Baba escape from the thieves' cave.
    (C) She stops two attempts by the thieves to hurt Ali Baba.
    (D) She tricks the thieves into giving away who their leader is.
What Do You Think?  What do you think is the main cause of problems in the story?
參考答案（依課文判斷）：1. B　2. C
