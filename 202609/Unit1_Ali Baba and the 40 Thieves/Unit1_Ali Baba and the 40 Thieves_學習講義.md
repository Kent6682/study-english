> 大師名作選｜出自《一千零一夜》｜Synopsis by Ryan Miller

## 課文

**¶1** Ali Baba was a hardworking but poor woodcutter. One day, while he was gathering wood in the forest, he *spotted a group of 40 thieves approaching* in the distance. He quickly climbed a tree to hide and watch. The leader stepped up to a large rock and called out, "Open, sesame!" A door appeared in the rock, the men filed inside, and the door closed behind them.

**¶2** When the coast was clear, Ali Baba climbed down and tried the words himself. The door opened, and inside the **cave**, he found gold, **jewels**, and **silk**. He took only a modest amount and quietly made his way home.

**¶3** His brother Cassim soon found out and insisted on going to the cave himself. Ali Baba **warned** him to be careful, but Cassim was *too **greedy** to listen*. He entered the cave without trouble—yet in his excitement, he completely forgot the magic words to get back out. When the thieves returned and saw him inside, they killed him as a warning to anyone else *who* might *dare* enter their secret spot.

**¶4** Ali Baba recovered his brother's body, but this helped the thieves realize *that someone else knew* their secret. Their leader came up with a plan. He disguised himself as an oil seller and asked to stay the night at Ali Baba's home. He also hid *his men, who* were ready to attack on his **signal**, inside large oil **jars**.

**¶5** That night, Morgiana, a clever **slave** girl, went to get some oil from the jars and found the hiding men. Before anyone could make a move, she **poured** boiling oil into each jar and killed them all. When their leader gave his signal and got no answer, he ran away in fear.

**¶6** He came back once more and **pretended** to be a guest at a feast. However, Morgiana knew who he was. As she danced for the guests, she drew a hidden dagger and struck him down before he could do anything. To thank her for her courage and quick thinking, Ali Baba granted Morgiana her freedom. He shared the treasure wisely and spent the rest of his days in peace.

## 五大必考文法

1. **spot + O + V-ing**：看見……正在……
2. **too + Adj. + to V.**：太……而不能……
3. **關係子句限定 vs. 非限定**：anyone else who（限定）／his men, who（非限定）
4. **dare**：助動詞 dare + 原形 V；一般動詞 dare to V
5. **that 名詞子句**：realize that…（受詞）／surprised that…（形容詞補語）
