一、讀門秘笈：Skimming 略讀（中英對照）
================================================================

There may be times when you want to get a sense of what a text is about without having to read every single word. That's why skimming is an important skill to learn. Skimming is a technique that allows us to gain a general understanding of a text, but how do we do it?
有時候你可能想大致了解一篇文章在講什麼，卻不必逐字閱讀。這就是為什麼略讀是一項值得學習的重要技巧。略讀是一種能讓我們對文章有大致理解的技巧，但我們該怎麼做呢？

1. Look at titles, headings, and subheadings to understand what a text is about
   看標題、大標與小標，了解文章在講什麼
2. Focus on the first and last sentences of paragraphs or sections—these may sum up what is being described
   專注於段落或章節的第一句與最後一句——它們可能總結了正在描述的內容
3. Pay attention to repeated words or phrases that seem important, such as bold or italicized words and numbered lists
   注意看起來重要的重複字詞或片語，例如粗體字、斜體字與編號清單
4. Check charts, graphs, or photo captions to understand the main idea
   查看圖表、圖形或照片說明，了解主旨

【Practice Time】
1. What is the main idea of the following paragraph?
   Libraries today offer much more than just books. Many libraries provide free Internet access, computer classes, study spaces, and community events for all ages. These services help libraries remain important places for learning and connection in modern communities.
   (A) Libraries only provide books and reading spaces.
   (B) Community events are an important library service.
   (C) Modern libraries offer many helpful services.
   (D) People no longer visit libraries as often.
   答案：(C)（第一句 much more than just books 與最後一句 remain important places 都在總結「現代圖書館提供多元服務」）
2. Based on the information provided, what is the graph most likely showing?
   （長條圖：Average ______ per month (in mm)；Aug. 約 230、Sept. 約 400、Oct. 約 330、Nov. 約 130）
   (A) Monthly rainfall (B) Monthly spending (C) Monthly sunshine hours (D) Monthly visitor numbers
   答案：(A)（單位是 mm 公釐，只有降雨量用長度計量）

二、完整課文（中英對照，依段落）
================================================================
Try skimming the following text to get a quick sense of what it's about.
（試著略讀下面這篇文章，快速掌握它在講什麼。）

Fish and Chips: Britain's Favorite Food
炸魚薯條：英國最受歡迎的食物
—Harry Jenkins

¶1（L1–8）
Most British people think fish and chips is the country's national dish. There are around 10,500 fish-and-chip shops in the UK, and the meals are sold in just about every pub and traditional restaurant across the nation. As such, British people eat roughly 382 million portions of fish and chips every year.
大多數英國人認為炸魚薯條是他們國家的國菜。英國約有一萬零五百家炸魚薯條店，而且全國幾乎每一間英式酒館和傳統餐廳都有販售這道餐點。因此，英國人每年大約吃掉三億八千兩百萬份炸魚薯條。

¶2（L9–24）
Records suggest that fish and chips was first served in the 1860s, and the earliest known shops were opened in London and Lancashire. The meal soon became popular with the working class as fish from the North Sea was easy to come by. In fact, fish and chips was so popular that it was even exempted from rationing during the Second World War, as people believe it improved morale.
紀錄顯示，炸魚薯條最早是在 1860 年代開始供應，而已知最早的店家開在倫敦和蘭開夏郡。由於北海的魚很容易取得，這道餐點很快就在勞工階級中流行起來。事實上，炸魚薯條受歡迎到在第二次世界大戰期間甚至免於配給，因為人們相信它提振了士氣。

¶3（L25–32）
Today, going to the fish-and-chip shop is still a big part of British life. Modern fish-and-chip shops, or "chippies," sell a huge variety of foods, from pies and sausages to pickled eggs and spring rolls. There's so much to choose from, so people still love them.
如今，去炸魚薯條店仍是英國生活中很重要的一部分。現代的炸魚薯條店，也就是「chippies」，販售種類繁多的食物，從派、香腸到醃漬蛋和春捲都有。可選擇的東西非常多，所以人們依然很愛這些店。

【Reading Skill Practice】
1. What is the last paragraph about?
   (A) Fish and chips outside of Britain (B) The best fish and chips in Britain
   (C) The price of fish and chips today (D) Fish and chip shops in modern Britain
   答案：(D)（末段首句 Today, going to the fish-and-chip shop ... 與 Modern fish-and-chip shops 重複出現）

【課文旁的略讀提示】
• The title tells us that the article will be about fish and chips.（標題告訴我們文章主題是炸魚薯條。）
• The paragraph's opening line suggests it will cover the history of the dish.（第二段首句暗示這段要講這道菜的歷史。）
• The word popular is repeated. This indicates that British people like fish and chips.（popular 重複出現，顯示英國人喜歡炸魚薯條。）

四、Language Notes 關鍵解析
================================================================
1. come by 的不同意思
   (a) 指「獲得、取得」，常用 sth be easy/hard/difficult to come by 表示「某物很容易／很難獲得」。
       Good jobs are hard to come by in this town.（在這個鎮上，好工作很難找到。）
   (b) 指「順道拜訪、造訪」，之後可接人或地點，為常見口語用法。
       Could you come by my office this afternoon to sign the papers?（你今天下午可以順道來我辦公室簽文件嗎？）
   (c) 指「經過、路過」，與 pass by 意思相同。
       Many buses come by this street every day.（許多公車每天都會經過這條街。）

2. so Adj./Adv. that S. + V. 如此……以致於……；非常……結果……
   so 後面接形容詞或副詞，用來強調程度之高，表達「某種程度過於強烈或明顯，以致於造成某種結果」，該結果即用 that 子句表示。
   The soup was so hot that it burned my tongue.（湯太燙了，結果燙到了我的舌頭。）
   【比較用法】such 也可表示「如此……以致於……」：
   such + a(n) + 單數可數名詞 / 複數可數名詞 / 不可數名詞 + that + S. + V.
   It was such an easy test that everyone passed.（這次考試非常簡單，大家都考及格了。）
   They are such friendly people that everyone likes them.（他們是如此友善的人，以致於大家都喜歡他們。）

【Info Box】
• 第 6 行 as such 在此指「因此、由於這個原因」。
• 第 15 行 become popular with the working class 表示「受到勞工階級的歡迎；在工人階級中流行起來」；其中 popular with sb 指「受到某人喜愛；在某人中流行起來」。
• 第 22 行及物動詞 ration [ˋræʃən] 在此表示「對……實行配給；定量供應……」，文中為動名詞用法（rationing）。
