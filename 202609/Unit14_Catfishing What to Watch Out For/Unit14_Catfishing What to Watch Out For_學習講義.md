## 二、課文（中英對照）

**¶1** Have you ever heard the term *catfishing*? It may sound strange, but it has a simple meaning: Catfishing is when someone pretends to be a different person online, usually to trick others.
> 你聽過 catfishing 這個詞嗎？它聽起來也許很奇怪，但意思很簡單：catfishing 是指有人在網路上假裝成另一個人，通常是為了欺騙別人。

**¶2** The term became popular as a result of the 2010 documentary film *Catfish*. The film follows a man who develops feelings for a woman he has only talked to online. However, when he finally meets her in person, she turns out to be someone completely different. Since the film's release, catfishing has become widely recognized as a serious threat to personal safety and privacy.
> 這個詞是因為 2010 年的紀錄片《鯰魚》而流行起來的。這部片講述一名男子對一位只在網路上聊過天的女子漸漸產生感情。然而，當他終於與她當面見面時，她竟然是一個完全不同的人。自從這部片上映以來，網路假冒身分詐騙就被廣泛認為是對個人安全與隱私的嚴重威脅。

**¶3** When you meet someone online, it's important to pay attention to the things that they ask of you. If they request money, personal details, or private photos, they may be a catfish. People with honest intentions do not need these things simply to get to know you. Also, keep in mind that in the age of AI, spotting a catfish is becoming increasingly difficult, so it's always better to play it safe.
> 當你在網路上認識某人時，留意對方向你要求哪些東西很重要。如果他們索取金錢、個人資料或私密照片，他們可能就是假冒身分的騙子。真心誠意的人僅僅為了認識你，並不需要這些東西。此外，要記住，在人工智慧的時代，要識破網路騙子正變得越來越困難，所以謹慎行事總是比較好。

**¶4** Fake online identities can be very convincing, but there are still ways to identify a catfish. Fake social media accounts, for example, often have very few photos, connections, or posts. The account may only have been created recently, or its profile pictures may have been taken from another person's online profile. When any of these signs show up, it's worth being more careful.
> 假的網路身分可能非常令人信服，但仍有方法可以辨識出網路騙子。舉例來說，假的社群媒體帳號通常只有非常少的照片、好友或貼文。這個帳號可能是最近才建立的，或者它的大頭貼可能是從別人的網路個人檔案盜用來的。當出現任何這些跡象時，就值得更加小心。

**¶5** It can also help to connect with people over a phone or video call. If someone keeps making excuses to avoid this, it may be a sign that they are not who they claim to be. You can also search for them on other social media platforms to see if they have a real and active online presence.
> 透過電話或視訊與對方聯繫也會有幫助。如果某人一直找藉口迴避這件事，這可能是個跡象，表示他們並不是他們所聲稱的那個人。你也可以在其他社群媒體平台上搜尋他們，看看他們在網路上是否有真實且活躍的足跡。

**¶6** These days, meeting people online has become very common, and many close and meaningful relationships have started this way—there is nothing wrong with it. However, it's still important to stay alert. If something feels wrong, trust your instincts and take steps to protect yourself.
> 如今，在網路上認識人已經變得非常普遍，而且許多親密又有意義的關係都是這樣開始的——這並沒有什麼不對。然而，保持警覺仍然很重要。如果覺得有什麼不對勁，就相信你的直覺，並採取行動保護自己。

## 四、Language Notes

1. **turn out (to be) Adj./N.／Adv.／to V**：結果竟然是……（出乎意料）
2. **since**：介系詞＋過去時間點／連接詞＋過去式子句，主句用現在完成式
3. **if**：條件副詞子句「如果」（現在式代未來）vs. 名詞子句「是否」（＝whether）
4. **連綴動詞**：be／become／stay／keep／seem＋Adj./N.；look／feel／smell／sound／taste＋Adj.／like＋N.
