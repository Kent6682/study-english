Catfishing: What to Watch Out For
網路假冒身分詐騙：如何自保
—Harry Jenkins

【第一部分】

¶1（L1–5）
Have you ever heard the term catfishing? It may sound strange, but it has a simple meaning: Catfishing is when someone pretends to be a different person online, usually to trick others.
你聽過 catfishing 這個詞嗎？它聽起來也許很奇怪，但意思很簡單：catfishing 是指有人在網路上假裝成另一個人，通常是為了欺騙別人。

¶2（L6–15）
The term became popular as a result of the 2010 documentary film Catfish. The film follows a man who develops feelings for a woman he has only talked to online. However, when he finally meets her in person, she turns out to be someone completely different. Since the film's release, catfishing has become widely recognized as a serious threat to personal safety and privacy.
這個詞是因為 2010 年的紀錄片《鯰魚》而流行起來的。這部片講述一名男子對一位只在網路上聊過天的女子漸漸產生感情。然而，當他終於與她當面見面時，她竟然是一個完全不同的人。自從這部片上映以來，網路假冒身分詐騙就被廣泛認為是對個人安全與隱私的嚴重威脅。

¶3（L16–26）
When you meet someone online, it's important to pay attention to the things that they ask of you. If they request money, personal details, or private photos, they may be a catfish. People with honest intentions do not need these things simply to get to know you. Also, keep in mind that in the age of AI, spotting a catfish is becoming increasingly difficult, so it's always better to play it safe.
當你在網路上認識某人時，留意對方向你要求哪些東西很重要。如果他們索取金錢、個人資料或私密照片，他們可能就是假冒身分的騙子。真心誠意的人僅僅為了認識你，並不需要這些東西。此外，要記住，在人工智慧的時代，要識破網路騙子正變得越來越困難，所以謹慎行事總是比較好。

【第二部分】

¶4（L1–10）
Fake online identities can be very convincing, but there are still ways to identify a catfish. Fake social media accounts, for example, often have very few photos, connections, or posts. The account may only have been created recently, or its profile pictures may have been taken from another person's online profile. When any of these signs show up, it's worth being more careful.
假的網路身分可能非常令人信服，但仍有方法可以辨識出網路騙子。舉例來說，假的社群媒體帳號通常只有非常少的照片、好友或貼文。這個帳號可能是最近才建立的，或者它的大頭貼可能是從別人的網路個人檔案盜用來的。當出現任何這些跡象時，就值得更加小心。

¶5（L11–18）
It can also help to connect with people over a phone or video call. If someone keeps making excuses to avoid this, it may be a sign that they are not who they claim to be. You can also search for them on other social media platforms to see if they have a real and active online presence.
透過電話或視訊與對方聯繫也會有幫助。如果某人一直找藉口迴避這件事，這可能是個跡象，表示他們並不是他們所聲稱的那個人。你也可以在其他社群媒體平台上搜尋他們，看看他們在網路上是否有真實且活躍的足跡。

¶6（L19–26）
These days, meeting people online has become very common, and many close and meaningful relationships have started this way—there is nothing wrong with it. However, it's still important to stay alert. If something feels wrong, trust your instincts and take steps to protect yourself.
如今，在網路上認識人已經變得非常普遍，而且許多親密又有意義的關係都是這樣開始的——這並沒有什麼不對。然而，保持警覺仍然很重要。如果覺得有什麼不對勁，就相信你的直覺，並採取行動保護自己。

四、Language Notes 關鍵解析
================================================================
【第一部分】
1. turn out 原來是……；結果發現……
   turn out 用來表示事情最後的結果，常帶有「出乎意料」的語氣。
   sb/sth turn out + (to be) Adj./N.｜Adv./介系詞片語｜to V
   • My new neighbor turned out to be a doctor. 結果我的新鄰居竟然是一名醫生。
   • The quiet boy turned out funny. 那個安靜的男孩結果竟然很幽默。
   • Harriet was anxious about the surprise party, but it turned out well. 哈莉特對這場驚喜派對感到焦慮，但結果進行得很順利。
   • The free concert turned out to draw a huge crowd. 結果這場免費演唱會吸引了大批群眾。

2. since 表示「自從……以來」的用法
   (a) 作介系詞時，後面接過去的時間點或事件，子句常用現在完成式（文中用法）或現在完成進行式表示。
       Since + 過去的時間點/事件, S. + have/has + p.p. / been V-ing
       • Joel and I have been seeing each other since high school. 喬爾和我自高中以來就在交往了。
   (b) 作連接詞時，後面接過去式子句，主要子句常用現在完成式或現在完成進行式表示。
       S. + have/has + p.p. / been V-ing since + S. + V-ed
       • They have lived in Japan since they got married three years ago. 他們自三年前結婚後就一直住在日本。

【第二部分】
1. if 子句的不同意思與用法
   (a) 引導條件副詞子句，表示某條件成立時可能產生的情況或結果，意為「如果、假如」（第 12 及 24 行）。
       If + S. + V., S. + will/can/may/shall + V.｜祈使句
       • If Jack calls me, I will tell him the news. 如果傑克打電話給我，我會告訴他這個消息。
       • If the light turns red, stop the car. 如果燈號變紅，就把車停下來。
   (b) 引導名詞子句，作為動詞的受詞或補語，表示「是否」。此時 if 通常可用 whether 代替（第 17 行）。
       S. + V. + if/whether S. + V.
       • Bailey wonders if/whether Max is at home. 貝莉想知道麥克斯是否在家。（作受詞用）
       • I'm not sure if/whether the store is open now. 我不確定那家店現在是否有營業。（作補語用）
       ➜ whether 引導的名詞子句可作主詞，if 子句無此用法。

2. 連綴動詞的用法
   連綴動詞用來連接主詞與主詞補語，主詞補語通常為形容詞或名詞，用來說明主詞的狀態、特徵或身分。常見的連綴動詞包括 be、become（第 20 行）、stay（第 24 行）、keep、seem 等。
   • Morgan became nervous before the speech. 摩根在演講前變得緊張。
   • Tom and Amy stayed friends for many years. 湯姆和艾美維持了多年的友誼。
   延伸學習：連綴動詞與感官有關時，後面常接形容詞或 like + N.。
   look、feel、smell、sound、taste + Adj.｜like + N.
   • I had no idea that salad could taste so good. 我沒想到沙拉竟然可以這麼美味。
   • This place feels like home. 這地方感覺像家一樣。

【Reading Comprehension（第一部分）】
1. Which question is NOT answered in the first part?
   (A) What does catfishing mean?
   (B) How did the term catfishing become popular?
   (C) Why is catfishing seen as a serious problem?
   (D) How many catfishing cases were there last year?
   答案：(D)
2. According to the first part, why is spotting a catfish becoming more difficult?
   (A) Because of the growth of AI
   (B) Because private photos are easy to find
   (C) Because people meet offline more often
   (D) Because of money requests from honest people
   答案：(A)

【Integrated Question（第二部分）】
Which of the following is NOT an example of catfishing from the second part?
• Nora：I met a funny guy in an online game. His account was created last month; he has only one photo, no connections, and zero posts.
• Mark：A girl started messaging me on social media. I later found that her photos were the same as those on another person's account.
• Lizzy：I have been chatting with a boy for a few days. I tried to video chat with him, but he always makes excuses not to.
• Eric：A man sent me a message saying my password had been stolen. He told me to click the link in the message to change my password.
答案：Eric（這是釣魚連結詐騙 phishing，不是假冒身分交友）
