UNIT 12｜主題式會話｜Introducing Yourself 自我介紹
到國外留學時，你知道如何用英文辦理宿舍入住、完成新生報到、參與迎新活動，以及和同學討論課堂用書嗎？

==================================================
【主題句型】
==================================================
宿舍入住：Hi, I'm moving into the dorm today. I'd like to register.
  你好，我今天要搬進宿舍，想辦理報到登記。
  ➔ dorm [dɔrm] 指「宿舍」，為 dormitory [ˋdɔrmə͵torɪ] 的簡稱。
  ➔ register [ˋrɛdʒɪstɚ] 指「登記、註冊」，可作及物或不及物用。

新生報到：Hi, I'm here for the orientation check-in.
  你好，我是來辦理新生報到的。
  ➔ check-in 為片語動詞 check in（報到）的名詞形式。
  ➔ orientation [͵orɪɛnˋteʃən] 原指「定向、適應」，在此指「新生訓練；迎新活動」，內容包括校園導覽、選課說明及校園資源介紹等，
    通常以 welcome session [ˋsɛʃən]（歡迎介紹會）開場，其中 session 表示「（特定活動的）期間、場次」。

迎新活動：Hey there, I'm Daphne. Nice to meet you.
  嗨，我是達芙妮。很高興認識你。
  ➔ Hey there 表示「嗨，你好啊」，語氣輕鬆、友善，多用於非正式場合。
  初次見面時，除了 Nice to meet you 之外，還可使用以下說法：
    · Great/Good to meet you. 很高興認識你。（語氣自然、偏口語）
    · Pleased to meet you. 很高興認識你。（較正式，常見於較禮貌場合）
    · It's a pleasure to meet you. 很榮幸認識你。（語氣正式且最禮貌）

購買課本：Do you know which textbook we need for our coding class?
  你知道我們程式設計課要用哪一本課本嗎？
  ➔ coding 為 code [kod] 的動名詞，code 作動詞時指「為……編碼」，即「程式設計、寫程式」。

【課文】
==================================================
1 Dorm Move-In
Andrew arrives at the student dorm.
A = Andrew　R = Receptionist
L01 A: Hi, I'm moving into the dorm today. I'd like to register.
L02 R: Welcome! Can I have your name, please?
L03 A: I'm Andrew Wang. My enrollment number is F267754.
L04 R: Let me check . . . Yes, you're in room 405 on the fourth floor.
L05 A: Great. Do I get a key card to enter the building?
L06 R: Yes, use this key card for the main entrance and your room.
L07 A: Are there any important dorm rules I should know?
L08 R: Everything is in this welcome book. Quiet hours start at 10 p.m., and guests must sign in at the front desk.

2 Orientation Check-In
Andrew approaches a check-in staff member.
A = Andrew　C = Check-in staff member
L09 A: Hi, I'm here for the orientation check-in.
L10 C: Can I see a form of ID and your acceptance letter?
L11 A: Sure. Here's my ID card and acceptance email.
L12 C: Thank you. I've confirmed your information—you're registered as a first-year student.
L13 A: OK, that's great. What should I do next?
L14 C: Here's your orientation schedule, an information folder, and a campus map.
L15 A: Thanks. Where am I supposed to go now?
L16 C: Head to the student center for the welcome session at 10 a.m.
L17 A: Perfect. Thank you for your help.
L18 C: You're welcome. Enjoy your first day!

3 Orientation Event
Two students meet during the orientation event.
D = Daphne　A = Andrew
L19 D: Hey there, I'm Daphne. Nice to meet you.
L20 A: Nice to meet you too. I'm Andrew. I'm from Kaohsiung, Taiwan. What about you?
L21 D: I'm Canadian, from Ottawa. What made you choose this school?
L22 A: I heard the computer science program is excellent.
L23 D: Same here. I also really like the international environment.
L24 A: Are you enjoying orientation so far?
L25 D: I am! It's a great way to get familiar with the campus.
L26 A: Do you want to check out the next part of the campus tour together?
L27 D: Absolutely! Let's go.

4 Buying Textbooks
Daphne and Andrew are discussing textbooks.
A = Andrew　D = Daphne
L28 A: Do you know which textbook we need for our coding class?
L29 D: It's called Introduction to Computer Programming, 5th Edition.
L30 A: Got it. (Searching for it online) It looks quite expensive though.
L31 D: I heard you can rent books instead of buying new copies.
L32 A: That sounds like a much better idea. Where can I do that?
L33 D: The library has a rental program, and some students sell used copies online. Used books are usually cheaper—sometimes half the original price.
L34 A: In that case, I'll check online first.
L35 D: Good idea. Let me know if you need help finding one.

【Vocabulary 字彙】（* 為大考出現過的字彙）
==================================================
對話 1–2
*1. entrance [ˋɛntrəns] n. 入口；大門
    Please wait for me at the hotel entrance.
*2. confirm [kənˋfɝm] v. 確認；證實；使更加確信
    Mom confirmed the bus time before we left.
*3. campus [ˋkæmpəs] n. 校園
    Henry and Bella walked across campus after lunch.
*4. supposed [səˋpozd] adj. 應該的；被預期的（be supposed to V. 指「應該……」）
    Grandpa is supposed to take medicine twice a day.
字彙小筆記（僅供參考）
  receptionist [rɪˋsɛpʃənɪst] n. 櫃檯接待人員；服務檯人員
  enrollment [ɪnˋrolmənt] n. 註冊；入學登記
  acceptance [əkˋsɛptəns] n. 接納；接受（acceptance letter 可指「錄取通知書；錄取信」）
  folder [ˋfoldɚ] n. 文件夾、資料夾

對話 3–4
*1. environment [ɪnˋvaɪrənmənt] n. 環境
    Michael enjoys working in a team environment.
*2. familiar [fəˋmɪljɚ] adj. 熟悉的（get familiar with sth 指「熟悉……、通曉……」）
    Logan needs time to get familiar with this city.
*3. rent [rɛnt] v. 租借、租用；出租
    補 rental [ˋrɛntl̩] adj. 租借的；租賃的（第 22 行）
    Fiona wants to rent a dress for the party.
*4. original [əˋrɪdʒənl̩] adj. 原本的、最初的；原始的
    I don't think this is the original image.
字彙小筆記（僅供參考）
  absolutely [ˋæbsə͵lutlɪ] adv. （表肯定回答）當然；絕對地

【Info Box】
==================================================
對話 1–2
• 第一段標題 move-in 為片語動詞 move in 的名詞形式，指「搬入、入住」。
• 第 7 行 key card 指「門禁卡」。
• 第 10 行 quiet hour 指「寧靜時段」，通常指晚上十點後需保持安靜的時間；第 11 行 sign in 作不及物用，指「登記、簽到」。
• 第 15 行 ID 是 identification [aɪ͵dɛntəfəˋkeʃən] 的簡稱，指「身分證明」。
• 第 23 行 head 在此作不及物動詞，指「朝……前進；前往……」。
對話 3–4
• 第 6 行 Canadian [kəˋnedɪən] 可作名詞或形容詞，指「加拿大人；加拿大的」；Ottawa [ˋɑtəwə] 指「渥太華」，為加拿大首都。
• 第 10 行 Are you enjoying N./V-ing? 常用來詢問對方對正在進行的活動或體驗的感受，表示「你喜歡……嗎？你覺得……怎麼樣？」。
• 第 12 行片語動詞 check out 在此作及物用，指「看看、參觀、瞧瞧」，常用於查看或檢視有趣或吸引人的事物。
• 第 25 行 in that case 表示「既然如此；那樣的話」。
