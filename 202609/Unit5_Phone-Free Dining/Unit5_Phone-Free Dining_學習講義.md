# Unit 5｜Phone-Free Dining: The War Over Phones at the Table 學習講義

> 正反意見｜用餐該禁手機嗎？餐桌上的手機論戰｜—Kathy Chapman

## 四格大意

| 階段 | 內容 | 關鍵英文 |
|---|---|---|
| 起 | 埼玉六席拉麵店禁止用餐時滑手機，店主不忍麵條放涼，引發全球討論 | made international news by banning phones |
| 承 | 支持方：麵店老闆 Ken 要麵一上桌就吃；老師 Marcus 喜歡家庭聚餐收起手機 | at their best the moment they hit the bowl |

## 課文

**¶1** In January 2026, a six-seat ramen shop in Saitama, Japan, made **international** news by *banning* phones during meals. **Owner** Kawada Yuichi *watched too many customers let their noodles go cold* while they **stared** at a screen. The policy quickly started a **global** conversation. Here, five people share their opinions.

**¶2 Ken, 47, noodle shop owner:** "My noodles are *at their best* *the moment* they hit the bowl. I *put* a lot of time and *effort into* making the **broth**. I believe asking customers for their full attention is fair—it's part of the experience they're paying for. It's not too much to ask someone to enjoy their meal before they **scroll**."

**¶3 Jess, 20, college student:** "I eat alone between classes *all the time*, and my phone is part of that. I would always *be sure to* check that I'm not bothering anyone. If I'm watching a video, I will use my **headphones**, and I try not to stay long *once* I've finished my meal. I think it's possible to eat and use my phone. It doesn't have to be *one or the other*."

**¶4 Sarah, 32, office worker:** "*Half the reason* I try new restaurants is to post about them. Ban my phone and I'll just find somewhere else to go. Restaurants may be *missing out on* **promotion** from customers who post pictures and **reviews** online. This is **especially** important for new or smaller businesses that are trying to build a **reputation**."

**¶5 Marcus, 41, teacher:** "I like the idea of phone-free restaurants for family meals. When phones are *put away*, people talk more and *pay attention to* each other. I think some restaurants are trying to create that kind of **atmosphere**. If someone is eating on their own, however, that's a different case."

**¶6 Diana, 22, waiter:** "Some restaurants are small, busy, and have **limited** seating. Because of this, sometimes people have to *wait in line* for quite a long time. If someone is **constantly** checking their phone, they may *end up taking* a long time to finish their meal. I understand the ban in **situations** like these."

## 必考句型

1. **感官動詞 + O + 原形 V／V-ing**：watched too many customers let their noodles go cold（let + O + 原形 V；go cold 變涼）
2. **the moment (that) = as soon as = once**：the moment they hit the bowl／once I've finished my meal
3. **put effort into + N./V-ing**：put a lot of time and effort into making the broth
4. **miss out on + N./V-ing**：missing out on promotion
5. **put away**（收起來；存錢）：When phones are put away（被動）
6. **end up + V-ing**：end up taking a long time to finish their meal
7. **祈使句 + and + 結果**：Ban my phone and I'll just find somewhere else to go.

## 立場整理（Integrated Questions）

| 人物 | 立場 | 理由 |
|---|---|---|
| Ken（麵店老闆） | 強烈支持 | 麵一上碗最好吃；投入心力熬湯；全神貫注是體驗的一部分 |
| Jess（大學生） | 反對 | 自律使用手機，邊吃邊用不必二選一 |
| Sarah（上班族） | 反對 | 發文是嘗新餐廳的理由；禁令讓小店錯失宣傳 |
| Marcus（老師） | 支持（家庭聚餐） | 收起手機讓家人多交流；獨自用餐另當別論 |
| Diana（服務生） | 有條件理解 | 小店座位有限、客人要排隊，一直看手機拖慢用餐 |

最強烈支持者：**Ken and Marcus**；「建立好形象」的片語：**build a reputation**。
