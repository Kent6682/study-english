## Scene 1｜新同學報到 A New Classmate Arrives

📍 開學第一週，加拿大來的交換學生艾瑪第一天到校，莉莉和傑森負責帶她認識環境。

本場單字：entrance, confirm, campus, supposed, international, environment, familiar, rent, original

**Lily**：Hi, you must be Emma! We were told to meet you at the main **entrance**.  
　　嗨，你一定是艾瑪吧！老師要我們在大門口跟你碰面。

**Emma**：Yes, that's me. Thanks for waiting. Can I just **confirm** that this is Class 205?  
　　對，是我，謝謝你們等我。我可以先確認一下這裡是 205 班嗎？

**Jason**：That's right. Welcome! I'm Jason, and this is Lily.  
　　沒錯，歡迎！我是傑森，這位是莉莉。

**Emma**：Nice to meet you both. Your **campus** is so big!  
　　很高興認識你們。你們的校園好大！

**Lily**：No problem. We're **supposed** to show you around today.  
　　沒問題，我們今天應該要帶你四處逛逛。

**Jason**：Are you the first **international** student at our school?  
　　你是我們學校第一位國際學生嗎？

**Emma**：I think so. Everything is new to me—the language, the food, the whole **environment**.  
　　我想是吧。一切對我都很新鮮：語言、食物，整個環境都是。

**Lily**：Don't worry. You'll get **familiar** with everything in a few weeks.  
　　別擔心，幾個禮拜後你就會熟悉一切了。

**Jason**：By the way, where are you living?  
　　對了，你住在哪裡？

**Emma**：My family decided to **rent** an apartment near the MRT station.  
　　我家人決定在捷運站附近租一間公寓。

**Lily**：Nice! That's only ten minutes from here by bike.  
　　讚！騎腳踏車到這裡只要十分鐘。

**Emma**：Really? My **original** plan was to take the bus, but maybe I'll buy a bike.  
　　真的嗎？我原本打算搭公車，不過也許我會買台腳踏車。

**Jason**：Good idea. Lots of students ride to school here.  
　　好主意，這裡很多學生騎車上學。

**Lily**：Come on, let's go to class. The bell is about to ring!  
　　走吧，去上課，鐘聲快響了！

### 實用口語句型

- **You must be ...**　你一定是……吧（初次見面確認身分）
- **show sb around**　帶某人四處參觀
- **get familiar with ...**　熟悉……
- **The bell is about to ring.**　鐘聲快響了。be about to V. 即將……

## Scene 2｜戲劇社排練 Drama Club Rehearsal

📍 戲劇社在排練《阿里巴巴與四十大盜》，傑森演阿里巴巴，莉莉演聰明的女僕瑪潔娜。

　　好，各位，我們再排一次山洞那場戲。

**Jason**：Wait, where are the **jewels**? Ali Baba is supposed to find a pile of treasure.  
　　等等，珠寶在哪裡？阿里巴巴應該要發現一堆寶藏啊。

**Director**：They're in that box, next to the **silk** cloth. Just **pretend** they're real.  
　　在那個箱子裡，絲綢布旁邊。你就假裝它們是真的。

**Jason**：Got it. And then my brother comes in, right?  
　　了解。然後我哥哥會進來，對吧？

**Director**：Yes. He's so **greedy** that he wants all the treasure for himself.  
　　對。他貪心到想把寶藏全部占為己有。

**Lily**：What about my part? When do I come on stage?  
　　那我的部分呢？我什麼時候上台？

**Director**：You're Morgiana, the clever **slave** girl. You enter after the thieves hide in the oil **jars**.  
　　你演瑪潔娜，那個聰明的女奴。你在盜賊躲進油罐之後上場。

**Lily**：And then I **pour** hot oil into them, right? That part is a little scary.  
　　然後我把熱油倒進去，對吧？那段有點可怕。

**Director**：Don't worry, it's only water on stage. But first, the leader gives a **signal** to his men.  
　　別擔心，台上只是水而已。不過在那之前，首領會先給手下一個暗號。

**Jason**：What does he do exactly?  
　　他具體要做什麼？

**Director**：He knocks on the wall three times. Lily, that's when you turn and **warn** Ali Baba with your eyes.  
　　他會敲牆三下。莉莉，那時你要轉身用眼神警告阿里巴巴。

**Lily**：Like this? Ali Baba, watch out!  
　　像這樣嗎？阿里巴巴，小心！

**Director**：Perfect! You two are going to be great.  
　　完美！你們倆一定會演得很棒。

**Jason**：I hope so. The show is only two weeks away!  
　　希望如此。距離演出只剩兩週了！

### 實用口語句型

- **Just pretend they're real.**　就假裝它們是真的
- **come on stage**　上台
- **Watch out!**　小心！

## Scene 3｜英式餐館的無手機晚餐 A Phone-Free Dinner

📍 莉莉和傑森到一家英式餐館吃炸魚薯條，這家店有一條特別的規定。

本場單字：pub, review, dine, owner, constantly, stare, especially, variety, portion, roughly, limited, suggest, situation

**Jason**：This British **pub** has great **reviews** online. Everyone talks about the fish and chips.  
　　這家英式酒館在網路上評價很好，大家都在討論它的炸魚薯條。

**Lily**：Look at the sign on the door. It says, 'Phone-free **dining**.'  
　　你看門上的告示，寫著「無手機用餐」。

**Waiter**：Welcome! Our **owner** started this rule last year. Please put your phones in this box.  
　　歡迎光臨！我們老闆去年開始實施這個規定，請把手機放進這個盒子裡。

**Jason**：Seriously? But I **constantly** check my messages.  
　　真的假的？可是我一直都在看訊息耶。

**Waiter**：That's exactly why. People used to **stare** at their screens all through dinner.  
　　這正是原因。以前客人整頓晚餐都盯著螢幕看。

**Lily**：I like the idea, **especially** for a meal with friends.  
　　我喜歡這個點子，尤其是跟朋友吃飯的時候。

**Waiter**：Here's the menu. We have a wide **variety** of dishes, but the fish is our best seller.  
　　這是菜單。我們有各式各樣的料理，不過魚是我們最暢銷的。

**Jason**：How big is one **portion**? I'm really hungry.  
　　一份有多大？我真的很餓。

**Waiter**：It's **roughly** the size of a plate, with lots of chips on the side.  
　　大約一個盤子那麼大，旁邊還附很多薯條。

**Lily**：Wow. But the dessert menu looks **limited**—there are only two choices.  
　　哇。不過甜點菜單看起來選擇不多，只有兩種。

**Jason**：Well, the long line outside **suggests** this place is really good.  
　　嗯，外面排的長長人龍顯示這家店真的很好吃。

**Lily**：True. And without phones, we'll actually have to talk to each other tonight.  
　　確實。而且沒有手機，我們今晚就真的得好好聊天了。

**Jason**：OK, I'll accept the **situation**. No phones, just food and friends.  
　　好吧，我接受這個狀況。沒有手機，只有美食和朋友。

**Lily**：Now you're talking! Let's order.  
　　這才對嘛！我們點餐吧。

### 實用口語句型

- **Seriously?**　真的假的？
- **best seller**　最暢銷的商品
- **on the side**　（餐點）附在旁邊的
- **Now you're talking!**　這才對嘛！對方終於說出你想聽的話

## Scene 4｜看動物紀錄片 Watching a Nature Documentary

📍 週末晚上，莉莉和傑森在莉莉家看動物紀錄片，媽媽也一起看。

本場單字：charming, hug, scientist, threaten, strategy, freezing, nest, romantic, practical, icy

**Lily**：Look at that red panda! It's so **charming**.  
　　你看那隻紅貓熊！好可愛。

**Jason**：It's standing on two legs and opening its arms. Does it want a **hug**?  
　　牠用兩隻腳站起來，還張開雙臂，是想要抱抱嗎？

**Mom**：Actually, no. The **scientist** on the show just explained it.  
　　其實不是。節目裡的科學家剛剛解釋過了。

**Lily**：Wait, what did she say? I missed it.  
　　等等，她說了什麼？我沒聽到。

**Mom**：It stands up when it feels **threatened**. It's trying to look bigger.  
　　牠覺得受到威脅時就會站起來，是想讓自己看起來比較大。

**Jason**：Oh! So it's a **strategy** to scare away enemies.  
　　喔！所以那是嚇跑敵人的策略。

**Lily**：Not so cute after all. What's next?  
　　原來沒那麼可愛。接下來是什麼？

**Jason**：Penguins in Antarctica. It looks **freezing** cold there.  
　　南極的企鵝。那裡看起來超冷。

**Mom**：Watch this. The male gives the female a small stone for her **nest**.  
　　看這段，公企鵝會送母企鵝一顆小石頭築巢。

**Lily**：Aww, that's so **romantic**! It's like a gift of love.  
　　哇，好浪漫！就像愛的禮物。

**Mom**：Maybe, but it's also **practical**. The stones keep the eggs off the **icy** ground.  
　　也許吧，不過這也很實際，石頭能讓蛋不直接碰到冰冷的地面。

**Jason**：Smart birds. I couldn't live in a place like that.  
　　聰明的鳥。我可沒辦法住在那種地方。

**Lily**：Me neither. I'll stay here on the sofa with my snacks.  
　　我也是。我要待在沙發上配零食。

**Mom**：Then pass me the chips, please.  
　　那請把洋芋片遞給我。

### 實用口語句型

- **I missed it.**　我錯過了（沒聽到／沒看到）
- **after all**　畢竟；到頭來
- **Me neither.**　我也不。附和否定句時用
- **Pass me ..., please.**　請把……遞給我

　　成交。走越多步，就能賺越多點數。

　　附近有公園嗎？我不想走太遠。

**Lily**：There's a hiking **trail** behind the library. It's only two kilometers long.  
　　圖書館後面有一條步道，才兩公里長。

**Jason**：OK. Wow, look at the map. We're planting **colorful** flowers everywhere we go!  
　　好。哇，你看地圖，我們走到哪就種下五顏六色的花！

**Lily**：I like this game because it's so **casual**. You don't need to be good at games.

　　對啊，而且我不知不覺已經走了五千步。

**Lily**：See? Walking is more fun with a goal.  
　　看吧？有目標的話，走路更有趣。

**Jason**：Next weekend, let's take a longer **journey**—maybe to the river park.  
　　下個週末我們走遠一點吧，也許去河濱公園。

**Lily**：Sounds great. I'll bring water and sandwiches.  
　　聽起來很棒，我帶水和三明治。

## Scene 8｜這個帳號是真的嗎？ Is That Profile Real?

📍 傑森最近在網路上認識一個新朋友，莉莉覺得不太對勁。

本場單字：recently, platform, claim, term, fake, develop, request, threat, private, detail, identity, avoid

**Jason**：I **recently** met someone online. She's really nice, and we chat every day.  
　　我最近在網路上認識一個人，她人很好，我們每天聊天。

**Lily**：Which **platform** did you meet her on?  
　　你在哪個平台認識她的？

**Jason**：A gaming app. She **claims** to be a model from Korea.  
　　一個遊戲 app。她說自己是韓國的模特兒。

**Lily**：Hmm. Have you ever seen her face on a video call?  
　　嗯……你有在視訊通話裡看過她的臉嗎？

**Jason**：No. She always says her camera is broken. Why?  
　　沒有，她總說她的鏡頭壞了。怎麼了？

**Lily**：Have you heard the **term** 'catfishing'? It's when someone uses a **fake** profile to fool people.  
　　你聽過 catfishing 這個說法嗎？就是有人用假帳號來騙人。

**Jason**：You think she's not real? But we've **developed** a real friendship!  
　　你覺得她不是真的？可是我們已經培養出真正的友誼了！

**Lily**：That's exactly how it works. Has she ever asked for money?  
　　這正是他們的手法。她有跟你要過錢嗎？

**Jason**：Well... last night she **requested** 1,000 dollars for a plane ticket to visit me.  
　　呃……昨晚她跟我要一千塊，說要買機票來看我。

**Lily**：Jason, that's a big warning sign. This person could be a real **threat**.  
　　傑森，這是很大的警訊，這個人可能真的對你有威脅。

**Jason**：Oh no. I also told her some **private** things about my family.  
　　糟了，我還跟她說了一些家裡的私事。

**Lily**：Don't share any more **details**, like your address or school.  
　　別再透露任何資料了，像是你的住址或學校。

**Jason**：Should I just block her?  
　　我是不是直接封鎖她就好？

**Lily**：Yes, and report the account. Her real **identity** could be anyone.  
　　對，而且要檢舉這個帳號。她的真實身分可能是任何人。

**Jason**：I feel so stupid.  
　　我覺得自己好蠢。

**Lily**：Don't. Just remember to **avoid** sending money to people you've never met.  
　　別這樣想。只要記得，避免把錢匯給沒見過面的人。

### 實用口語句型

- **a warning sign**　警訊
- **That's exactly how it works.**　這正是它的運作方式（手法）
- **block / report an account**　封鎖／檢舉帳號
- **I feel so stupid.**　我覺得自己好蠢

## Scene 9｜意料之外的一天 An Unexpected Day

📍 莉莉跟傑森分享她上週末那個「本來很普通」的一天。

本場單字：normal, asleep, dull, chess, curious, champion, proof, honor

**Jason**：How was your weekend?  
　　你週末過得怎麼樣？

**Lily**：It started as a totally **normal** Saturday. I almost fell **asleep** on the bus.  
　　一開始是個再普通不過的星期六，我在公車上差點睡著。

**Jason**：Sounds **dull**.  
　　聽起來好無聊。

**Lily**：It was, until I got off at the park. An old man was playing **chess** alone.  
　　本來是啦，直到我在公園下車。有個老先生一個人在下西洋棋。

**Jason**：Alone? That's strange.  
　　一個人？好奇怪。

**Lily**：I was **curious**, so I asked if I could join him.  
　　我很好奇，所以問他我可不可以跟他下一盤。

**Jason**：Did you win?  
　　你贏了嗎？

**Lily**：No way! He beat me in five minutes. Then he told me he was a national **champion** in the 1980s.  
　　怎麼可能！他五分鐘就打敗我了。然後他說他是一九八〇年代的全國冠軍。

**Jason**：Really? Did you believe him?  
　　真的嗎？你相信他嗎？

**Lily**：Not at first. But he showed me an old newspaper photo as **proof**.  
　　一開始不信。但他拿了一張舊報紙照片給我看當作證明。

**Jason**：Wow, that's so cool!  
　　哇，好酷！

**Lily**：He said it was an **honor** to teach a young player. Now he's going to teach me every Sunday.  
　　他說能教年輕棋手是他的榮幸，以後他每個星期天都要教我。

**Jason**：So a boring day turned into the start of something new.  
　　所以無聊的一天變成了新事物的開始。

**Lily**：Exactly. You never know what a day will bring.  
　　沒錯，你永遠不知道一天會帶來什麼。

### 實用口語句型

- **No way!**　怎麼可能！
- **at first**　起初
- **turn into ...**　變成……
- **You never know ...**　世事難料；你永遠不知道……
