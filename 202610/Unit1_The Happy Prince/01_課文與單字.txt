Unit 1｜The Happy Prince《快樂王子》
大師名作選｜原著：Oscar Wilde（奧斯卡・王爾德，1854–1900）｜Synopsis by Kathy Chapman
課文朗讀：第一部分 正常 Track 4／慢速 Track 56；第二部分 正常 Track 6／慢速 Track 57

【導讀】
《快樂王子》是愛爾蘭作家王爾德的經典童話。故事運用擬人化手法，描述一位生前從未嘗過悲傷滋味的王子，死後被鑄成雕像矗立於城市中央。從高處俯瞰，他第一次目睹貧苦人民的困境，內心深受震動，於是請求善良的小燕子協助，將身上的黃金與寶石送給最需要幫助的人。

================================================================
一、完整課文（中英對照，依段落）
================================================================

【第一部分】

¶1（L1–4）
In a city in northern Europe, a statue of the Happy Prince stood high over the streets. His entire body was covered in thin leaves of shining gold. Two sapphires shone brightly as his eyes, and there was a large red ruby on the handle of his sword.
在北歐的一座城市裡，快樂王子的雕像高高矗立在街道上方。他全身覆蓋著閃亮的薄金箔。兩顆藍寶石化作他的雙眼閃閃發亮，他的劍柄上還鑲著一顆巨大的紅寶石。

¶2（L5–11）
One evening, a small swallow flew into the city. He was tired from his long journey south and decided to rest on the statue. As he settled between the prince's feet, one drop of water suddenly fell onto him, and then another. The swallow looked up in surprise and saw that the statue was crying. "Who are you, and why are you crying?" asked the swallow. "I am the Happy Prince," the statue replied.
一天傍晚，一隻小燕子飛進了這座城市。往南方的長途旅程讓他疲憊不堪，於是決定在雕像上歇一歇。他在王子的雙腳之間安頓下來時，一滴水突然落在他身上，接著又是一滴。燕子驚訝地抬頭一看，發現雕像正在哭泣。「你是誰？為什麼在哭？」燕子問。「我是快樂王子。」雕像回答。

¶3（L11–19）
The prince explained that when he was alive, he lived in a beautiful palace and never knew sadness. He enjoyed music, dancing, and joy every day. Now, from his high place above the city, he could see hunger, sickness, and suffering everywhere. Though his heart was made of lead, the troubles of the poor filled him with pain.
王子解釋說，他在世時住在一座美麗的宮殿裡，從來不知道悲傷是什麼。他每天享受音樂、舞蹈和歡樂。如今，從城市上方的高處，他看見處處都是飢餓、疾病與苦難。雖然他的心是鉛做的，窮人的苦難仍讓他充滿痛苦。

【第二部分】

¶4（L20–25）
The prince wanted the swallow to help a poor seamstress whose son was ill. He asked the bird to take the ruby from his sword and deliver it to her. The swallow agreed and brought the ruby to the woman.
王子希望燕子去幫助一位兒子生了病的貧窮女裁縫。他請小鳥把劍上的紅寶石取下來，送去給她。燕子答應了，把紅寶石帶給了那位婦人。

¶5（L26–35）
Next, the prince asked him to give one sapphire eye to a poor writer and the other to a girl who sold matches. Now blind, the prince told the swallow to strip away his gold and give it to the poor. By winter, all the gold was gone, and the cold became too much for the swallow, who died at the prince's feet. At that moment, the prince's lead heart cracked in two from sadness.
接著，王子請燕子把一隻藍寶石眼睛送給一位貧窮的作家，另一隻送給一個賣火柴的女孩。此時雙眼已盲的王子，要燕子把他身上的金箔剝下來，分給窮人。到了冬天，金箔全都沒了，燕子再也抵擋不住嚴寒，死在王子的腳邊。就在那一刻，王子的鉛心因悲傷而裂成了兩半。

¶6（L36–45）
Without its gold and jewels, the statue was removed to make room for a new one. The prince's broken lead heart was thrown away beside the dead swallow. Far above the city, God asked an angel to bring him the two most precious things there. The angel returned with the swallow and the prince's heart, and God said that they would live forever in heaven.
失去了金箔與寶石，雕像被移走，好騰出位置給一座新雕像。王子破裂的鉛心被丟棄在死去的燕子旁邊。在城市的遙遠上空，上帝要一位天使把城裡最珍貴的兩樣東西帶給他。天使帶著燕子和王子的心回來，上帝說他們將在天堂永遠活下去。

三、Language Notes 關鍵解析（課文藍字標示處）
================================================================
1. one... another／one... the other
   • 數量不限定時：one（一個）... another（另一個，不特定的再一個）
     課文：one drop of water suddenly fell onto him, and then another.
   • 只有兩個時：one（其中一個）... the other（剩下的那一個）
     課文：give one sapphire eye to a poor writer and the other to a girl who sold matches.（王子只有兩隻眼睛）
   • There are two apples on the table. One is red, and the other is green.

2. the + Adj. 的不同意義
   (a) 指具某種特徵的一群人，視為複數，接複數動詞：the poor 窮人、the rich 富人、the sick 病人、the young 年輕人
       • The sick were cared for by the nurses.
   (b) 指抽象的事物、概念，視為單數：the impossible 不可能的事
       • Some people work hard to try to achieve the impossible.

3. 授與動詞的用法（bring、give 等）
   授與動詞 + sb + sth ＝ 授與動詞 + sth + to/for + sb
   ※ 直接受詞 sth 為代名詞時，只能用「授與動詞 + sth + 介系詞 + sb」（deliver it to her）。
   • to：強調傳遞方向（bring, give, hand, lend, offer, pass, send, show, tell, write）
   • for：強調受益者（buy, choose, cook, find, get, make, order, prepare, save）
   • She sent her friend a birthday card.／Mom bought a new backpack for my sister.

4. Adj., S + V. 的用法（分詞構句省略 being）
   The prince now was blind, and he told the swallow to strip away his gold.
   Step 1 兩子句由 and 連接且主詞相同 → 省略連接詞與其中一個主詞
   Step 2 動詞改分詞：was → being → Now being blind, the prince told...
   Step 3 being 可省略 → Now blind, the prince told the swallow to strip away his gold.
   • Lost in the mall, the little girl began to cry.（那個小女孩在商場裡迷路了，開始哭泣。）

What Do You Think?  Why do you think the swallow agreed to help the Happy Prince?
  句型提示：I think the main reason is that...／From my point of view,...

Reading Comprehension
 1. Why did the swallow stop in the city?
    (A) He was looking for food. (B) He wanted to visit the palace.
    (C) He was tired from his journey south. (D) He was searching for the Happy Prince.
 2. What did the prince tell the swallow to do with his gold?
    (A) Return it to the palace (B) Keep it for the winter (C) Sell it in the market (D) Give it to the poor
 參考答案：1. C　2. D
