## 課文（逐段）

**¶1** In a city in northern Europe, a **statue** of the Happy Prince stood high over the streets. His entire body was covered in thin leaves of shining gold. Two sapphires shone brightly as his eyes, and there was a large red ruby on the handle of his **sword**.

**¶2** One evening, a small swallow flew into the city. He was tired from his long journey south and decided to rest on the statue. As he settled between the prince's feet, *one* drop of water suddenly fell onto him, and then *another*. The swallow looked up in surprise and saw that the statue was crying. "Who are you, and why are you crying?" asked the swallow. "I am the Happy Prince," the statue replied.

**¶3** The prince explained that when he was alive, he lived in a beautiful **palace** and never knew sadness. He enjoyed music, dancing, and joy every day. Now, from his high place above the city, he could see **hunger**, sickness, and **suffering** everywhere. Though his heart was made of lead, the troubles of *the poor* filled him with pain.

**¶4** The prince wanted the swallow to help a poor seamstress whose son was ill. He asked the bird to take the ruby from his sword and deliver it to her. The swallow agreed and *brought* the ruby to the woman.

**¶5** Next, the prince asked him to *give one* sapphire eye to a poor writer and *the other* to a girl who sold matches. *Now blind, the prince* told the swallow to **strip** away his gold and *give* it to the poor. By winter, all the gold was gone, and the cold became too much for the swallow, who died at the prince's feet. At that moment, the prince's lead heart cracked in two from sadness.

**¶6** Without its gold and **jewels**, the statue was removed to make room for a new one. The prince's broken lead heart was thrown away beside the dead swallow. Far above the city, God asked an **angel** to *bring* him the two most **precious** things there. The angel returned with the swallow and the prince's heart, and God said that they would live **forever** in heaven.

## 四大必考文法

1. **one... another／one... the other**：總數不限用 another；只有兩個時第二個用 the other。
2. **the + Adj.**：指一群人（複數，the poor need...）或抽象概念（單數，the impossible）。
3. **授與動詞**：V + 人 + 物 ＝ V + 物 + to/for + 人；物是代名詞時只能用後者（deliver it to her）。
4. **Adj., S + V.**：主詞相同時，省略連接詞與主詞、be 動詞改 being 後再省略（Now blind, the prince told...）。

## 補充句型

- be tired from（因……而累）／in surprise（驚訝地）／fill sb with N（使充滿）
- 所有格關代 whose：a seamstress **whose** son was ill
- 非限定關代：the swallow, **who** died at the prince's feet
- make room for（騰出空間給）／crack in two（裂成兩半）
- the two most precious things（數詞 + most + adj. + N）
