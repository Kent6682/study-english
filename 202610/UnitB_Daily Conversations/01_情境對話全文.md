## Scene 1｜博物館校外教學 A Field Trip to the Museum

📍 高二校外教學，莉莉和傑森跟著導覽員參觀歷史博物館。

本場單字：statue, sword, palace, jewel, precious, military, pilot, navy, originally, represent, visible, shiny, security

**Guide**：Welcome, everyone. This hall shows how people lived in the old **palace** hundreds of years ago.  
　　歡迎大家。這個展廳展示了幾百年前人們在古老宮殿裡的生活。

**Lily**：Jason, look at that **statue** by the door. He looks so serious.  
　　傑森，你看門邊那座雕像，他看起來好嚴肅。

**Jason**：That's a general. See the **sword** in his hand?  
　　那是一位將軍。有看到他手上的劍嗎？

**Guide**：Good eye. He led the **military** in the south, and that weapon **represents** his power.  
　　眼力真好。他曾率領南方的軍隊，那把武器象徵他的權力。

**Lily**：Wow! And what's inside that glass case? Everything looks so **shiny**.  
　　哇！那個玻璃櫃裡面是什麼？每樣東西看起來都好閃亮。

**Guide**：Those are the queen's **jewels**. They're the most **precious** items in the whole museum.  
　　那些是皇后的珠寶，是整座博物館裡最珍貴的收藏。

**Jason**：No wonder there are two **security** guards standing right next to them.  
　　難怪旁邊站著兩位保全人員。

**Guide**：Exactly. Now, this room was **originally** a storeroom. Today it shows old uniforms.  
　　沒錯。這個房間原本是儲藏室，現在用來展示舊制服。

**Lily**：That dark blue jacket must be from the **navy**, right?  
　　那件深藍色外套一定是海軍的吧？

**Guide**：Right. And the leather jacket next to it belonged to a **pilot** in World War II.  
　　對。旁邊那件皮夾克屬於二戰時的一位飛行員。

**Jason**：His name is still **visible** on the pocket. That's so cool.  
　　口袋上的名字還看得見，好酷。

**Lily**：I want to take a picture. Are photos allowed here?  
　　我想拍照。這裡可以拍照嗎？

**Guide**：Yes, just turn off your flash, please.  
　　可以，請記得關掉閃光燈。

**Jason**：Got it. Lily, stand next to that jacket. I'll take one for you.  
　　了解。莉莉，站到那件夾克旁邊，我幫你拍一張。

### 實用口語句型

- **Good eye.**　眼力真好。稱讚對方觀察入微
- **No wonder ...**　難怪……
- **Are photos allowed here?**　這裡可以拍照嗎？
- **Got it.**　了解、知道了

## Scene 2｜換季逛街買衣服 Shopping for Summer Clothes

📍 放學後，莉莉和傑森到服飾店挑夏天的衣服。

本場單字：sleeve, loose, extra, introduce, product, release, chain, plastic, bold, daring

**Lily**：It's getting hot. I need some new clothes for summer.  
　　天氣變熱了，我需要買幾件夏天的新衣服。

**Jason**：This store is part of a big **chain**. They have shops all over Taiwan.  
　　這間店是大型連鎖店，全台灣都有分店。

**Clerk**：Hi there! We just **released** our summer collection this week.  
　　嗨！我們這週剛推出夏季新品。

**Lily**：Great. Do you have this shirt with short **sleeves**?  
　　太好了。這件襯衫有短袖的嗎？

**Clerk**：Yes, and it's a bit **loose**, so it feels cool on hot days.  
　　有，而且版型有點寬鬆，大熱天穿起來很涼爽。

**Jason**：What about this orange one? The color is really **bold**.  
　　那這件橘色的呢？顏色好大膽。

**Lily**：Maybe too bright for me. But you should try that **daring** green jacket!  
　　對我來說可能太亮了。不過你應該試試那件超敢的綠色外套！

**Jason**：No way! People would stare at me all day.  
　　才不要！大家會一整天盯著我看。

**Clerk**：By the way, we've **introduced** a new rule. We don't give out **plastic** bags anymore.  
　　對了，我們推出了一項新規定：不再提供塑膠袋。

**Lily**：That's fine. I brought my own bag.  
　　沒關係，我有帶自己的袋子。

**Clerk**：Thanks! And if you buy two items, you get an **extra** 10% off.  
　　謝謝！買兩件的話，再額外打九折。

**Jason**：Then I'll get these socks too. Are they a new **product**?  
　　那我也買這雙襪子。這是新產品嗎？

**Clerk**：Yes, they're made from recycled bottles.  
　　是的，是用回收寶特瓶做的。

**Lily**：Cool. Saving money and helping the planet—perfect!  
　　酷，省錢又愛地球，完美！

### 實用口語句型

- **It's getting hot.**　天氣變熱了。get + 形容詞表示「變得……」
- **No way!**　才不要！／不可能！
- **By the way, ...**　對了、順帶一提
- **10% off**　打九折（off 後面是「減掉」的百分比）

## Scene 3｜晚餐的香菜之爭 The Great Coriander Debate

📍 傑森到莉莉家做分組報告，留下來吃晚餐。

本場單字：flavor, debate, specific, reaction, raw, oven, spread, process, hunger, admit

**Mom**：Dinner's ready! I made beef noodle soup, and the chicken is still in the **oven**.  
　　晚餐好了！我煮了牛肉麵，雞肉還在烤箱裡。

**Jason**：It smells amazing. I could die of **hunger** right now!  
　　聞起來好香。我現在快餓死了！

**Mom**：Help yourself. You can put some coriander on top.  
　　自己來，上面可以加點香菜。

**Lily**：Oh no, here we go again. Mom and I **debate** this every week.  
　　喔不，又來了。我跟我媽每個禮拜都在爭論這件事。

**Jason**：Wait, you don't like coriander? I love its fresh **flavor**.  
　　等等，你不喜歡香菜？我很愛它清新的味道。

**Lily**：To me, it tastes like soap. I can't stand it, especially when it's **raw**.  
　　對我來說它吃起來像肥皂，我受不了，尤其是生的時候。

**Mom**：Actually, scientists found a **specific** gene that makes coriander taste like soap to some people.  
　　其實科學家找到一個特定的基因，會讓某些人覺得香菜吃起來像肥皂。

**Jason**：Really? So it's about how your brain **processes** the smell?  
　　真的嗎？所以跟大腦怎麼處理那個氣味有關？

**Mom**：Exactly. Everyone's **reaction** is different. It's not just being picky.  
　　沒錯，每個人的反應都不一樣，不只是挑食而已。

**Lily**：See, Mom? Science is on my side.  
　　看吧，媽？科學站在我這邊。

**Mom**：Fine, I'll **admit** it. But you could still try a little.  
　　好啦，我承認。不過你還是可以試一點點。

**Jason**：Coriander fans are everywhere now. The trend has even **spread** to snacks and ice cream!  
　　現在到處都是香菜迷，這股風潮甚至擴散到零食和冰淇淋了！

**Lily**：Coriander ice cream? That's my worst nightmare.  
　　香菜冰淇淋？那是我最可怕的惡夢。

**Mom**：More for us, then. Jason, pass me your bowl.  
　　那我們就多吃一點。傑森，把你的碗給我。

### 實用口語句型

- **Here we go again.**　又來了。對重複發生的事表示無奈
- **I can't stand it.**　我受不了。stand 在此指「忍受」
- **... is on my side.**　……站在我這邊
- **More for us, then.**　那我們就多吃一點。對方不吃時的玩笑話

## Scene 4｜吵架後和好 Making Up After a Fight

📍 午休時間，傑森和好朋友阿肯吵架了，跑來找莉莉商量。

本場單字：advice, confident, apologize, situation, replace, significant, angel, forever, courage

**Lily**：Jason, you look down. What's wrong?  
　　傑森，你看起來很沮喪，怎麼了？

**Jason**：Ken and I had a fight yesterday. He hasn't talked to me since.  
　　我跟阿肯昨天吵架了，他到現在都沒跟我說話。

**Lily**：About what?  
　　為了什麼事？

**Jason**：I made fun of his drawing in front of everyone. I thought it was just a joke.  
　　我在大家面前取笑他的畫，我以為只是開玩笑。

**Lily**：Hmm. That drawing was for the art contest. It's **significant** to him.  
　　嗯……那幅畫是要參加美術比賽的，對他來說很重要。

**Jason**：I know. Can I ask for your **advice**? I don't know how to fix this **situation**.  
　　我知道。可以給我一點建議嗎？我不知道該怎麼收拾這個局面。

**Lily**：Just **apologize** to him face-to-face. Don't do it by text.  
　　直接當面跟他道歉，不要用傳訊息的。

**Jason**：But I'm not **confident** that he'll forgive me.  
　　可是我沒把握他會原諒我。

**Lily**：It takes **courage**, but he'll see that you mean it.  
　　這需要勇氣，但他會看出你是真心的。

**Jason**：Should I get him new markers to **replace** his old, dried-up ones?  
　　要不要買新的麥克筆給他，換掉他那些乾掉的舊筆？

**Lily**：That's a sweet idea. Just don't let the gift do all the talking.  
　　這主意很貼心，只是別讓禮物替你把話都說完。

**Jason**：Got it. Thanks, Lily. You're an **angel**.  
　　懂了。謝謝你，莉莉，你人真好。

**Lily**：Ha! Just go talk to him. Friends like Ken don't come along every day.  
　　哈！快去找他談吧。像阿肯這樣的朋友可不是天天都遇得到。

**Jason**：You're right. I don't want to lose him **forever**.  
　　你說得對，我不想永遠失去這個朋友。

### 實用口語句型

- **What's wrong?**　怎麼了？
- **make fun of sb/sth**　取笑某人／某事
- **face-to-face**　當面地
- **mean it**　是認真的、是真心的

## Scene 5｜演唱會那一晚 A Night at the Concert

📍 莉莉和傑森到體育場看最喜歡的歌手開唱。

本場單字：award, stadium, engagement, quit, focus, creative, bomb, firework, classic, unity, toward

**Jason**：I can't believe we're finally at the **stadium**! There must be 40,000 people here.  
　　不敢相信我們終於到體育場了！這裡一定有四萬人。

**Lily**：Let's walk **toward** the main gate. Our seats are on the left side.  
　　我們往大門走吧，我們的座位在左邊。

**Jason**：Did you know she won another **award** last month?  
　　你知道她上個月又得了一個獎嗎？

**Lily**：Of course! Her new album is so **creative**. Every song sounds different.  
　　當然！她的新專輯超有創意，每首歌聽起來都不一樣。

**Jason**：I heard she almost **quit** music a few years ago.  
　　聽說她幾年前差點放棄音樂。

**Lily**：Yeah, it was a hard time for her. Now her **focus** is on writing her own songs.  
　　對，那段時間她過得很辛苦。現在她的重心放在自己寫歌。

**Jason**：And she just announced her **engagement**, too! Everyone is so happy for her.  
　　而且她剛宣布訂婚！大家都替她開心。

**Lily**：Shh, the lights are going down. It's starting!  
　　噓，燈暗了，要開始了！

*(After the show)*

**Jason**：Wow. That show was the **bomb**!  
　　哇，這場表演太讚了！

**Lily**：The **fireworks** at the end were unbelievable.  
　　最後的煙火真是不可思議。

**Jason**：My favorite part was when she sang that **classic** from her first album.  
　　我最喜歡她唱第一張專輯那首經典歌的時候。

**Lily**：Me too! Forty thousand people singing together—I've never felt that much **unity**.  
　　我也是！四萬人一起合唱，我從來沒感受過這麼強的團結感。

**Jason**：My voice is gone, but it was totally worth it.  
　　我的聲音都啞了，但完全值得。

**Lily**：Let's hurry to the MRT before the crowd gets worse.  
　　我們趕快去捷運站，趁人潮還沒變得更擠。

### 實用口語句型

- **There must be ...**　一定有……（有把握的推測）
- **the bomb**　（俚語）超讚的。只用在口語，寫作和考試不要用
- **It was totally worth it.**　完全值得
- **before the crowd gets worse**　趁人潮變得更擠之前

## Scene 6｜社團練習 Club Practice

📍 排球社練球，教練提醒大家注意安全，還宣布一個好消息。

本場單字：stretch, sweat, strip, suffering, protect, excellence, promote, bar

**Coach**：OK, team. Before we start, I want everyone to **stretch** for ten minutes.  
　　好，各位。開始之前，每個人先伸展十分鐘。

**Jason**：Coach, my knee still hurts a little from last week.  
　　教練，我的膝蓋上週受的傷還有點痛。

**Coach**：Then wear your knee pad. It will **protect** your knee when you dive for the ball.  
　　那就戴上護膝，你撲球的時候它能保護你的膝蓋。

**Jason**：It's not that bad, though. I can still play.  
　　不過沒那麼嚴重啦，我還可以打。

**Coach**：Jason, don't keep **suffering** in silence. Tell me when something hurts, OK?  
　　傑森，別一直默默忍著，哪裡痛就跟我說，好嗎？

**Jason**：Yes, Coach. I will.  
　　是，教練，我會的。

*(One hour later)*

**Lily**：Phew, I'm covered in **sweat**. It's so hot in this gym.  
　　呼，我全身都是汗，體育館裡好熱。

**Coach**：Good work today. **Strip** off those wet jackets and put on something dry, or you'll catch a cold.  
　　今天表現很好。把濕掉的外套脫掉，換上乾的衣服，不然會感冒。

**Jason**：Does anyone have a snack? I'm starving.  
　　有人有點心嗎？我快餓死了。

**Lily**：Here, have a protein **bar**. I always bring a few.  
　　來，吃條蛋白棒。我都會帶幾條。

**Coach**：By the way, the school wants to **promote** our club at the open house next month.  
　　對了，學校想在下個月的校園開放日宣傳我們社團。

**Lily**：Really? That's great news!  
　　真的嗎？太好了！

**Coach**：They said our team shows **excellence** in both sports and teamwork.  
　　他們說我們隊在運動和團隊合作上都表現卓越。

**Jason**：Then we'd better win next week's game.  
　　那我們下週的比賽最好要贏。

**Coach**：Exactly. Same time tomorrow. Don't be late!  
　　沒錯。明天同一時間，不要遲到！

### 實用口語句型

- **suffer in silence**　默默忍受、不說出來
- **catch a cold**　感冒
- **I'm starving.**　我快餓死了（口語誇飾）
- **We'd better ...**　我們最好……（had better + 原形動詞）

## Scene 7｜畢旅規劃夏威夷 Dreaming of Hawaii

📍 班上決定畢業旅行的目的地，莉莉和傑森負責規劃行程。

　　全班投票了。如果存得到錢，我們的畢業旅行要去夏威夷！

**Jason**：Nice! I want a **relaxed** trip—beaches, sunshine, and no homework.  
　　讚！我想要輕鬆的旅程：海灘、陽光，還有沒有作業。

**Lily**：I get the **appeal**, but I also want to learn something.  
　　我懂那種吸引力，但我也想學點東西。

**Jason**：OK, what's on your list?  
　　好吧，你的清單上有什麼？

**Lily**：First, a show about **native** Hawaiian culture, with music and dancing.  
　　首先，一場介紹夏威夷原住民文化的表演，有音樂和舞蹈。

**Jason**：Sounds good. I also read about a hiking trail that goes through an old **tunnel**.  
　　聽起來不錯。我還看到一條健行步道會穿過一條舊隧道。

**Lily**：Cool. If we drive **farther** north, there are beaches with sea turtles.  
　　酷。如果再往北開，有可以看到海龜的海灘。

**Jason**：Sea turtles! Can we stay in a beach cabin up there?  
　　海龜！我們可以住在那邊的海灘小木屋嗎？

**Lily**：Some are really simple. One cabin doesn't even have **electricity**!  
　　有些非常簡陋，有一間小木屋甚至沒有電！

**Jason**：No power means no phones. Maybe that's not so bad.  
　　沒電就不能滑手機，或許也沒那麼糟。

**Lily**：Ha! For shopping, we can walk along Kalakaua **Avenue** in Waikiki.  
　　哈！購物的話，我們可以去逛威基基的卡拉卡瓦大道。

**Jason**：And let's take photos at sunset, when the palm trees cast long **shadows**.  
　　還有我們在日落時拍照吧，那時棕櫚樹會拉出長長的影子。

**Lily**：Love it. I'll make a budget plan. **Meanwhile**, you check flight prices.  
　　好喜歡。我來做預算表，你同時去查機票價格。

**Jason**：Deal. Let's start saving our lunch money!  
　　成交。我們開始存午餐錢吧！

### 實用口語句型

- **I get the appeal.**　我懂它吸引人的地方
- **Sounds good.**　聽起來不錯
- **cast long shadows**　拉出長長的影子
- **Deal.**　成交、就這麼說定了

## Scene 8｜期末讀書計畫 A Study Plan for Finals

📍 期末考前三週，莉莉幫傑森擬定讀書計畫。

本場單字：goal, maintain, task, skip, achievement, memory, further, brief, track, progress

**Jason**：Finals are in three weeks, and I haven't started studying. I'm starting to panic.  
　　期末考剩三週，我還沒開始讀，我開始慌了。

**Lily**：Don't worry. Let's set a **goal** together. What score do you want in math?  
　　別擔心，我們一起訂個目標。你數學想考幾分？

**Jason**：Seventy would be great. Last time I got fifty-five.  
　　七十分就很棒了，上次我考五十五。

**Lily**：OK. Break the big job into small **tasks**, like one chapter a day.  
　　好。把大工作拆成小任務，像是一天讀一章。

**Jason**：But I can't study for hours. My **memory** is terrible.  
　　可是我沒辦法一次讀好幾個小時，我的記憶力超差。

**Lily**：Then take **brief** breaks. Study for 25 minutes, then rest for 5.  
　　那就短暫休息一下。讀二十五分鐘，休息五分鐘。

**Jason**：That sounds doable. But what if I get lazy?  
　　聽起來做得到。可是如果我變懶怎麼辦？

**Lily**：Use an app to **track** your study time. Seeing the numbers will keep you going.  
　　用 app 記錄你的讀書時間，看到數字會讓你繼續下去。

**Jason**：And what if I miss one day?  
　　那如果我有一天沒讀呢？

**Lily**：Just never **skip** two days in a row. That's the key to **maintaining** a habit.  
　　只要別連續兩天沒讀就好，這是維持習慣的關鍵。

**Jason**：Got it. Can we check each other's **progress** every Friday?  
　　了解。我們每週五互相檢查進度好不好？

**Lily**：Sure. And if you reach seventy, that's a real **achievement**. We'll celebrate.  
　　當然。如果你考到七十分，那就是真正的成就，我們來慶祝。

**Jason**：Bubble tea's on me! Maybe I can even take it **further** and aim for eighty.  
　　珍奶我請！說不定我還能更進一步，挑戰八十分。

**Lily**：That's the spirit! Now, open your book to chapter one.  
　　就是這個精神！現在，把書翻到第一章。

### 實用口語句型

- **in a row**　連續地
- **That sounds doable.**　聽起來做得到
- **Bubble tea's on me!**　珍奶我請！X is on me 表示「X 我請客」
- **That's the spirit!**　就是這個精神！
