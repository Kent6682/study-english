【一、完整英文課文（含行號與段落）】

──────────────────── Day 20 (p. 38) ────────────────────
[Paragraph 1] (Lines 01–08)
October is one of the best months to visit Oahu, the third-largest of the Hawaiian Islands. The days are sunny, the ocean is still warm enough for swimming, and gentle trade winds keep the afternoons comfortable. With the summer crowds gone, the island feels more relaxed.[1]

[Paragraph 2] (Lines 09–18)
Nature is a big part of Oahu's appeal.[2] Diamond Head, an old volcanic crater on the edge of Waikiki, is the island's most famous hike. The trail passes through a tunnel[3] and climbs to a century-old lookout* with sweeping views of the coast. Farther[4] north, the North Shore is known for giant winter waves, but calmer waters in other areas are perfect for snorkeling* or a boat tour.

[Paragraph 3] (Lines 19–28)
Beyond its beaches and scenery,* Oahu also has an interesting history. In the heart of Honolulu, 'Iolani Palace is the only royal palace in the US. King Kalākaua loved new technology so much that he lit the building with electricity[5] in 1887—years before the White House. Inside, the grand koa stairs and historic throne* room offer a look into Hawaii's royal past.

──────────────────── Day 21 (p. 40) ────────────────────
[Paragraph 4] (Lines 01–12)
Oahu's food tells the story of the people who have called it home. Native[1] Hawaiian dishes include poke, fresh raw[2] fish, and kalua pork, which is slow-cooked in an underground oven[3] until smoky and tender. There are also many dishes that were brought by immigrants.* Shave ice, for example, is a fluffy treat with sweet fruit syrups that was introduced from Japan. At a traditional luau, visitors can sample many of these foods while enjoying live music and hula dancing.

[Paragraph 5] (Lines 13–21)
The end of October brings a different kind of excitement. Kalākaua Avenue[4] in Waikiki fills with people in creative costumes,* which turns the famous street into a lively Halloween celebration.* For those who want a real scare, the Haunted Plantation* in Waipahu is worth a visit. As you walk through the dark village, actors hide in the shadows,[5] ready to jump out at any moment!

[Paragraph 6] (Lines 22–28)
With warm days and smaller crowds, October in Oahu has a way of surprising you. Whether you're hiking Diamond Head or enjoying shave ice at sunset, it's easy to slow down and settle into island life.

【二、段落中英對照】

[第一段｜Day 20]
EN: October is one of the best months to visit Oahu, the third-largest of the Hawaiian Islands. The days are sunny, the ocean is still warm enough for swimming, and gentle trade winds keep the afternoons comfortable. With the summer crowds gone, the island feels more relaxed.
ZH: 十月是造訪夏威夷群島第三大島——歐胡島的最佳月份之一。白天天氣晴朗，海水依然溫暖得足以游泳，而溫和的信風讓午後保持舒適宜人。隨著夏日的人潮散去，整座島嶼感覺更加悠閒放鬆。

[第二段｜Day 20]
EN: Nature is a big part of Oahu's appeal. Diamond Head, an old volcanic crater on the edge of Waikiki, is the island's most famous hike. The trail passes through a tunnel and climbs to a century-old lookout with sweeping views of the coast. Farther north, the North Shore is known for giant winter waves, but calmer waters in other areas are perfect for snorkeling or a boat tour.
ZH: 大自然是歐胡島魅力的一大來源。位於威基基邊緣的古老火山口「鑽石頭山」，是島上最著名的健行路線。步道穿過一條隧道，並向上攀升至一座擁有壯闊海岸景觀的百年瞭望臺。更往北走，北岸（North Shore）以巨大的冬季海浪聞名，但其他地區較平靜的水域則非常適合浮潛或搭船遊覽。

[第三段｜Day 20]
EN: Beyond its beaches and scenery, Oahu also has an interesting history. In the heart of Honolulu, 'Iolani Palace is the only royal palace in the US. King Kalākaua loved new technology so much that he lit the building with electricity in 1887—years before the White House. Inside, the grand koa stairs and historic throne room offer a look into Hawaii's royal past.
ZH: 除了海灘與風景之外，歐胡島也有著一段有趣的歷史。位於檀香山中心地帶的伊奧拉尼宮（'Iolani Palace），是美國境內唯一的王宮。卡拉卡瓦國王（King Kalākaua）非常熱愛新科技，以致於他在 1887 年就用電力點亮了這座建築——比白宮還早了好幾年。在王宮內部，氣派的相思木樓梯與具歷史意義的王座廳，讓人一窺夏威夷的王室過往。

[第四段｜Day 21]
EN: Oahu's food tells the story of the people who have called it home. Native Hawaiian dishes include poke, fresh raw fish, and kalua pork, which is slow-cooked in an underground oven until smoky and tender. There are also many dishes that were brought by immigrants. Shave ice, for example, is a fluffy treat with sweet fruit syrups that was introduced from Japan. At a traditional luau, visitors can sample many of these foods while enjoying live music and hula dancing.
ZH: 歐胡島的美食訴說著那些以這座島嶼為家的人們的故事。夏威夷原住民料理包括「波奇」（poke，新鮮生魚塊）以及「卡魯瓦豬肉」（kalua pork）——這種豬肉會在地下烤爐中慢火烘烤，直到帶有煙燻風味且肉質軟嫩。還有許多料理是由外來移民帶來的。舉例來說，夏威夷刨冰（shave ice）就是一種淋上香甜水果糖漿、口感蓬鬆綿密的甜點，最初是由日本引進。在傳統的夏威夷宴會（luau）上，遊客可以一邊欣賞現場音樂與草裙舞，一邊品嚐許多這類美食。

[第五段｜Day 21]
EN: The end of October brings a different kind of excitement. Kalākaua Avenue in Waikiki fills with people in creative costumes, which turns the famous street into a lively Halloween celebration. For those who want a real scare, the Haunted Plantation in Waipahu is worth a visit. As you walk through the dark village, actors hide in the shadows, ready to jump out at any moment!
ZH: 十月底則帶來另一種截然不同的刺激感。威基基的卡拉卡瓦大道（Kalākaua Avenue）擠滿了身穿創意服裝的人們，將這條知名街道變成一場熱鬧的萬聖節慶典。對於那些想要體驗真正驚嚇感的人來說，位於瓦伊帕胡（Waipahu）的「鬧鬼莊園」（Haunted Plantation）值得一遊。當你走過漆黑的村莊時，演員們躲藏在陰暗處，隨時準備跳出來嚇人！

[第六段｜Day 21]
EN: With warm days and smaller crowds, October in Oahu has a way of surprising you. Whether you're hiking Diamond Head or enjoying shave ice at sunset, it's easy to slow down and settle into island life.
ZH: 有著溫暖的白天與較少的人潮，十月的歐胡島總有辦法帶給你驚喜。無論你是在鑽石頭山健行，或是在夕陽下享用刨冰，都能輕易地放慢腳步，融入愜意的海島生活。

*1. relaxed [rɪˋlækst] adj. 輕鬆的、悠閒的；放鬆的（*為大考出現過的字彙）
    • Mia looked relaxed after the test.
      （蜜雅在考試後看起來很輕鬆。）

*2. appeal [əˋpil] n. 吸引力、魅力
    • Seth doesn't understand the appeal of baseball.
      （賽斯不懂棒球的魅力何在。）

*3. tunnel [ˋtʌnl] n. 隧道；地道
    • Cars moved slowly in the tunnel.
      （汽車在隧道裡緩慢行駛。）

*4. farther [ˋfɑrðɚ] adv. （空間距離）更遠地（之後可接表方向的副詞，文中 farther north 即指「更往北、更北方」）
    • Farther south, the river becomes wider.
      （更往南走，這條河變得更寬闊。）

*5. electricity [ɪ͵lɛkˋtrɪsətɪ] n. 電力、電
    • Without electricity, the building was dark.
