# ⚡ Unit 9: A Taste of Hawaii: Exploring Oahu — 深度文法與翻譯解析

## 一、四大核心文法與關鍵解析（Language Notes）

### 1. `keep + O. + O.C.`（使……保持某種狀態）
- **課文原句**：`...gentle trade winds keep the afternoons comfortable.`（溫和的信風讓午後保持舒適宜人。）
- **核心公式**：
  $$\text{S. + keep + 受詞 (O.) + 受詞補語 (O.C.)}$$
- **五大受詞補語（O.C.）型態**：
  1. **形容詞（Adj.）**：說明受詞的狀態（⚠️ **不可用副詞**！）
     - *The teacher kept the class **quiet**.*（老師讓全班保持安靜。）
  2. **名詞（N.）**：說明受詞的身分或性質
     - *Charlotte decided to keep it **a secret**.*（夏綠蒂決定保守這個秘密。）
  3. **現在分詞（V-ing）**：受詞與動作為「主動、持續進行」關係
     - *The story kept Mason **thinking**.*（這個故事讓梅森一直思考著。）
  4. **過去分詞（p.p.）**：受詞與動作為「被動、已完成狀態」關係
     - *Abigail kept her phone **charged**.*（艾比蓋兒一直讓手機保持充電狀態。）
  5. **介系詞片語（Prep. Phrase）**：說明受詞所在的位置或處境
     - *Tristan kept the medicine **in the fridge**.*（崔斯坦把藥放在冰箱裡保存。）

- **【延伸必考】`with + O. + O.C.` 附帶狀況分詞構句（由於……；隨著……）**
  - **課文第 6 行**：`With the summer crowds gone, the island feels more relaxed.`
  - `gone` 為過去分詞作受詞補語，表「夏日人潮已經散去」的完成狀態。

---

### 2. `so + Adj./Adv. + that + S. + V.`（如此……以致於……）
- **課文原句**：`King Kalākaua loved new technology so much that he lit the building with electricity in 1887—years before the White House.`
- **核心對比（`so` vs. `such`）**：
  | 句型結構 | 強調重點 | 課本／大考例句 |
  | :--- | :--- | :--- |
  | **so + Adj./Adv. + that + S. + V.** | 強調**形容詞或副詞** | *The box was **so heavy that** Eden couldn't lift it.*<br/>*It rained **so hard that** Finn had to run inside.* |
  | **such + a/an + Adj. + 單數可數名詞 + that** | 強調**單數可數名詞** | *It was **such a heavy box that** Eden couldn't lift it.* |
  | **such + Adj. + 複數/不可數名詞 + that** | 強調**複數/不可數名詞** | *Oahu has **such beautiful scenery that** tourists love it.*（`scenery` 不可數！） |
  | **so + many/much/few/little + N. + that** | 強調**數量多少** | *There were **so many people that** we couldn't move.* |

---

### 3. `those who + V.`（凡是……的人；那些……的人）
- **課文原句**：`For those who want a real scare, the Haunted Plantation in Waipahu is worth a visit.`
- **核心規則**：
  1. `those` 為複數代名詞（指「那些人」），後方由主格關係代名詞 `who` 引導形容詞子句修飾 `those`。
  2. **雙重複數動詞陷阱**：
     - `who` 後方的關係子句動詞必須用**複數動詞**（如 `want` / `help`）。
     - 若整個 `Those who + V1...` 當主句主詞，主句動詞（`V2`）也必須用**複數動詞**（如 `earn`）！
  - **課本例句**：*`Those who help` others `earn` respect.*（幫助他人的人能獲得尊重。）

---

### 4. `Whether A or B, S. + V.`（無論 A 或是 B，……）
- **課文原句**：`Whether you're hiking Diamond Head or [you're] enjoying shave ice at sunset, it's easy to slow down and settle into island life.`
- **核心規則**：
  1. `whether ... or ...` 引導**讓步副詞子句**，表「不管情況是 A 還是 B，主要子句的結果都不受影響」。
  2. **省略機制**：當 `whether` 與 `or` 連接的兩個子句主詞相同且含 `be` 動詞時，可省略 `or` 後方重複的 `主詞 + be 動詞`（如文中省略了 `you're`）。
  3. **大考防呆**：表「無論……」的讓步副詞子句**只能用 `Whether`，絕對不可用 `If` 替換**！
  - **課本例句**：
    - *`Whether` Lauren calls me `or` I call her, we talk every night.*
    - *`Whether` Hudson wins `or` loses, he learns something.*

## 二、全課 19 句逐句結構與中翻英寫作秘訣

1. **October is one of the best months to visit Oahu, the third-largest of the Hawaiian Islands.**
   - `one of the + 最高級 + 複數名詞`（最……的……之一），後方 `the third-largest of the Hawaiian Islands` 為 `Oahu` 的同位語。
2. **The days are sunny, the ocean is still warm enough for swimming, and gentle trade winds keep the afternoons comfortable.**
   - `Adj./Adv. + enough + for N. / to V.`（足夠……可以……），注意 `enough` 修飾形容詞 `warm` 時必放其後！
3. **With the summer crowds gone, the island feels more relaxed.**
   - `feel + Adj.` 連綴動詞接過去分詞轉化之形容詞 `relaxed`（感到放鬆的、悠閒的）。
4. **Nature is a big part of Oahu's appeal.**
   - `a big part of + N.`（……的一大關鍵／重要部分）。
5. **Diamond Head, an old volcanic crater on the edge of Waikiki, is the island's most famous hike.**
   - 善用雙逗號同位語 `, an old volcanic crater on the edge of Waikiki,` 濃縮背景資訊。
6. **The trail passes through a tunnel and climbs to a century-old lookout with sweeping views of the coast.**
   - 對等連接詞 `and` 連接兩個第三人稱單數動詞 `passes through` 與 `climbs to`；`century-old` 為連字號複合形容詞，故 `century` 維持單數。
7. **Farther north, the North Shore is known for giant winter waves, but calmer waters in other areas are perfect for snorkeling or a boat tour.**
   - `Farther + 方向副詞 (north/south)` 表空間距離「更往北／更往南」；`be known for` 表「以……特色聞名」。
8. **Beyond its beaches and scenery, Oahu also has an interesting history.**
   - `Beyond + N.` 置於句首作轉折介系詞片語，表「除了……之外（還有）」，相當於 `In addition to / Besides`。
9. **In the heart of Honolulu, 'Iolani Palace is the only royal palace in the US.**
   - `in the heart of + 城市`（在……的市中心／核心地帶）。
10. **King Kalākaua loved new technology so much that he lit the building with electricity in 1887—years before the White House.**
    - `light` 的三態為 `light – lit – lit`（或 `lighted`）；`light A with B` 表「用 B 點亮 A」。
11. **Inside, the grand koa stairs and historic throne room offer a look into Hawaii's royal past.**
    - `offer a look into + N.`（讓人一窺……的樣貌）。
12. **Oahu's food tells the story of the people who have called it home.**
    - `call + 地點 + home` 為 `call + O. + O.C.` 結構，表「以某地為家、定居於某地」。
13. **Native Hawaiian dishes include poke, fresh raw fish, and kalua pork, which is slow-cooked in an underground oven until smoky and tender.**
    - `, which is slow-cooked...` 為非限定關係子句補充說明專有名詞 `kalua pork`；`until smoky and tender` 為 `until it is smoky and tender` 之省略。
14. **There are also many dishes that were brought by immigrants.**
    - `that were brought by immigrants` 為限定關係子句（被動語態）修飾 `many dishes`。
15. **Shave ice, for example, is a fluffy treat with sweet fruit syrups that was introduced from Japan.**
    - 先行詞為單數 `a fluffy treat`（附上介系詞片語 `with sweet fruit syrups`），故關係子句動詞用單數 `that was introduced`。
16. **At a traditional luau, visitors can sample many of these foods while enjoying live music and hula dancing.**
    - `while enjoying...` 為分詞構句（原為 `while they are enjoying...`）。
17. **The end of October brings a different kind of excitement.**
    - 無生命主詞（時間 `The end of October`）搭配及物動詞 `brings`，為道地英文寫作手法。
18. **Kalākaua Avenue in Waikiki fills with people in creative costumes, which turns the famous street into a lively Halloween celebration.**
    - `, which turns...` 以 `which` 代表前面整個主要子句的事件，故動詞 `turns` 用單數！`turn A into B` 表「把 A 變成 B」。
19. **For those who want a real scare, the Haunted Plantation in Waipahu is worth a visit.**
    - `be worth a visit` = `be worth visiting`（值得一遊）。
20. **As you walk through the dark village, actors hide in the shadows, ready to jump out at any moment!**
    - `, ready to jump out...` 為形容詞片語作主詞補語，補充說明主詞 `actors` 躲在暗處時的狀態。
21. **With warm days and smaller crowds, October in Oahu has a way of surprising you.**
    - `have a way of + V-ing`（總有辦法……；往往會……）。
22. **Whether you're hiking Diamond Head or enjoying shave ice at sunset, it's easy to slow down and settle into island life.**
    - `it is + Adj. + to V.` 虛主詞句型，搭配 `slow down`（放慢步調）與 `settle into`（融入、安頓於……）。
