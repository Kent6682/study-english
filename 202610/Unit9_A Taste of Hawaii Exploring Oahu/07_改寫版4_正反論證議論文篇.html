📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（正反論證議論文版・約 184 字）
⚖️ 文體特色：採用「Many tourists assume... However... First... Second... Therefore...」標準學測論說文架構！
Many tourists assume that Oahu is merely a crowded summer beach resort. However, visiting in October reveals that the island’s true appeal extends far beyond sunbathing. First, with the summer crowds gone, the island becomes far more relaxed as gentle trade winds keep the afternoons comfortable. Travelers can hike through Diamond Head’s tunnel to a century-old lookout or head farther north for peaceful snorkeling. Second, beyond coastal scenery, Oahu preserves remarkable history at ’Iolani Palace, where King Kalākaua valued new technology so much that he wired the building with electricity in 1887—years before the White House—illuminating its koa throne room.
許多遊客以為歐胡島只是一個擁擠的夏日海灘度假勝地。然而，在十月造訪會發現，這座島嶼真正的魅力遠不止於日光浴。首先，隨著夏日人潮散去，當溫和的信風讓午後保持舒適宜人時，整座島嶼變得更加悠閒放鬆。旅客可以穿過鑽石頭山的隧道健行至百年瞭望臺，或前往更北方享受寧靜的浮潛。其次，除了海岸風景之外，歐胡島在伊奧拉尼宮保存了非凡的歷史——卡拉卡瓦國王如此重視新科技，以致於他在 1887 年就為這棟建築鋪設電力線路，比白宮早了好幾年照亮其相思木王座廳。
Furthermore, Oahu’s cuisine embodies the heritage of people who have called it home, combining Native Hawaiian poke (raw fish) and kalua pork slow-cooked in an underground oven with fluffy shave ice introduced by Japanese immigrants at traditional luaus. Finally, late October offers unique cultural thrills: Kalākaua Avenue hosts a lively Halloween celebration filled with creative costumes, while Waipahu’s Haunted Plantation satisfies those who seek a real scare with actors hiding in the shadows. Therefore, whether one prefers exploring royal heritage or savoring local flavors, October proves the ideal season to settle into island life.
此外，歐胡島的美食體現了那些以這座島嶼為家的人們的文化遺產，在傳統夏威夷宴會上結合了原住民生魚波奇、地下烤爐慢火烘烤的卡魯瓦豬肉，以及日本移民引進的蓬鬆刨冰。最後，十月下旬提供了獨特的文化刺激感：卡拉卡瓦大道舉辦充滿創意裝扮的熱鬧萬聖節慶典，而瓦伊帕胡的鬧鬼莊園則以躲在陰暗處的演員滿足那些尋求真正驚嚇感的人。因此，無論一個人偏好探索王室遺產或是細細品味在地風味，十月都證明是融入海島生活的最理想季節。

Part A — 克漏字綜合測驗 (Cloze Test, 第 1–5 題)
Many tourists assume that Oahu is merely a summer beach resort. (__1__), visiting in October reveals that its true appeal extends far beyond sunbathing. First, with the summer crowds gone, gentle trade winds (__2__) while travelers hike through Diamond Head’s tunnel or head farther north for snorkeling. Second, beyond coastal (__3__), ’Iolani Palace showcases royal innovation: King Kalākaua valued new technology so much that he wired the building with electricity in 1887. Furthermore, local cuisine combines Native Hawaiian raw fish poke and kalua pork cooked in an underground oven with Japanese shave ice. Finally, Waipahu’s Haunted Plantation satisfies (__4__) seek a real scare. Therefore, (__5__) one prefers history or food, October is the ideal time to visit.

1. 請選出最適合填入 (__1__) 的轉折副詞：

✅ 正解 (B) However！前句提出「許多遊客以為歐胡島只是夏日海灘度假村」的迷思，後句翻轉指出「十月造訪能展現超越日光浴的真正魅力」，前後為轉折對比關係。

2. 請選出最適合填入 (__2__) 的結構：

✅ 正解 (A) keep the afternoons comfortable！keep + O. + Adj. 結構。

3. 請選出最適合填入 (__3__) 的名詞：

✅ 正解 (C) scenery！scenery 為不可數名詞，不加 a 也沒有複數形 sceneries（若用可數名詞 view 則須加 s 或冠詞）。

4. 請選出最適合填入 (__4__) 的詞彙：

✅ 正解 (C) those who！後方接複數動詞 seek，必用 those who。

5. 請選出最適合填入 (__5__) 的連接詞：

✅ 正解 (B) whether！引導讓步副詞子句 whether one prefers history or food。
Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (第 6–9 題)

6. In the 19th century, sugar ________ in Hawaii attracted thousands of immigrant workers from Japan, China, and Portugal.

✅ 正解 (A) plantations（大型農場、種植園、莊園）！19 世紀夏威夷的蔗糖「種植園」吸引了大批亞洲與歐洲移民勞工。

7. The lively street festival turned the quiet avenue into a massive ________ of local music, dance, and food.

✅ 正解 (C) celebration（慶典、慶祝活動）！將寧靜的大道變成一場盛大的在地音樂、舞蹈與美食「慶典」。

8. Rumor has it that the abandoned mansion on the hill is ________, and strange noises can be heard at midnight.

✅ 正解 (D) haunted（鬧鬼的）！山丘上的廢棄豪宅據傳「鬧鬼」。

9. ________ its famous beaches, Oahu also offers visitors a rich royal history and diverse culinary traditions.

✅ 正解 (A) Beyond（除了……之外還有）！對應課文第三段首句 "Beyond its beaches and scenery, Oahu also has an interesting history."
Part C — 重要文法與中翻英除錯 (第 10–12 題)

10. The tour guide kept all the visitors ________ by telling fascinating stories about King Kalākaua’s royal palace.

✅ 正解 (C) interested！keep + O. (all the visitors) + O.C.，因為受詞是「人（遊客）」，表示「使遊客一直保持感興趣的狀態」必用過去分詞 interested！

11. Those who ________ to experience Oahu’s Halloween events ________ advised to book their flights early.

✅ 正解 (C) wish; are！Those who 引導的子句動詞（wish）與主句動詞（are advised）皆須為複數。

12. 【中翻英除錯】「隨著夏日人潮散去，這座島嶼在十月感覺更加悠閒放鬆。」哪一句英文完全正確？

✅ 正解 (B)！(A) 介系詞 With 後不可接過去式動詞 went；(C) 連接詞 Because 後缺少助動詞 have/are；(D) feels 後應接形容詞 relaxed 而非動詞原形 relax。
Part D — 大考閱讀測驗 (第 13–15 題)

13. How does the author organize this argumentative essay?

✅ 正解 (A)！作者先反駁「歐胡島只是夏日海灘勝地」的常見迷思，接著以 First（自然氣候）、Second（王室歷史）、Furthermore（多元美食）、Finally（萬聖節慶典）四個論點支撐主張。

14. According to the essay, how does Oahu’s food reflect its cultural history?

✅ 正解 (B)！歐胡島美食結合了原住民傳統料理（生魚波奇、地下烤爐慢烤豬肉）與外來移民引進的料理（如日式刨冰），體現了多元族群定居於此的歷史。

15. What is the author’s tone toward visiting Oahu in October?

✅ 正解 (C) Persuasive and appreciative（具說服力且讚賞的）！作者透過嚴謹論據說服讀者十月是造訪歐胡島的理想季節，並讚賞其自然與文化價值。
