📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（客觀科普報導改寫版・約 178 字）
📰 文體特色：學測人文地理閱讀測驗最常見的「自然環境＋歷史建築＋文化飲食」客觀報導結構
As the third-largest Hawaiian island, Oahu reaches peak appeal in October. With summer crowds gone, the atmosphere becomes relaxed while steady trade winds keep the afternoons comfortable. Hikers ascending Diamond Head pass through a narrow tunnel to a century-old lookout, whereas farther north, calm coastal waters alongside the North Shore invite snorkeling. Beyond natural scenery, Honolulu’s ’Iolani Palace highlights royal innovation: King Kalākaua admired modern science so much that he equipped the palace with electricity in 1887, illuminating its koa stairs and throne room years before the White House.
身為夏威夷第三大島，歐胡島在十月達到魅力巔峰。隨著夏日人潮散去，整體氛圍變得悠閒放鬆，而穩定的信風則讓午後保持舒適宜人。攀登鑽石頭山的健行者會穿過狹窄隧道抵達百年瞭望臺，而在更往北之處，北岸周邊平靜的海岸水域則吸引人們浮潛。除了自然風景外，檀香山的伊奧拉尼宮更凸顯了王室的創新精神：卡拉卡瓦國王如此推崇現代科學，以致於他在 1887 年就為王宮裝設電力，比白宮早了好幾年照亮其相思木樓梯與王座廳。
Local cuisine reflects diverse groups who have called the island home. Native dishes feature poke made from raw fish and kalua pork roasted in an underground oven, alongside Japanese shave ice brought by immigrants and served at traditional luaus. Late October also transforms Kalākaua Avenue with creative costumes for a vibrant Halloween celebration. Meanwhile, for those who seek thrills, actors at Waipahu’s Haunted Plantation lurk in the shadows. Whether exploring craters or sampling island treats, visitors effortlessly embrace Oahu’s unhurried pace.
當地飲食反映了以這座島嶼為家的多元族群。原住民料理包含以生魚製成的波奇與地下烤爐烘烤的卡魯瓦豬肉，以及由外來移民引進、在傳統夏威夷宴會供應的日式刨冰。十月下旬更以創意裝扮將卡拉卡瓦大道轉變為充滿活力的萬聖節慶典。同時，對於那些尋求刺激感的人而言，瓦伊帕胡鬧鬼莊園的演員正潛伏在陰暗處。無論是探索火山口還是品嚐海島美食，遊客都能毫不費力地擁抱歐胡島從容不迫的步調。
🧩 三、課文「積木拆句」＋ 逐行中翻英秘訣
🛠️ 學習模式切換：
句 1
As the third-largest Hawaiian island, Oahu reaches peak appeal in October.
身為夏威夷第三大島，歐胡島在十月達到魅力巔峰。

[S] 主詞Oahu歐胡島
[V+O] 動詞+受詞reaches peak appeal達到魅力的巔峰
[M] 時間in October.在十月
💡 句首介系詞 As + 身分/地位, S. + V.
把原課文句尾的同位語移到句首作 As the third-largest Hawaiian island, Oahu...，是極佳的同義改寫技巧！
🎯 搭配詞升級
reach peak appeal（達到魅力最高峰）比 is the best month 更具學術報導感。
句 2

[Conj] 對等子句while steady trade winds keep the afternoons comfortable.同時穩定信風使午後保持舒適
💡 雙核心句型合一：With + O. + p.p. ＋ keep + O. + Adj.
在一句內同時示範本課兩大必考文法結構！
🎯 同義替換秘訣
原課文 gentle trade winds 換成 steady trade winds（穩定的信風）。
句 3

[V+M] 動詞片語pass through a narrow tunnel to a century-old lookout,穿過窄隧道抵達百年瞭望臺
[Conj] 對比連接詞whereas farther north, calm coastal waters invite snorkeling.然而更往北，平靜水域吸引浮潛
💡 現在分詞後位修飾：Hikers (who ascend) $\rightarrow$ Hikers ascending...
省略關係代名詞並將主動動詞改為 ascending（攀登）。
🎯 對比連接詞 whereas
在科普報導中，常用 whereas（然而、反觀）取代口語的 but。

Part A — 克漏字綜合測驗 (Cloze Test, 第 1–5 題)
Oahu reaches its peak appeal in October. (__1__) summer crowds gone, the island offers a relaxed atmosphere while trade winds keep the afternoons comfortable. Hikers climbing Diamond Head pass through a narrow tunnel to reach a historic lookout, whereas (__2__) north, tranquil waters invite snorkeling. In Honolulu, King Kalākaua admired modern science (__3__) he equipped ’Iolani Palace with electricity in 1887. Meanwhile, Oahu’s cuisine features poke made from raw fish and kalua pork roasted in an underground oven. In late October, actors at the Haunted Plantation lurk in the shadows for (__4__) seek a real scare. (__5__) exploring volcanic trails or tasting shave ice, travelers can easily embrace island life.

1. 請選出最適合填入 (__1__) 的介系詞：

✅ 正解 (A) With！With + O. (summer crowds) + p.p. (gone) 表附帶狀況「隨著夏日人潮散去」。(B)(C) 為連接詞，後方須接完整子句（含動詞）。

2. 請選出最適合填入 (__2__) 的副詞：

✅ 正解 (B) farther！farther north 表示實際地理空間上「更往北、更北方」。

3. 請選出最適合填入 (__3__) 的結構：

✅ 正解 (B) so much that！修飾動詞 admired 並連接結果子句 that he equipped...。

4. 請選出最適合填入 (__4__) 的代名詞與關係代名詞組合：

✅ 正解 (C) those who！後方動詞 seek 為複數動詞，故先行詞必為複數代名詞 those（若用單數 anyone who，動詞須加 s 為 seeks）。

5. 請選出最適合填入 (__5__) 的連接詞：

✅ 正解 (A) Whether！與後方的 or 搭配構成 Whether A or B, S. + V.（無論是 A 或是 B）。
Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (第 6–9 題)

6. The coastal trail leads hikers to a high ________ where they can enjoy sweeping views of the Pacific Ocean.

✅ 正解 (A) lookout（觀景點、瞭望臺）！可飽覽太平洋壯闊景觀的高處「瞭望臺」。

7. Traditional Hawaiian kalua pork is slow-cooked in an underground ________ until the meat becomes smoky and tender.

✅ 正解 (C) oven（烤爐、烤箱）！an underground oven 指地下烤爐。

8. Many iconic dishes in Hawaii, such as shave ice, were originally brought to the islands by Asian ________.

✅ 正解 (B) immigrants（外來移民）！由亞洲「移民」帶到夏威夷群島。

9. Even after living abroad for decades, many expats still ________ Taiwan home because of its warm hospitality.

✅ 正解 (C) call！call + 地點 + home 表示「將某地稱為家、視某地為家園」。
Part C — 重要文法與中翻英除錯 (第 10–12 題)

10. The lifeguard kept the swimmers ________ by warning them about the giant winter waves on the North Shore.

✅ 正解 (B) safe！keep + O. (the swimmers) + O.C. (safe) 說明受詞狀態必用形容詞 safe。

11. The underground oven was ________ hot that the pork became completely tender within a few hours.

✅ 正解 (A) so！後方單獨接形容詞 hot 與 that 子句，必用 so + Adj. + that。

12. 【中翻英除錯】「凡是造訪伊奧拉尼宮的人都會對它的歷史感到驚奇。」哪一句英文完全正確？

✅ 正解 (C)！Those who 後方的關係子句動詞（visit）與主要子句動詞（are）皆須使用複數動詞！
