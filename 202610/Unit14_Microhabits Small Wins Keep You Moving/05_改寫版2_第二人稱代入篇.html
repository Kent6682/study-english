Picture yourself setting an exciting goal on New Year's Day, only to give up before February arrives. You might blame yourself for lacking motivation or self-discipline, yet your plan was simply too huge to maintain. This is where microhabits can help you succeed. A microhabit is a tiny task that feels effortless to start and nearly impossible to skip. Instead of promising to exercise for an hour, take a brief five-minute walk after lunch. Completing it gives your brain a sense of achievement, which inspires you to keep going. Simply focus on one change at a time and clear away anything that gets in your way.
想像你自己在元旦設定了一個令人興奮的目標，結果在二月到來前卻放棄了。你可能會責怪自己缺乏動力或自律，但其實你的計畫只是太大而難以維持。這就是微習慣能幫助你成功的地方。微習慣是一項微小的任務，讓人覺得毫不費力就能開始，且幾乎不可能略過不做。與其承諾要運動一整小時，不如在午餐後進行短暫的五分鐘散步。完成它會帶給你的大腦一股成就感，這會激勵你持續下去。只要一次專注於一項改變，並清除任何會阻礙你的事物。
Once your microhabit becomes routine, anchor it to a daily habit so that the old habit acts as a trigger. Because the routine kicks things off for you, you never need memory. You can even take this further with habit stacking by performing several actions in a row right after you make your bed. Finally, tracking your progress on a checklist shows how far you've come. If you miss a day, calmly review what went wrong and adjust—because lasting habits lead to life-changing results.
一旦你的微習慣成為常規，將它固定在日常習慣上，讓舊習慣扮演誘因（觸發點）的角色。因為這項常規會替你啟動整個流程，你完全不需要依賴記憶力。你甚至可以在整理好床鋪後，透過連續執行好幾個動作的「習慣堆疊」將此做法更推進一步。最後，在核對清單上追蹤你的進展，能顯示出你已經走了多遠。如果你漏掉了一天，冷靜檢視哪裡出了問題並加以調整——因為持久的習慣終將帶來改變人生的成果。
