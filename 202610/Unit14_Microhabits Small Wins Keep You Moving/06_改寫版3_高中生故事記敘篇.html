How Five Words a Day Saved My High School English

每天五個單字如何拯救我的高中英文（第一人稱記敘文視角・100% 涵蓋必考字彙與文法）

😫 起 · 狂背百字的挫敗

1. 發誓日背百字，兩週後卻徹底放棄

高一開學時我設定每天背 100 個單字的目標，起初很興奮，兩週後卻放棄。原來不是我缺乏自律，而是目標太難維持！

only to give up / difficult to maintain
📱 承 · 改成微習慣並鎖手機

2. 每次只改一件事：午餐後背五個單字

英文老師介紹了微習慣。我改成午餐後短暫散步並背五個單字，把單字卡放桌上、收起阻礙我的手機，每天都充滿成就感。

hard to skip / sense of achievement
🛏️ 轉 · 晨間習慣堆疊連鎖

3. 鋪床→喝水→念五句課文，免靠記憶力

一旦這個微習慣穩定，我更用「習慣堆疊」把鋪床當成觸發點，連續完成喝水與朗讀五句英文，完全不用靠記憶力。

kicks things off / habit stacking
🏆 合 · 清單追蹤與段考逆襲

4. 漏掉一天就檢討調整，小行動創造大進步

我在書桌清單追蹤進度，看見自己走了多遠。偶爾漏掉一天就思考哪裡出錯並調整，學期末英文成績竟大幅躍進！

tracking my progress / what went wrong
📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（高中生故事記敘改寫版・約 185 字）
🧑‍🎓 文體特色：第一人稱高中生準備英文段考的真實心路歷程，可直接作為學測英文作文範本！
Last semester, I set an ambitious goal to memorize 100 English words daily and felt excited at first, only to give up two weeks later. I thought I failed because I lacked motivation or self-discipline, yet my plan was simply too big to maintain. That was where microhabits helped me. My teacher explained that microhabits are tiny actions that take very little effort. Instead of forcing myself to study for hours, I decided to review five words during a brief walk after lunch. Because the task was small, it was hard to skip and gave my brain a sense of achievement, which encouraged me to keep going. I focused on one change at a time and hid my phone—the main thing that got in my way.
上學期，我設定了一個每天背 100 個英文單字的遠大目標，起初感到興奮不已，結果兩週後卻放棄了。我以為自己失敗是因為缺乏動力或自律，但其實我的計畫只是太大而難以維持。那就是微習慣幫上忙的地方。我的老師解釋說，微習慣是只需要極少精力的微小行動。與其強迫自己苦讀好幾小時，我決定在午餐後短暫散步時複習五個單字。因為這項任務很小，很難略過不做，還帶給我的大腦一股成就感，這鼓勵了我持續下去。我一次專注於一項改變，並把手機藏起來——那是阻礙我的主要東西。
Once my microhabit became routine, I linked it to brushing my teeth so that my nightly routine became a trigger. It kicked things off automatically without needing memory. Later, I took this further with habit stacking, practicing three habits in a row right after I made my bed. Meanwhile, tracking my progress on a wall list showed how far I had come. Whenever I missed a day, I reflected on what went wrong to adjust, proving that lasting daily actions truly lead to big changes.
一旦我的微習慣成為常規，我便將它與刷牙連結起來，使晚間常規成為一個誘因（觸發點）。它自動啟動了整個流程，完全不需要靠記憶力。後來，我透過「習慣堆疊」將此做法更推進一步，在我整理好床鋪後立刻連續執行三個習慣。同時，在牆上清單追蹤我的進展，顯示出我已經走了多遠。每當我漏掉一天，我就反思哪裡出了問題以便調整，證明了持久的每日行動真的能帶來巨大的改變。

Part A — 克漏字綜合測驗 (Cloze Test：第 1～5 題)

Last semester, I set a goal to memorize 100 words a day, (__1__) give up two weeks later because the plan was too difficult to maintain. Fortunately, my teacher introduced microhabits to me. (__2__) forcing myself to study for hours, I reviewed just five words during a brief walk after lunch. Completing this small task gave my brain a sense of achievement(__3__) encouraged me to keep going. (__4__) the habit became routine, I took it further with habit stacking. Meanwhile, tracking my progress on a wall list showed (__5__) and built my confidence.
1. (__1__)
✅ 正解 (A)：, only to V 表示「不料卻……、結果卻……」。
2. (__2__)
✅ 正解 (B)：Instead of + V-ing 表示「與其……；而不是……」。
3. (__3__)
✅ 正解 (B)：非限定關係子句補充說明前句整件事，須加逗點並用 , which。
4. (__4__)
✅ 正解 (C)：Once + S + V 表「一旦……」。
5. (__5__)
✅ 正解 (B)：間接問句維持陳述語序 how far + S (I) + V (had come)。

Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (Vocabulary & Collocations：第 6～9 題)

6. I hid my smartphone in a drawer because it was the main distraction that __________.
✅ 正解 (A)：got in my way 指「阻礙了我、擋住我的路」。
7. Whenever I missed a day of vocabulary review, I reflected on what went wrong and tried to __________ my study schedule.
✅ 正解 (C)：adjust 意為「調整、調節」。
8. By linking vocabulary review to brushing my teeth, my nightly routine became an automatic __________.
✅ 正解 (B)：trigger 指「誘因、觸發訊號」。
9. Practicing three small habits __________ right after making my bed helped me form a strong morning chain.
✅ 正解 (C)：in a row 意為「連續地、一個接一個地」。

Part C — 重要文法與中翻英除錯 (Grammar & Translation：第 10～12 題)

10. That was __________ microhabits helped me overcome my bad study habits.
✅ 正解 (A)：That was where + S + V（那就是……幫上忙的地方），以 where 引導名詞子句作主詞補語。
11. Whenever I missed a day, I reflected on __________, proving that lasting daily actions lead to big changes.
✅ 正解 (B)：what went wrong 為複合關代 what 引導的名詞子句作介系詞 on 的受詞。
12. Which of the following sentences is grammatically CORRECT?
✅ 正解 (C)：(A) 動詞 lacked 後不可加 of；(B) lead to 的 to 為介系詞，後應接 N/V-ing；(D) 關係代名詞 that 前不可加逗號。

Part D — 大考閱讀測驗 (Reading Comprehension：第 13～15 題)

13. Why did the narrator fail at the beginning of last semester?
✅ 正解 (B)：第一段指出作者最初設定每天背 100 個單字的目標太大、難以維持（too big to maintain）。
14. How did the narrator remove the main obstacle that got in the way of studying?
✅ 正解 (C)：由第一段結尾 hid my phone—the main thing that got in my way 可知答案為 (C)。
15. What lesson did the narrator learn from this experience?
✅ 正解 (A)：全文最後點出核心教訓：持久的每日微小行動與彈性調整，終將帶來巨大的改變。
