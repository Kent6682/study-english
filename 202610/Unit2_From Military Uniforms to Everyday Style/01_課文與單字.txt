Unit 2｜From Military Uniforms to Everyday Style《從軍服到日常穿搭》
時尚生活｜作者：Ryan Miller
課文朗讀：第一部分 正常 Track 8／慢速 Track 58；第二部分 正常 Track 10／慢速 Track 59

================================================================
一、完整課文（中英對照，依段落）
================================================================

【第一部分】

¶1（L1–8）
You have probably come across clothes that remind you of the military while shopping. This is because many well-known fashion items began as functional uniforms for soldiers, sailors, and pilots. These clothes needed to be strong, comfortable, and practical. Today, people wear them simply because they look good. Let's look at four common examples.
你逛街時大概曾看過讓你聯想到軍隊的衣服。這是因為許多知名的時尚單品，最初都是為士兵、水手和飛行員設計的功能性制服。這些衣服必須堅固、舒適又實用。如今，人們穿它們只是因為好看。讓我們來看看四個常見的例子。

¶2（L9–17）
The bomber jacket, or flight jacket, was first made for airplane pilots when cockpits were open and very cold. Early versions appeared during World War I, and around 1950 the US Air Force introduced the MA-1 jacket, which was made from nylon. This material kept pilots warm and dry. Over time, the flight jacket became a popular everyday item for both men and women.
轟炸機夾克又稱飛行夾克，最初是在駕駛艙還是開放式、非常寒冷的年代，為飛機駕駛員製作的。早期的款式出現在第一次世界大戰期間，而美國空軍在 1950 年左右推出了以尼龍製成的 MA-1 夾克。這種材質讓飛行員保持溫暖乾燥。隨著時間過去，飛行夾克成了男女皆宜的熱門日常單品。

¶3（L18–26）
Similarly, aviator sunglasses were originally made to help pilots. In the 1930s, pilots struggled with the bright sun. The American company Bausch & Lomb developed these special glasses to cover the whole eye area and protect against strong light. They called this design Ray-Ban, which is now perhaps the most famous sunglasses brand in the world.
同樣地，飛行員太陽眼鏡原本也是為了幫助飛行員而製作的。在 1930 年代，飛行員飽受刺眼陽光所苦。美國的博士倫公司研發了這種特殊眼鏡，用來遮住整個眼部並抵擋強光。他們把這款設計命名為雷朋，如今它或許是全世界最有名的太陽眼鏡品牌。

【第二部分】

¶4（L1–12）
It may sound surprising, but the modern T-shirt also has its origins in the military. From 1913, the US Navy started giving sailors short-sleeved white shirts to wear under their heavy uniforms. Working inside ships was very hot, so sailors often took off their uniform jackets and just wore the T-shirts. Later, actors such as Marlon Brando wore them in films, and this made them very fashionable.
聽起來或許令人驚訝，但現代的 T 恤其實也起源於軍隊。從 1913 年起，美國海軍開始發給水手短袖白襯衫，讓他們穿在厚重的制服底下。在船艙內工作非常炎熱，所以水手常常脫掉制服外套，只穿著 T 恤。後來，像馬龍・白蘭度這樣的演員在電影中穿上它，這讓 T 恤變得非常時髦。

¶5（L13–20）
Cargo pants are another well-known example. The British and US armies were among the first to use them. Soldiers needed the extra pockets to carry various items while keeping their hands free. In the 1990s, skateboarders and young people started wearing them because they were loose and comfortable.
工作褲是另一個知名的例子。英國和美國陸軍是最早使用它的軍隊之一。士兵需要額外的口袋來攜帶各種物品，同時又能空出雙手。到了 1990 年代，滑板玩家和年輕人因為工作褲寬鬆又舒適而開始穿它。

¶6（L21–29）
As you can see, military history has had a significant influence on modern fashion. Some of the world's most recognizable everyday styles started out as functional military gear. The next time you wear one of these pieces, remember that even items that were created for practical purposes may become fashion one day.
如你所見，軍事歷史對現代時尚有著重大的影響。世界上一些最容易辨識的日常穿搭風格，起初都是功能性的軍用裝備。下次你穿上其中一件單品時，請記得：即使是為實用目的而創造的物品，有一天也可能成為時尚。

三、Language Notes 關鍵解析
================================================================
【第一部分】
1. come across 的不同意思與用法
   (a) 作及物用：偶然遇到；無意中發現
       課文：You have probably come across clothes that...
       • Ed came across a useful source for articles online.
       ※ remind sb of N 使人想起……；while V-ing 在（做）……的同時
   (b) 作不及物用，接 as + N./Adj.：給人……的印象；讓人覺得……
       • My cat comes across as shy, but she is actually friendly.
2. look 作連綴動詞：看起來……
   look + Adj.（課文：they look good）／look like + N.
   • This material looks smooth and soft to touch.
   • That strange dark cloud looks like a monster.
   ※ 第二部分 sound 也是連綴動詞（It may sound surprising）。
Info Box：US Air Force 美國空軍｜keep + O + Adj. 使……保持某狀態（kept pilots warm and dry／keeping their hands free）｜struggle with sth 與……奮鬥、艱難應付｜Bausch & Lomb 博士倫｜Ray-Ban 雷朋（擋住 ban 光線 ray）

【第二部分】
1. make + O. + O.C.：使……成為／變成……，受詞補語可為名詞或形容詞
   課文：this made them very fashionable.
   • Years of practice made him a skilled musician.
   • The long and pointless meeting made Tina tired.
2. start out 的用法：起初……、剛開始……
   start out as N.（起初的身分、狀態是……，課文用法，與 begin as N. 相似）
   start out V-ing（起初做某事）
   • The popular app started out as a school project.
   • Mark started out selling clothes online before opening his own store.
3. the next time S. + V.：下次……的時候（具連接詞功能，引導時間副詞子句）
   • The next time I visit Paris, I want to eat even more tasty food.
Info Box：took 為 take 的過去式，take off 脫下｜Marlon Brando 馬龍・白蘭度（1924–2004）｜as you can see 如你所見
Word Focus：字尾 -able（有……特質的；能……的）：fashionable 流行的｜recognizable 可辨識的
