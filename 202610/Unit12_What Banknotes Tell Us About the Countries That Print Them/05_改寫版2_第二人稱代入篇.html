📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（第二人稱 You 代入改寫版・約 178 字）
💬 文體特色：以第二人稱 you 直接與讀者對話提問，訓練學測閱讀測驗最愛的「情境導入式科普文」
Have you ever paused to inspect the banknotes in your wallet? Usually, you might only think about the purchasing power money represents, yet if you look closely, your cash will reveal a country’s history, values, and identity. Travel to Costa Rica, and you will see that the colón comes in colorful notes featuring coral reefs and sloths. Meanwhile, if you visit Switzerland, you will notice that Swiss franc notes no longer show portraits of people; instead, they are vertical and display working hands to honor national excellence in science and the arts. You can also explore history through important figures—on the UK’s £5 note, you will spot Winston Churchill with Big Ben visible behind him at three o’clock, the exact hour of his “blood, toil, tears, and sweat” speech.
你是否曾經停下來仔細檢查皮夾裡的紙鈔？通常，你可能只會想到金錢所代表的購買力，但如果你仔細觀察，你手中的現金將會揭露一個國家的歷史、價值觀與身分認同。到哥斯大黎加旅遊，你會看到科隆紙鈔有著印上珊瑚礁與樹懶的繽紛款式。與此同時，如果你造訪瑞士，你會注意到瑞士法郎紙鈔已不再印有人物肖像；取而代之的是，它們採直立式設計並展示工作中的雙手，以表彰國家在科學與藝術上的卓越表現。你也可以透過重要人物探索歷史——在英國五英鎊紙鈔上，你會發現溫斯頓．邱吉爾，他身後清晰可見指向三點鐘的大笨鐘，那正是他發表「鮮血、辛勞、眼淚與汗水」演說的確切時刻。
Aside from observing what your notes show, you can learn even more from why those images appear. When you hold a Euro note, its fictional buildings promote unity across 21 nations, while Australian notes remind you of equal rights by featuring a woman on every bill. You will also feel that many modern notes are made of polymer, a tough plastic first introduced in Australia so counterfeiters cannot easily copy them. Tilt your note toward the light, and you will see security features like holograms and shiny strips—proving that money really does have plenty to tell you!
除了觀察你手中的紙鈔呈現什麼之外，你還能從那些圖像為何出現學到更多。當你拿著歐元紙鈔時，其虛構建築促進了 21 國之間的團結，而澳洲紙鈔則透過在每張鈔票印上女性來提醒你平權的重要。你也會摸出許多現代紙鈔是由聚合物製成的，這是一種由澳洲率先引進的堅韌塑膠，使偽鈔製造者無法輕易複製。把你的紙鈔對著光線傾斜，你就會看見全像圖與閃亮防偽條等安全特徵——證明了金錢確實有許多故事要告訴你！

1. so ＝ so that 引導目的副詞子句「以便、好讓」。
2. first introduced... 為過去分詞片語修飾 plastic。

that

  • 功能：表示目的「好讓、以便」，口語或敘事常省略 that。

Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (Vocabulary & Collocations：第 6～9 題)

6. When you tilt a modern banknote toward the light, you can see a ________ strip and a 3D hologram.
✅ 正解 (B)：a shiny strip（一條閃亮的防偽條）。
7. The Euro currency uses fictional bridges to foster a spirit of ________ among 21 European countries.
✅ 正解 (A)：a spirit of unity（團結一致的精神）。
8. Police officers arrested a group of ________ who were printing fake $100 bills in an underground basement.
✅ 正解 (C)：在地下室印製假百元鈔票的「偽鈔製造者（counterfeiters）」。
9. Vicky trained hard every day and finally came ________ first place in the national swimming competition.
✅ 正解 (C)：Language Notes 重點：come in + first/second/third (place) 表示「獲得第……名」。

10. ________ closely at the banknote in your hand, and you will discover many unique details.
✅ 正解 (C)：後面有對等連接詞 , and you will discover...，故句首須用原形動詞開頭的祈使句 Look closely at...！若沒有 and 才選分詞構句 Looking。
11. It may not speak aloud, but money really ________ have plenty of stories to tell you.
✅ 正解 (C)：不可數名詞 money 為第三人稱單數，強調一般動詞 have 時需用助動詞 does（does have）。
12. 【中翻英除錯】「除了英文之外，葛蕾絲也想學義大利文。」請選出文法完全正確的句子：
✅ 正解 (A)：表示「除了……之外，還有……（包含）」用 Aside from 或 Besides（注意 D 選項 Beside 少 s 意思是「在……旁邊」，C 選項 Except 是排除法）。

13. Why does the author address the reader as “you” throughout this rewritten version?
✅ 正解 (B)：使用第二人稱 you 能拉近與讀者的距離，引導讀者親自拿起皮夾中的紙鈔觀察細節。
