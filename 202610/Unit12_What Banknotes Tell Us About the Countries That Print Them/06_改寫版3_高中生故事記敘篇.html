📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（高中生第一人稱故事篇・約 180 字）
🧑‍🎓 文體特色：以高中生第一人稱 I 與爺爺共賞錢幣冊的溫馨故事改寫，訓練學測英文作文記敘文描寫力
Last Sunday, my grandfather showed me his collection of world banknotes and explained that money—beyond the wealth it represents—tells stories about a country’s history, values, and identity. Turning the first page, I saw that the Costa Rican colón comes in colorful notes with coral reefs and cute sloths. Meanwhile, Grandpa pointed to his Swiss franc notes, which no longer have images of people; instead, they are vertical and feature working hands to honor Switzerland’s excellence in science and the arts. Next, we admired historical figures like Winston Churchill on the UK’s £5 note, with Big Ben visible behind him at three o’clock—marking his famous “blood, toil, tears, and sweat” speech.
上週日，爺爺向我展示他的世界紙鈔收藏冊，並解釋說金錢——除了它所代表的財富之外——還訴說著一個國家的歷史、價值觀與身分認同。翻開第一頁，我看到哥斯大黎加科隆有著印上珊瑚礁與可愛樹懶的繽紛紙鈔。與此同時，爺爺指著他的瑞士法郎紙鈔，上面已不再有人物肖像；取而代之的是，它們採直立式設計並以工作中的雙手為特色，表彰瑞士在科學與藝術上的卓越表現。接著，我們欣賞了歷史重要人物，例如英國五英鎊紙鈔上的溫斯頓．邱吉爾，他身後清晰可見指向三點鐘的大笨鐘——紀念他著名的「鮮血、辛勞、眼淚與汗水」演說。
Aside from looking at what notes show, Grandpa taught me to ask why those designs were chosen. He explained that Euro notes’ fictional buildings promote unity across 21 nations, while Australia champions equal rights by featuring a woman on every note. When I touched an Australian bill, I learned that many modern notes are made of polymer, a durable plastic that stops counterfeiters from copying them, complete with security features like holograms and shiny strips. That afternoon, I realized money really does have a lot to say!
除了觀察紙鈔呈現什麼之外，爺爺還教我去追問為何選擇那些設計。他解釋說，歐元紙鈔的虛構建築促進了 21 國之間的團結，而澳洲則透過在每張紙鈔印上女性來擁護平等權利。當我觸摸一張澳幣時，我得知許多現代紙鈔是由聚合物製成的，這是一種能阻止偽鈔製造者複製的耐用塑膠，還配備了全像圖與閃亮防偽條等安全特徵。那天下午，我體會到金錢確實有好多故事要訴說！

Part A — 克漏字綜合測驗 (Cloze Test：第 1～5 題)

Last weekend, my grandfather showed me his world currency album. On the first page, I saw that the Costa Rican colón (__1__) colorful notes with coral reefs and sloths. (__2__), his Swiss franc notes (__3__) have portraits of famous people; instead, they are vertical and show working hands. (__4__) looking at the pictures, Grandpa told me to think about why they were chosen, like the fictional buildings on Euro notes that promote unity. Finally, when I touched an Australian bill, I learned that it (__5__) polymer plastic with holograms and shiny strips to stop counterfeiters from copying it.
1. (__1__)
✅ 正解 (A)：comes in colorful notes（有色彩繽紛的紙鈔款式）。
2. (__2__)
✅ 正解 (B)：Meanwhile（同時；另一方面）對照另一頁的瑞士法郎。
3. (__3__)
✅ 正解 (C)：no longer + have（不再有……）。
4. (__4__)
✅ 正解 (A)：Aside from + V-ing（除了看圖案之外，爺爺還告訴我……）。
5. (__5__)
✅ 正解 (B)：is made of polymer plastic（由聚合物塑膠製成）。

Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (Vocabulary & Collocations：第 6～9 題)

6. After running five kilometers in the summer heat, Kevin’s T-shirt was completely soaked with ________.
✅ 正解 (C)：在夏日酷熱中跑完五公里後，T 恤完全被「汗水（sweat）」浸濕。
7. Unlike traditional horizontal banknotes, modern Swiss franc notes have a unique ________ design.
✅ 正解 (A)：不同於傳統水平橫向紙鈔，現代瑞士法郎有著獨特的「直立式、垂直的（vertical）」設計。
8. Abraham Lincoln and Martin Luther King Jr. are two of the most important ________ in American history.
✅ 正解 (B)：美國歷史上最重要的兩位「人物（figures）」。
9. The store clerk told me that the new smartphones would come ________ next Friday.
✅ 正解 (C)：Language Notes 重點：come in 第二個意思為「（商品）到貨、進貨」。

Part C — 重要文法與中翻英除錯 (Grammar & Translation：第 10～12 題)

10. Advanced security features like holograms and shiny strips stop counterfeiters from ________ banknotes easily.
✅ 正解 (C)：stop + O. + from + V-ing（阻止某人做某事），介系詞 from 後面必接動名詞 copying。
11. ________ the first page of Grandpa’s album, I saw colorful banknotes from Costa Rica.
✅ 正解 (A)：主句主詞 I 主動執行「翻頁」動作，且兩句之間沒有連接詞 and，故用現在分詞構句 Turning the first page...。
12. 【中翻英除錯】「除了一些小錯誤外，你的作文非常好。」請選出文法與語意完全正確的句子：
✅ 正解 (A)：課本第 51 頁 Language Notes 原句！Aside from 可同時表達「包含（besides）」與「排除（except for）」，此處表排除（除了一些小錯誤外，整體很好），而 In addition to 只能表加法包含，故選 (A)。

Part D — 大考閱讀測驗 (Reading Comprehension：第 13～15 題)

13. How did the narrator learn about world banknotes in this story?
✅ 正解 (B)：敘事者於週日和爺爺一起翻閱爺爺的世界紙鈔收藏冊而學到各國紙鈔知識。
14. What did the narrator notice when touching the Australian banknote?
✅ 正解 (B)：觸摸澳幣時，體會到它是由耐用的聚合物塑膠（polymer plastic）製成，並具備全像圖與閃亮防偽條。
15. What lesson did Grandpa teach the narrator in the second paragraph?
✅ 正解 (C)：爺爺教導敘事者，除了看紙鈔上印了什麼，更要思考「為何選擇那些圖案設計（why those designs were chosen）」。
