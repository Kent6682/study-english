【Day 26 課文原文與中英對照】(Pages 48–49)

[Paragraph 1]
(L1) You might have heard the expression “money talks.” (L2) Usually, this means that money—or the wealth it represents¹—influences people, but money speaks in other ways, too. (L5) Look closely at banknotes, and they will tell you about their countries’ history, values, and identity.
👉 你可能聽過「金錢萬能（有錢能使鬼推磨／錢會說話）」這句話。通常，這意味著金錢——或者它所代表的財富——會影響人們，但金錢也會以其他方式說話。仔細觀察紙鈔，它們就會告訴你關於其發行國家的歷史、價值觀與身分認同。

[Paragraph 2]
(L8) Many countries use their money to show what makes them special. (L9) Some countries, such as Costa Rica, proudly display their nation’s plants and animals. (L12) The Costa Rican colón comes in bright, colorful notes with images of coral reefs, butterflies, monkeys, and sloths.* (L15) Meanwhile,² in Switzerland, the nation’s excellence³ in science and the arts is honored. (L17) Today, Swiss franc notes no longer have images of people. (L18) Instead, they are vertical* and feature pictures of hands working at various activities.
👉 許多國家利用他們的貨幣來展現使自己獨特的事物。有些國家，例如哥斯大黎加，自豪地展示國家的動植物。哥斯大黎加科隆（colón）有著色彩鮮豔明亮的紙鈔，上面印有珊瑚礁、蝴蝶、猴子與樹懶的圖像。與此同時，在瑞士，該國在科學與藝術方面的卓越表現受到表彰。現今，瑞士法郎紙鈔上已不再有人物肖像。取而代之的是，它們採直立式設計，並以雙手從事各種活動的圖案為特色。

[Paragraph 3]
(L21) History is another common topic. (L22) Many notes around the world show important figures who helped shape their countries’ pasts. (L24) The UK’s £5 note shows Winston Churchill, with Big Ben visible⁴ behind him. (L25) The clock face reads three o’clock—the exact time of Churchill’s famous “blood, toil,* tears, and sweat”⁵ speech.
👉 歷史是另一個常見的主題。世界各地的許多紙鈔都印有幫助塑造國家過往的重要人物。英國的五英鎊紙鈔展現了溫斯頓．邱吉爾，他身後清晰可見大笨鐘。鐘面顯示為三點鐘——正是邱吉爾發表著名的「鮮血、辛勞、眼淚與汗水」演說的確切時間。

【Day 27 課文原文與中英對照】(Pages 50–51)

[Paragraph 4 / Day 27 Para 1]
(L1) ① Aside from looking at what notes show, we can learn a lot from why those things are shown. (L3) Euro notes’ fictional* buildings promote¹ a spirit of unity² and fairness among the 21 European countries and 358 million people that use them. (L6) ② In other regions, the ideal of equal rights is favored, as in Australia, one of the few countries in the world to feature a woman on every single note.
👉 除了觀察紙鈔上呈現了什麼之外，我們還可以從「為什麼呈現這些事物」中學到很多。歐元紙鈔上的虛構建築，在使用歐元的 21 個歐洲國家與 3.58 億人口之間，促進了一種團結與公平的精神。在其他地區，平等權利的理想備受推崇，例如澳洲，它是世界上少數在每一張紙鈔上都印有女性人物的國家之一。

[Paragraph 5 / Day 27 Para 2]
(L11) Interestingly, Australia was also the first country to introduce polymer* banknotes. (L13) ③ Many modern notes are made of polymer, a type of plastic³ that’s harder for counterfeiters* to copy. (L15) ④ They also include security⁴ features like holograms,* shiny⁵ strips, and hidden writing for the same reason. (L18) From these developments, we can see the efforts countries take to keep their money safe.
👉 有趣的是，澳洲也是第一個引進聚合物（塑膠）紙鈔的國家。許多現代紙鈔是由聚合物製成的，這是一種讓偽鈔製造者更難複製的塑膠材質。基於同樣的原因，它們也包含了像是全像防偽圖案、閃亮防偽條以及隱藏文字等安全防偽特徵。從這些發展中，我們可以看出各國為了確保貨幣安全所付出的努力。

[Paragraph 6 / Day 27 Para 3]
(L21) ⑤ It may not speak aloud, but money really does have a lot to say. (L22) The next time you hold a banknote, take a closer look. (L23) You may discover that it is more than just a way to pay for things—it is also filled with curious and unique details.
👉 金錢或許不會大聲說話，但它確實有許多故事要訴說。下次當你拿著一張紙鈔時，不妨仔細瞧瞧。你可能會發現，它不僅僅是一種用來付錢買東西的工具——它還充滿了引人好奇且獨樹一幟的細節。

【Vocabulary 核心必考單字表】

--- Day 26 ---
*1. represent [͵rɛprɪˋzɛnt] v. 代表；象徵
    • The colors on the flag represent different values of the country.
      國旗上的顏色代表著該國不同的價值觀。
*2. meanwhile [ˋmin͵waɪl] adv. 同時；在此期間
    • I was cooking dinner; meanwhile, my brother was setting the table.
      我正在煮晚餐；同時，我弟弟正在擺餐具。
*3. excellence [ˋɛksələns] n. 卓越、傑出
    • Isabel’s work showed true excellence.
      伊莎貝爾的作品展現出真正的卓越水準。
*4. visible [ˋvɪzəbəl] adj. 看得見的；清楚可見的
    • The stars were clearly visible in the night sky.
      星星在夜空中清晰可見。
*5. sweat [swɛt] n. 汗水 v. 流汗（動詞三態同形：sweat / sweat / sweat）
    • Diego’s shirt was wet with sweat after he ran in the heat.
      迪亞哥在酷熱中跑步後，襯衫被汗水浸濕了。

--- Day 27 ---
*1. promote [prəˋmot] v. 促進、提倡、推廣
    • The school wants to promote saving water.
      學校想要提倡節約用水。
*2. unity [ˋjunətɪ] n. 團結；團結一致
    • The team showed unity during the difficult game.
      這支隊伍在艱困的比賽中展現了團結一致。
*3. plastic [ˋplæstɪk] n. 塑膠（材料、製品） adj. 塑膠製的
    • We should limit the use of single-use plastic.
      我們應該限制一次性塑膠的使用。
*4. security [sɪˋkjʊrətɪ] n. 安全、保護措施（security feature 指「安全設計；防偽特徵」）
    • The building has strict security checks at the entrance.
      這棟大樓在入口處有嚴格的安全檢查。
*5. shiny [ˋʃaɪnɪ] adj. 閃亮的（shiny strips 直譯為「閃亮條帶」，即「光影防偽條；金屬反光條」）
    • The wooden floor became shiny after it was washed.
      木地板清洗過後變得閃閃發亮。
