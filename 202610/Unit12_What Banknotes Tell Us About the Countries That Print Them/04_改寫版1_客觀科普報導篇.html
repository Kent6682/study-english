📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（客觀科普報導改寫版・約 175 字）
📰 文體特色：以學測常見的「客觀文化與科技科普報導」視角，進行高頻同義詞與句構替換
Beyond the financial wealth that money represents, banknotes serve as cultural mirrors reflecting a nation’s history, values, and identity. The Costa Rican colón comes in vibrant notes displaying native wildlife like coral reefs and sloths. Meanwhile, Switzerland honors its national excellence in science and the arts: modern Swiss franc notes no longer portray famous people, but instead adopt a vertical layout featuring hands engaged in various tasks. Historical figures also remain prominent, as seen on the UK’s £5 note, which depicts Winston Churchill with Big Ben visible behind him at three o’clock—recalling his iconic “blood, toil, tears, and sweat” speech.
除了金錢所代表的財務財富之外，紙鈔還扮演著文化明鏡的角色，反映出一個國家的歷史、價值觀與身分認同。哥斯大黎加科隆有著色彩鮮豔的紙鈔款式，展示珊瑚礁與樹懶等本土野生動植物。與此同時，瑞士表彰其在科學與藝術上的國家卓越表現：現代瑞士法郎紙鈔不再描繪著名人物，而是改採直立式版面，以從事各種任務的雙手為特色。歷史人物同樣佔據重要地位，如英國五英鎊紙鈔描繪了溫斯頓．邱吉爾，他身後清晰可見指向三點鐘的大笨鐘——令人回想起他標誌性的「鮮血、辛勞、眼淚與汗水」演說。
Aside from examining what banknotes display, researchers study why such imagery is chosen. Euro notes depict fictional architecture to promote unity and fairness across 21 European nations, whereas Australia champions equal rights by featuring women on every note. Australia was also the first country to introduce polymer currency. Today, many modern notes are made of this durable plastic and integrate security features like holograms, shiny strips, and hidden text to deter counterfeiters, proving that money does have a rich story to tell.
除了檢視紙鈔展示什麼圖案之外，研究人員也探討為何選擇這類意象。歐元紙鈔描繪虛構建築以促進 21 個歐洲國家的團結與公平，而澳洲則透過在每張紙鈔印上女性來擁護平等權利。澳洲同時也是第一個引進聚合物貨幣的國家。如今，許多現代紙鈔皆由這種耐用的塑膠製成，並整合了全像圖、閃亮防偽條與隱藏文字等安全設計以遏阻偽鈔製造者，證明了金錢確實擁有豐富的故事可說。

Banknotes serve as cultural mirrors that reflect a country’s values and identity. For example, the Costa Rican colón (__1__) vibrant notes featuring coral reefs and sloths. (__4__) examining what is printed on currency, historians also analyze why certain symbols are selected, such as the fictional bridges on Euro notes that promote unity. Finally, many modern banknotes (__5__) polymer plastic and include shiny strips to stop counterfeiters.

✅ 正解 (A)：come in + 款式/顏色 表「有……的款式」，不及物片語動詞無被動。
2. (__2__)
✅ 正解 (B)：Meanwhile（與此同時、另一方面）轉折銜接哥斯大黎加與瑞士的案例。

✅ 正解 (C)：一般動詞 portray 之前用 no longer 表示「不再」。

Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (Vocabulary & Collocations：第 6～9 題)

6. The university is internationally known for its academic ________ in medical research and engineering.
✅ 正解 (B)：academic excellence in...（在……領域的學術卓越表現）。
7. Because the characters and places in this novel are completely ________, they do not exist in the real world.
✅ 正解 (A)：完全「虛構的（fictional）」，不存在於真實世界。
8. After hours of hard work and ________ under the hot sun, the farmers finally finished harvesting the rice.
✅ 正解 (C)：在烈日下數小時的苦幹與「辛勞（toil）」。
9. The international sports festival aims to ________ friendship and mutual understanding among young athletes.
✅ 正解 (B)：旨在「促進、提倡（promote）」年輕運動員間的友誼與相互理解。

13. What does the phrase “cultural mirrors” in the first sentence of the rewritten passage imply?
