【一、關鍵解析 Language Notes 完整拆解】

1. come in 的意思與用法 (Day 26)
   片語動詞 come in 作不及物用（無被動語態），大考常見三大意思：
   ⓐ 表示某商品「有（顏色、樣式、尺寸等）可供選擇」：
      • The Costa Rican colón comes in bright, colorful notes...
        哥斯大黎加科隆有著色彩鮮豔明亮的紙鈔款式。
      • These shoes come in black, brown, and green.
        這款鞋子有黑色、咖啡色和綠色可選擇。
   ⓑ 表示「（商品）到貨」：
      • The new phones will come in next week.
        新款手機將於下週到貨。
   ⓒ 表示「獲得（名次）」，後常接 first / second / third (place)：
      • Vicky came in third (place) in the competition.
        薇琪在比賽中獲得第三名。

2. no longer 不再、已不 (Day 26)
   - 位置口訣：「be 後、普動前」（置於一般動詞之前、be 動詞或助動詞之後）。
   - 同義轉換：
     no longer + V.
     = not . . . any longer（英美皆常用）
     = not . . . anymore（美語常用）
   - 例句對照：
     • Today, Swiss franc notes no longer have images of people.
     • We no longer live in the house by the river.
       = We don’t live in the house by the river any longer.
       = We don’t live in the house by the river anymore.
     • The special discount is no longer on offer.

3. aside from + N./V-ing 除了……之外 (Day 27)
   aside from 兼具「包含（加法）」與「排除（減法）」兩種語意，須依上下文判斷：
   ⓐ 表「包含（除了……之外，還有……）」＝ besides / in addition to：
      • Aside from looking at what notes show, we can learn a lot from why those things are shown.
      • Aside from English, Grace also wants to learn Italian.
      • Besides going to school, my sister attends dance lessons on Tuesdays.
   ⓑ 表「排除（除了……之外，其餘都……）」＝ except for：
      • Aside from some minor mistakes, your essay is very good.
        除了一些小錯誤外，你的作文非常好。

4. be made of N. vs. be made from N. 由……製成 (Day 27)
   - be made of + 原料（物理變化：成品仍看得出原料，原料性質未明顯改變）：
     • Many modern notes are made of polymer, a type of plastic...
     • The chairs in the house are all made of wood.
   - be made from + 原料（化學變化：製作過程中原料已發生較大變化，從成品看不出原料）：
     • Cheese can be made from the milk of cows, sheep, or goats.
       起司可以用牛奶、綿羊奶或山羊奶製成。

【二、課文補充句型與 Info Box 精華】

1. 祈使句 + and + S. + will + V.（那麼就會……）
   • Look closely at banknotes, and they will tell you about their countries’ history, values, and identity.
     （＝ If you look closely at banknotes, they will tell you...）

2. with + O. + OC (adj. / V-ing / p.p.) 附帶狀況結構
   • The UK’s £5 note shows Winston Churchill, with Big Ben visible behind him.
     （O. = Big Ben, OC = visible 形容詞表狀態）

3. the first / few + N. + to V.（序數或最高級概念名詞後接不定詞修飾）
   • ...Australia, one of the few countries in the world to feature a woman on every single note.
   • Australia was also the first country to introduce polymer banknotes.

4. 強調助動詞 do / does / did + 原形動詞（真的、確實）
   • It may not speak aloud, but money really does have a lot to say.

5. The next time + S. + V.（下次當……時，以現在簡單式代替未來式）
   • The next time you hold a banknote, take a closer look.

6. more than just + N. / be filled with + N.
   • You may discover that it is more than just a way to pay for things—it is also filled with curious and unique details.

【三、課本隨堂練習解答與解析】

[Day 26 Reading Comprehension (p. 49)]
1. What is the main idea of the introduction?
   Ans: (A) Banknotes can tell us interesting things about a country.
   解析：第一段指出仔細觀察紙鈔，它們會告訴你該國的歷史、價值觀與身分認同。

2. How are Swiss franc notes described in the passage?
   Ans: (B) They are vertical and show a particular theme.（或對照課文：瑞士法郎採直立式設計，並以雙手從事各種活動為主題，彰顯瑞士引以為傲的科學與藝術卓越成就；注意 A 選項若解讀為「向人們展示瑞士引以為傲的事物」亦符合文意，但課文特別強調 no longer have images of people，而是 vertical 且呈現特定雙手活動主題。）

[Day 27 Integrated Questions (p. 51)]
1. Some of the sentences in day 2 are numbered ① to ⑤. Which of them expresses the author’s opinion rather than a fact?
   Ans: ⑤ (It may not speak aloud, but money really does have a lot to say.)
   解析：①～④ 皆為客觀陳述各國紙鈔設計與防偽特徵的事實（Fact），唯有 ⑤「金錢或許不會大聲說話，但它確實有很多話要說」屬於作者的主觀評論與修辭觀點（Opinion）。

2. According to day 2, which of the following statements is NOT correct?
   Ans: (B) Austria was the first country to feature a woman on every note.
   解析：文中提到的是澳洲（Australia）而非奧地利（Austria），且澳洲是「少數在每張紙鈔上都有女性的國家之一（one of the few countries）」以及「第一個引進塑膠紙鈔（polymer banknotes）的國家」。
