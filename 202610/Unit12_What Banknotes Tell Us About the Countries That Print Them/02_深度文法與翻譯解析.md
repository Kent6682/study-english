## 🔍 一、關鍵解析 (Language Notes) 深度拆解

### 1. `come in` 的三大核心意思與用法（不及物片語動詞，無被動）
- **ⓐ 表示某商品「有（顏色、樣式、尺寸等）可供選擇」**（課文用法）
  - *The Costa Rican colón **comes in** bright, colorful notes...*（哥斯大黎加科隆有色彩鮮豔明亮的紙鈔款式。）
  - *These shoes **come in** black, brown, and green.*（這款鞋子有黑、咖啡、綠三色可選。）
- **ⓑ 表示「（商品）到貨、進貨」**
  - *The new phones will **come in** next week.*（新款手機將於下週到貨。）
- **ⓒ 表示「獲得（名次）」**（後接 `first / second / third (place)`）
  - *Vicky **came in** third (place) in the competition.*（薇琪在比賽中獲得第三名。）

---

### 2. `no longer`（不再、已不）的位置與同義轉換
- **位置口訣**：「**be 後、普動前**」（置於一般動詞之前、`be` 動詞或助動詞之後）。
- **三大同義公式**：
  $$\text{S. + no longer + V.} = \text{S. + aux./be not + V. ... any longer} = \text{S. + aux./be not + V. ... anymore}$$
- **例句對照**：
  - *Today, Swiss franc notes **no longer** have images of people.*
  - *We **no longer** live in the house by the river.*
    $= \text{We **don't** live in the house by the river **any longer** / **anymore**.}$

---

### 3. `aside from + N. / V-ing`（除了……之外）的「加法 vs. 減法」語意
- **ⓐ 表「包含（加法：除了……之外，還有……）」＝ `besides` / `in addition to`**（課文用法）
  - ***Aside from** looking at what notes show, we can learn a lot from why those things are shown.*
  - ***Aside from** English, Grace also wants to learn Italian.*
- **ⓑ 表「排除（減法：除了……之外，其餘皆……）」＝ `except for`**
  - ***Aside from** some minor mistakes, your essay is very good.*

---

### 4. `be made of` vs. `be made from`（由……製成）
| 片語 | 變化性質 | 判斷標準 | 課本例句 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`be made of + N.`** | **物理變化** | 成品**仍看得出**原料，性質未明顯改變 | *Many modern notes **are made of** polymer, a type of plastic...*<br>*The chairs in the house **are all made of** wood.* |
| **`be made from + N.`** | **化學變化** | 製作過程原料已發生大變化，成品**看不出**原料 | *Cheese can **be made from** the milk of cows, sheep, or goats.* |

## 🧩 二、全課重要句構與 Info Box 精華

1. **祈使句 + `and` + S. + `will` + V.**（只要……，就會……）
   - *Look closely at banknotes, **and** they **will tell** you about their countries’ history, values, and identity.*
2. **`with + O. + OC (adj. / V-ing / p.p.)` 附帶狀況結構**
   - *The UK’s £5 note shows Winston Churchill, **with Big Ben visible** behind him.*（`visible` 為形容詞作受詞補語，表狀態）
3. **`feature` 的一字多詞性轉換**
   - 作**動詞**「以……為特色；呈現」：*...they are vertical and **feature** pictures of hands working at various activities.*
   - 作**名詞**「特色、功能、防偽設計」：*They also include security **features** like holograms, shiny strips, and hidden writing...*
4. **強調助動詞 `do / does / did + V.`（真的、確實）**
   - *It may not speak aloud, but money really **does have** a lot to say.*
5. **`more than just + N.`（不僅僅是……）與 `be filled with + N.`（充滿……）**
   - *You may discover that it is **more than just** a way to pay for things—it is also **filled with** curious and unique details.*
