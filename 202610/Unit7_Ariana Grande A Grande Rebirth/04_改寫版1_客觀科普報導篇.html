📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（客觀科普報導改寫版・約 175 字）
📰 文體特色：以客觀娛樂與心理報導視角，剖析流行巨星如何從創傷與倦怠中重塑職涯
With her latest album petal released during a major world tour, award-winning pop star Ariana Grande appears unstoppable. Beginning her career at age 20 with Yours Truly and the hit single “Problem,” her popularity soared. However, just as her career was going from strength to strength, tragedy struck three times in 2017: an attacker set a bomb off at a Manchester stadium, a close friend passed away, and her engagement came to a sudden end. Grande was left deeply shaken, and although she seemed to bounce back, she recently admitted that she considered quitting music for good in 2022.
隨著最新專輯《petal》在大型世界巡演期間發行，獲獎無數的流行巨星亞莉安娜看來勢不可擋。她 20 歲以《Yours Truly》和暢銷單曲〈Problem〉展開職涯，人氣急速飆升。然而，正當她的事業蒸蒸日上時，悲劇在 2017 年接連發生了三次：攻擊者在曼徹斯特體育館引爆炸彈、摯友離世，以及婚約突然告終。亞莉安娜因而深受震驚打擊，儘管表面看似振作，她最近坦承曾在 2022 年考慮永遠退出樂壇。
Ironically, portraying the confident good witch Glinda in the film Wicked may have ended up saving her musical passion. The character's self-assurance began to rub off on Grande, inspiring a bold shift: she decided to leave behind exhausting tours and press events to make the creative process her primary focus on her own terms. This renewal produced the chart-topping eternal sunshine (2024) and petal, a daring new work that delights fans in her vibrant new musical era.
諷刺的是，在電影《魔法壞女巫》中飾演自信的好女巫葛琳達，最終可能拯救了她的音樂熱情。該角色的自信開始感染了亞莉安娜，啟發了一項大膽轉變：她決定拋下累人的巡演與媒體活動，按照自己的步調將創作過程作為首要重心。這場重生孕育了冠軍專輯《eternal sunshine》（2024）以及《petal》，一部在她充滿活力的全新音樂時代中令樂迷欣喜的大膽創新之作。
🧩 三、課文「積木拆句」＋ 逐行中翻英秘訣
🛠️ 學習模式切換：
句 1
With her latest album petal released during a major world tour, award-winning pop star Ariana Grande appears unstoppable.
隨著最新專輯《petal》在大型世界巡演期間發行，獲獎無數的流行巨星亞莉安娜看來勢不可擋。
[M] 附帶狀況 (With+O+OC)With her latest album petal released during a major world tour,隨著最新專輯《petal》在大型世巡期間被發行
[S] 主詞award-winning pop star Ariana Grande獲獎無數的流行巨星亞莉安娜
[V+C] 連綴動詞+補語appears unstoppable看來勢不可擋
💡 附帶狀況句型：With + O. + p.p.
因為專輯是「被發行」，受詞補語使用過去分詞 released；appears unstoppable 同義替換原課文的 seems truly unstoppable。
🎯 同義替換秘訣
原課文 in the middle of a major world tour 換成更精煉的 during a major world tour。
句 2
Beginning her career at age 20 with Yours Truly and the hit single “Problem,” her popularity soared.
她 20 歲以《Yours Truly》和暢銷單曲〈Problem〉展開職涯，人氣急速飆升。
[M] 分詞片語Beginning her career at age 20 with Yours Truly and the hit single “Problem,”20 歲以《Yours Truly》與暢銷單曲展開職涯
[S] 主詞her popularity她的人氣
[V] 動詞soared急速飆升
💡 學測高頻動詞：soar（暴漲、高飛）
原課文 caused her popularity to grow fast 可濃縮為 her popularity soared / skyrocketed。
🎯 同義替換秘訣
「在 20 歲時」除了 at the age of 20，也可簡寫為 at age 20。
句 3
However, just as her career was going from strength to strength, tragedy struck three times in 2017: an attacker set a bomb off at a Manchester stadium, a close friend passed away, and her engagement came to a sudden end.
然而，正當她的事業蒸蒸日上時，悲劇在 2017 年接連發生了三次：攻擊者在曼徹斯特體育館引爆炸彈、摯友離世，以及婚約突然告終。
[Conj] 時間子句However, just as her career was going from strength to strength,然而正當她的事業蒸蒸日上時
[S+V] 主句tragedy struck three times in 2017:悲劇在 2017 年降臨三次：
[Conj] 冒號平行子句an attacker set a bomb off..., a close friend passed away, and her engagement came to a sudden end.引爆炸彈、摯友離世，且婚約突然告終。
💡 冒號（Colon）引出三大平行事件
在科普或新聞報導中，先用總括句 tragedy struck three times: 再用三個平行子句列舉細節，邏輯極度清晰。
🎯 核心片語複習
go from strength to strength（蒸蒸日上）、set a bomb off（引爆炸彈）、come to a sudden end（突然告終）。
句 4
Grande was left deeply shaken, and although she seemed to bounce back, she recently admitted that she considered quitting music for good in 2022.
亞莉安娜因而深受震驚打擊，儘管表面看似振作，她最近坦承曾在 2022 年考慮永遠退出樂壇。
[S+V+C] 被動狀態Grande was left deeply shaken,亞莉安娜深受震驚打擊
[Conj] 讓步子句and although she seemed to bounce back,且儘管她看似振作起來
[S+V+O] 坦承內容she recently admitted that she considered quitting music for good in 2022.她最近坦承曾在 2022 年考慮永遠退出樂壇。
💡 兩大核心句型同句串聯
前半句溫習 be left + p.p.（was left deeply shaken），後半句溫習 consider + V-ing（considered quitting）。
🎯 同義替換秘訣
原課文 appeared to bounce back 與 seemed to bounce back 完全等義。

Part A — 克漏字綜合測驗 (Cloze Test：第 1～5 題)

Ariana Grande's early career went (__1__) after her debut album and the single “Problem.” Yet a series of tragedies in 2017—including a bomb attack at a UK stadium and a broken engagement—left the young singer deeply (__2__). By 2022, she seriously considered (__3__) the music industry for good. Ironically, portraying the witch Glinda (__4__) saving her passion. As Glinda's confidence rubbed off (__5__) her, Grande chose to leave exhausting tours behind and prioritize the creative process on her own terms.
1. (__1__)
✅ 正解 (A)：go from strength to strength 表事業「蒸蒸日上、越來越成功」。(C) 越來越糟 (D) 僅夠餬口。
2. (__2__)
✅ 正解 (C)：leave + O. + p.p.，受詞 the young singer 感到震驚，故用過去分詞 shaken。
3. (__3__)
✅ 正解 (B)：consider + V-ing 為必考動名詞搭配。
4. (__4__)
✅ 正解 (B)：對過去已發生事件的肯定推測用 may + have + p.p.（may have ended up + saving）。
5. (__5__)
✅ 正解 (C)：rub off on sb（感染／影響某人）。

Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (第 6～9 題)

6. Winning the national science ________ gave the high school student great confidence to pursue a career in engineering.
✅ 正解 (A)：贏得全國科學「獎項（award）」帶給這位高中生很大的自信。
7. Advertising agencies are always looking for ________ young designers who can come up with fresh and original ideas.
✅ 正解 (B)：能提出新穎原創點子的「有創意的（creative）」年輕設計師。
8. After losing the first two sets, the tennis player managed to ________ back and win the championship match.
✅ 正解 (C)：bounce back 意為「振作起來、反敗為勝、迅速恢復」。
9. Someone accidentally ________ the fire alarm while cooking in the dormitory kitchen last night.
✅ 正解 (A)：set off 除了引爆炸彈，也常指「觸發（火災警報器、警鈴）」。

Part C — 重要文法與中翻英除錯 (第 10～12 題)

10. The ground is all wet this morning; it ________ heavily while we were sleeping last night.
✅ 正解 (B)：推測昨晚睡覺時（過去時間）「可能下過大雨」，使用 may + have + p.p.。
11. 【中翻英除錯】「她開朗的態度很快就感染了辦公室裡的每個人。」哪一個句子完全正確？
✅ 正解 (C)：A rub off on B 必須以「特質／態度（Her cheerful attitude）」作主詞，並搭配介系詞 on。
12. 【中翻英除錯】「在考慮辭掉工作之後，他決定創業。」哪一個句子完全正確？
✅ 正解 (A)：After + V-ing (considering) + consider + V-ing (quitting)，且 decide + to V。
