📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（第二人稱 You 代入改寫版・約 178 字）
💬 文體特色：以「Have you ever wondered... / Imagine...」引導讀者換位思考，為學測閱讀測驗高頻開場手法
When you listen to Ariana Grande's new album petal during her world tour, you might think the award-winning star is truly unstoppable. Yet have you ever wondered what happens when tragedy strikes just as your career is going from strength to strength? After her debut Yours Truly and the hit “Problem” caused her popularity to explode, Grande faced three devastating blows in 2017: someone set a bomb off in a Manchester stadium, her close friend died, and her engagement came to a sudden end. Put yourself in her shoes—you would be left deeply shaken too.
當你在亞莉安娜的世界巡演期間聆聽她的新專輯《petal》時，你可能會認為這位獲獎無數的巨星真的勢不可擋。但你可曾想過，當你的事業正蒸蒸日上時卻遭逢悲劇，會發生什麼事？在首張專輯《Yours Truly》與熱門單曲〈Problem〉使她的人氣爆發後，亞莉安娜在 2017 年面對了三次毀滅性的打擊：有人在曼徹斯特體育館引爆炸彈、她的摯友過世，且她的婚約突然告終。設身處地站在她的立場想想——你也會受到極度震驚與打擊。
Even if you appear to bounce back, hidden pressure can make you consider quitting your passion for good, just as Grande admitted feeling while filming Wicked in 2022. Ironically, playing the confident good witch Glinda may have ended up rescuing her career. Once a positive mindset begins to rub off on you, you can leave behind exhausting expectations and make the creative process your true focus on your own terms—a bold choice that brought us eternal sunshine and her daring new musical era to delight us all.
即使你表面看似振作起來，隱藏的壓力仍可能讓你考慮永遠放棄自己的熱情，正如同亞莉安娜坦承在 2022 年拍攝《魔法壞女巫》時所感受到的一樣。諷刺的是，飾演自信的好女巫葛琳達最終可能拯救了她的事業。一旦正向的心態開始感染你，你就能拋下令人筋疲力竭的期待，按照自己的步調將創作過程作為真正的重心——這個大膽的選擇為我們帶來了《eternal sunshine》以及她令所有人欣喜的大膽全新音樂時代。

🛠️ 學習模式切換：
句 1–2

當你在亞莉安娜的世界巡演期間聆聽她的新專輯《petal》時，你可能會認為這位獲獎無數的巨星真的勢不可擋。但你可曾想過，當你的事業正蒸蒸日上時卻遭逢悲劇，會發生什麼事？
[Conj] 時間副詞子句When you listen to Ariana Grande's new album petal...,當你聆聽她的新專輯《petal》時
[S+V+O] 主句you might think (that) the award-winning star is truly unstoppable.你可能會覺得這位獲獎巨星勢不可擋。
[V+O] 疑問破題Yet have you ever wondered what happens when tragedy strikes...但你可曾想過當悲劇降臨時會發生什麼事？
💡 間接問句作受詞：wonder + what happens...
what 本身兼具疑問詞與主詞角色，故後面直接接單數動詞 happens。

[Conj] 時間子句After her debut ... caused her popularity to explode,在首專與熱門單曲使她人氣爆發後
[S+V+O] 三重打擊Grande faced three devastating blows in 2017...她在 2017 年面對三次毀滅性打擊
[V] 祈使句+假設結果Put yourself in her shoes—you would be left deeply shaken too.設身處地想想——你也會深受震驚打擊。
💡 祈使句代條件句 + would be left + p.p.
Put yourself in her shoes（設身處地站在她的立場）隱含「如果你處於她的處境」，故後面用助動詞 would be left deeply shaken。

「設身處地為某人著想」英文諺語為 put yourself in someone's shoes。

[S+V+O+OC] 使役+動名詞hidden pressure can make you consider quitting your passion for good,隱藏的壓力仍可能讓你考慮永遠放棄熱情
[Conj+S+V] 條件與行動Once a positive mindset begins to rub off on you, you can leave behind exhausting expectations...一旦正向心態開始感染你，你就能拋下累人的期待……
💡 make + O. + 原形 V (consider) + V-ing (quitting)
雙層動詞考點：使役動詞 make 決定了 consider 用原形；而 consider 決定了後面的 quitting 必須用動名詞！

Part A — 克漏字綜合測驗 (Cloze Test：第 1～5 題)

Imagine achieving massive popularity at age twenty. Just as your career is (__1__) from strength to strength, three terrible tragedies strike at once. You would undoubtedly be (__2__) deeply shaken. Even if you appear to bounce back, the emotional weight might make you consider (__3__) your dream for good. Yet for Ariana Grande, playing the good witch Glinda (__4__) rescuing her career. Once Glinda's confidence rubbed off (__5__) her, she decided to leave exhausting tours behind and create music on her own terms.
1. (__1__)
✅ 正解 (A)：go from strength to strength（蒸蒸日上）。
2. (__2__)
✅ 正解 (C)：被動語態 would be left deeply shaken（使你處於極度震驚的狀態）。
3. (__3__)
✅ 正解 (C)：consider + V-ing（quitting）。
4. (__4__)
✅ 正解 (A)：對過去事件的推測 may have ended up (+ rescuing)。
5. (__5__)
✅ 正解 (B)：rub off on sb（感染／影響某人）。

Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (第 6～9 題)

6. After working at the stressful company for ten years, Kevin decided to ________ his job for good and open a bakery.
✅ 正解 (A)：quit his job for good（永遠辭去他的工作）。
7. The young couple announced their ________ at the family dinner and showed everyone the diamond ring.
✅ 正解 (C)：在家庭聚餐宣布他們的「訂婚／婚約（engagement）」並展示鑽戒。
8. Making health and sleep your main ________ during exam week will actually help you perform better.
✅ 正解 (D)：把健康與睡眠當作你的主要「重心／焦點（focus）」。
9. Moving to a foreign country where you don't speak the language is a very ________ decision.
✅ 正解 (A)：搬到語言不通的外國是個非常「大膽的、勇敢的（bold）」決定。

Part C — 重要文法與中翻英除錯 (第 10～12 題)

10. Why did you leave the refrigerator door ________ all night? All the milk has gone bad!
✅ 正解 (B)：leave + O. + Adj.，描述門窗「開著的狀態」必須用形容詞 open（不可用過去分詞 opened）；反之「關著的狀態」則用 closed。
11. 【中翻英除錯】「你可曾想過，即使你表面看似振作，壓力仍可能讓你考慮永遠放棄？」請選出文法正確的句子：
✅ 正解 (A)：appear to V（不能用被動）+ make + O. + 原形 V (consider) + consider + V-ing (quitting)。(D) 錯在 Even though 與 but 不可連用。
12. 【中翻英除錯】「我們匆忙趕火車時，不小心把雨傘忘在咖啡廳了。」請選出文法正確的句子：
✅ 正解 (B)：leave + N. + behind（把某物遺留在某處）；注意 (A) 若受詞為代名詞 it，必須放在中間 left it behind，不可寫 left behind it。

Part D — 大考閱讀測驗 (第 13～15 題)

13. What does the idiom “Put yourself in her shoes” in Paragraph 1 invite the reader to do?
✅ 正解 (B)：put yourself in someone's shoes 指設身處地站在對方的立場體會其感受。
14. According to the passage, why is it important to let a positive mindset “rub off on you”?
✅ 正解 (A)：由第二段最後一句可知，一旦正向心態感染了你，你就能拋下累人的期待，按自己的步調專注於創作過程。
15. What is the main purpose of rewriting Ariana Grande's story using the second-person pronoun “you”?
✅ 正解 (B)：使用第二人稱 you 能引發讀者同理心（empathy），並將天后從創傷中重生的經驗轉化為讀者自身面對壓力時的生命啟發。
