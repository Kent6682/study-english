📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（高中生第一人稱記敘文改寫版・約 180 字）
🧑‍🎓 文體特色：學測英文作文「從挫折中重新振作」第一人稱滿分記敘文範本
Last month, after my voice cracked during a national choir contest in the city stadium, I missed the top award and was left deeply shaken. I must admit that I seriously considered quitting singing for good. Then my sister showed me an article about Ariana Grande, whose popularity exploded at age 20 with Yours Truly and “Problem.” Just as her career was going from strength to strength, tragedy struck three times in 2017 when someone set a bomb off at her Manchester concert, her close friend died, and her engagement came to a sudden end.
上個月，在市立體育館舉辦的全國合唱比賽中，我的聲音破音並錯失最高獎項，讓我受到極度震驚與打擊。我必須承認，我曾認真考慮永遠退出歌唱圈。後來姊姊給我看了一篇關於亞莉安娜的文章，她的人氣在 20 歲時憑藉《Yours Truly》和〈Problem〉大爆發。正當她的事業蒸蒸日上之際，悲劇在 2017 年接連發生三次：有人在她的曼徹斯特演唱會引爆炸彈、她的摯友過世，且她的婚約突然告終。
Though she seemed to bounce back, Grande nearly gave up on music in 2022. Ironically, playing the confident good witch Glinda in Wicked may have ended up saving her career. Reading how Glinda's courage began to rub off on Grande inspired me to make a bold change too. I decided to leave behind my fear of losing and make the creative joy of music my main focus on my own terms. Listening to eternal sunshine and her daring new album petal, I feel unstoppable and ready to delight our school in my new musical era!
雖然她看似振作起來，亞莉安娜在 2022 年卻差點放棄音樂。諷刺的是，在《魔法壞女巫》中飾演自信的好女巫葛琳達最終可能拯救了她的事業。讀到葛琳達的勇氣如何開始感染亞莉安娜，也啟發我做出大膽的改變。我決定拋下對落敗的恐懼，按照自己的步調，把音樂的創作樂趣當作主要重心。聽著《eternal sunshine》與她大膽創新的新專輯《petal》，我覺得自己勢不可擋，準備好在我的全新音樂時代帶給全校歡愉！

Part A — 克漏字綜合測驗 (Cloze Test：第 1～5 題)

After losing the choir competition in the city stadium, I (__1__) deeply shaken and seriously considered (__2__) singing for good. However, reading about Ariana Grande changed my mind. Even after three tragedies struck while her career was going (__3__), she found healing through her movie role Glinda, which (__4__) saving her love for music. As Grande's story rubbed off (__5__) me, I decided to leave my fear behind and sing on my own terms.
1. (__1__)
✅ 正解 (B)：主詞 I 處於被震撼的狀態，使用被動語態 was left deeply shaken。
2. (__2__)
✅ 正解 (C)：consider + V-ing（considered quitting）。
3. (__3__)
✅ 正解 (A)：go from strength to strength（蒸蒸日上）。
4. (__4__)
✅ 正解 (B)：may have ended up (+ saving) 對過去事件的肯定推測。
5. (__5__)
✅ 正解 (C)：rub off on sb（感染／影響某人）。

Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (第 6～9 題)

6. The magic show was so amazing that it ________ all the children and parents in the audience.
✅ 正解 (A)：魔術表演太精彩了，使台下所有孩童與家長都「感到高興、喜悅（delighted）」。
7. With the rise of artificial intelligence, humanity is stepping into a brand-new technological ________.
✅ 正解 (C)：人類正邁入一個全新的科技「時代／紀元（era）」。
8. ________, the rain started pouring down just five minutes after we finished washing our car outside.
✅ 正解 (B)：「諷刺的是、出人意料地（Ironically）」，我們才剛洗完車五分鐘就下起傾盆大雨。
9. Even though my brother failed his driving test twice, his coach refused to ________ on him.
✅ 正解 (C)：give up on sb 表示「對某人失去信心而放棄」。

Part C — 重要文法與中翻英除錯 (第 10～12 題)

10. Ariana Grande, ________ first album Yours Truly was released in 2013, showed clear musical talent from a young age.
✅ 正解 (D)：空格後為名詞 first album，需用所有格關係代名詞 whose 表示「她的首張專輯」。
11. 【中翻英除錯】「火災使濃煙從建築物中竄出，讓附近居民感到十分驚慌。」請選出文法完全正確的句子：
✅ 正解 (A)：leave + O. (thick smoke) + V-ing (rising) 表濃煙持續主動竄出；leaving nearby residents terrified 表居民「感到驚慌（p.p.）」。
12. 【中翻英除錯】「從今以後，我決定拋下對失敗的恐懼，把創作過程當作我的主要重心。」請選出文法完全正確的句子：
✅ 正解 (B)：From now on（從今以後）+ decide to V (leave behind ... and make ...)。

Part D — 大考閱讀測驗 (第 13～15 題)

13. Why did the high school narrator consider quitting singing for good at the beginning of the story?
✅ 正解 (A)：由第一段第一句可知，主角在市立體育館的全國合唱比賽破音並錯失最高獎項，因而深受打擊想放棄唱歌。
14. How did Ariana Grande's experience with the character Glinda help the narrator?
✅ 正解 (B)：主角從 Glinda 感染亞莉安娜的故事獲得啟發，決定拋下對落敗的恐懼，按自己的步調享受音樂創作的樂趣。
15. Which proverb best summarizes the lesson learned by both Ariana Grande and the high school student?
✅ 正解 (C)：兩人的故事都印證了「從挫折與創傷的灰燼中，能浴火重生出更堅強、更忠於自我的新生命（A Grand Rebirth）」。
