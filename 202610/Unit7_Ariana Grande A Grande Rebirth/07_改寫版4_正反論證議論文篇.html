📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（正反論證議論文改寫版・約 182 字）
⚖️ 文體特色：學測論說文「傳統迷思（Some argue...）vs. 實證反駁（However, the case of... proves...）」標準架構
In the modern music industry, many people assume that nonstop touring and endless press events are required to keep a singer's popularity growing. Admittedly, award-winning star Ariana Grande's early career went from strength to strength after her debut Yours Truly and the hit “Problem.” However, forcing artists to maintain this pace after severe trauma can be destructive. In 2017, tragedy struck three times when someone set a bomb off in a Manchester stadium, her close friend died, and her engagement came to a sudden end. Grande was left deeply shaken, and although she appeared to bounce back, she recently admitted that relentless pressure made her consider quitting music for good in 2022.
在現代音樂產業中，許多人認為不間斷的巡演與無止盡的媒體宣傳活動，是維持歌手人氣成長的必要條件。誠然，獲獎巨星亞莉安娜的早期職涯在首專《Yours Truly》與暢銷曲〈Problem〉後確實蒸蒸日上。然而，在遭受嚴重創傷後強迫藝術家維持這種步調可能極具破壞性。2017 年，悲劇接連發生三次：有人在曼徹斯特體育館引爆炸彈、她的摯友過世，且她的婚約突然告終。亞莉安娜因而深受震驚打擊，儘管表面看似振作，她最近坦承無情的壓力讓她在 2022 年考慮永遠退出樂壇。
Ironically, stepping away into acting may have ended up saving her artistry. Playing the confident good witch Glinda in Wicked allowed the character's self-belief to rub off on Grande, leading to a bold resolution: she chose to leave behind exhausting schedules and make the creative process her core focus on her own terms. The resulting success of eternal sunshine and her daring new album petal proves that when artists prioritize inner renewal in a new era, they become truly unstoppable and continue to delight the world.
諷刺的是，暫時抽身投入演戲最終可能拯救了她的藝術生命。在《魔法壞女巫》中飾演自信的好女巫葛琳達，讓該角色的自信感染了亞莉安娜，促成了一項大膽決定：她選擇拋下令人筋疲力竭的行程，按照自己的步調將創作過程作為核心重心。隨後《eternal sunshine》與大膽新專《petal》的成功證明了：當藝術家在新時代優先重視內在重生時，他們將變得真正勢不可擋，並持續帶給世界歡愉。

💡 議論文讓步轉折公式：Admittedly, ... However, ...
先用 Admittedly（誠然、不可否認地）承認對方觀點的部分合理性，再用 However 提出強力反駁，是學測論說文拿高分的關鍵結構！

Part A — 克漏字綜合測驗 (Cloze Test：第 1～5 題)

Admittedly, Ariana Grande's early career went (__1__) after her debut album. Yet after three tragedies in 2017 left her deeply (__2__), relentless touring pressure nearly broke her spirit, making her consider (__3__) music for good in 2022. Ironically, stepping into the role of the good witch Glinda (__4__) saving her artistry. As Glinda's confidence rubbed off (__5__) her, Grande chose to leave exhausting schedules behind and focus on the creative process on her own terms.
1. (__1__)
✅ 正解 (B)：go from strength to strength（蒸蒸日上）。
2. (__2__)
✅ 正解 (A)：leave + O. + p.p.（left her deeply shaken）。
3. (__3__)
✅ 正解 (C)：consider + V-ing（quitting）。
4. (__4__)
✅ 正解 (B)：may have ended up (+ saving) 對過去事件的肯定推測。
5. (__5__)
✅ 正解 (D)：rub off on sb（感染／影響某人）。

Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (第 6～9 題)

6. With ten consecutive wins this season, the championship basketball team seems completely ________.
✅ 正解 (A)：本季十連勝，這支冠軍籃球隊看來完全「勢不可擋（unstoppable）」。
7. The sudden ________ of the earthquake destroyed hundreds of buildings and left thousands of people homeless.
✅ 正解 (C)：地震的突發「悲劇／災難（tragedy）」摧毀了數百棟建築。
8. When the singer's new album ________ at midnight, millions of fans immediately streamed it online.
✅ 正解 (B)：drop 在流行音樂中常指「（專輯或單曲）推出、發行、上架」。
9. Critics ________ her latest painting as a daring masterpiece full of life and emotion.
✅ 正解 (A)：describe A as B（將 A 形容／描述為 B）。

Part C — 重要文法與中翻英除錯 (第 10～12 題)

10. Instead of taking a taxi, we walked in the rain and ended up ________ completely soaked.
✅ 正解 (C)：end up + V-ing（最終結果變成全身溼透 getting completely soaked）。
11. 【中翻英除錯】「這家公司自從五年前推出那款創新產品以來，業績就一直蒸蒸日上。」請選出文法完全正確的句子：
✅ 正解 (A)：主要子句（現在完成式 has gone） + since + 過去式子句（released ... five years ago），且固定片語為 go from strength to strength。
12. 【中翻英除錯】「誠然，巡迴演出能帶來可觀收入；然而，過度疲勞可能會讓歌手考慮永遠退出樂壇。」請選出文法完全正確的句子：
✅ 正解 (C)：句首評註副詞 Admittedly + 分號轉折 ; however, + make + O. + 原形 V (consider) + consider + V-ing (quitting)。

Part D — 大考閱讀測驗 (第 13～15 題)

13. What is the main argument presented in this essay?
✅ 正解 (B)：本文論證核心在於：相較於不間斷的高壓商業巡演，給予藝術家內在療癒與創作自主權（on her own terms）才能帶來長遠且勢不可擋的藝術成就。
14. Why does the author use the word “Admittedly” in the second sentence of Paragraph 1?
✅ 正解 (A)：在議論文中，Admittedly 用於「讓步承認（concession）」對立論點在早期確實成立，隨後再用 However 進行反駁。
15. How do the albums eternal sunshine and petal function in the final paragraph's argument?
✅ 正解 (B)：這兩張專輯的成功作為最終具體實證（concrete evidence），證明專注創作過程與自主步調能兼顧排行榜佳績與充沛的藝術生命力。
