【Day 16 課文原文（Page 34）】

【中英對照課文】

[Day 15 - Paragraph 1]
After releasing her new album petal earlier this year in the middle of a major world tour, Ariana Grande seems truly unstoppable.
今年稍早在大型世界巡迴演唱會期間發行新專輯《petal》後，亞莉安娜・格蘭德（Ariana Grande）看來真的勢不可擋。
However, not so long ago, the award-winning singer actually considered quitting music for good.
然而，就在不久前，這位獲獎無數的歌手其實曾考慮永遠退出樂壇。
What made her change her mind? A self-confident witch who is named Glinda!
是什麼讓她改變心意的呢？是一位名叫葛琳達（Glinda）、充滿自信的女巫！

[Day 15 - Paragraph 2]
Long before she took to screens across the world as Glinda, Grande showed clear talent from a young age.
早在她以葛琳達一角登上全球大銀幕之前，亞莉安娜從小就展現出明顯的才華。
Grande released her first album, Yours Truly (2013), at the age of just 20.
亞莉安娜在年僅 20 歲時發行了首張專輯《真情告白》（Yours Truly, 2013）。
The album was a commercial hit.
這張專輯在商業上大獲成功。
But the single “Problem” from her second album really caused her popularity to grow fast.
但她第二張專輯中的單曲〈Problem〉才真正讓她的人氣迅速飆升。

[Day 15 - Paragraph 3]
However, as her career was going from strength to strength, tragedy struck not once but three times.
然而，正當她的事業蒸蒸日上之際，悲劇卻接連發生了三次，而非只有一次。
In 2017, while Grande was playing a concert in Manchester, UK, someone set a bomb off in the stadium.
2017 年，當亞莉安娜在英國曼徹斯特舉辦演唱會時，有人在體育館內引爆了一顆炸彈。
Twenty-two people lost their lives, and hundreds more were hurt.
有 22 人喪生，另有數百人受傷。
Soon after, her close friend passed away, and her engagement came to a sudden end.
不久之後，她的摯友過世，而她的婚約也突然告終。
Grande was left deeply shaken by it all.
亞莉安娜因這一切而受到極度震驚與打擊。

[Day 16 - Paragraph 4]
Though Grande appeared to bounce back from these events by releasing several albums in the following years, the pressure was clearly building.
雖然亞莉安娜在接下來幾年透過發行數張專輯，看似從這些事件中振作起來，但壓力顯然正不斷累積。
She recently admitted that in 2022, while filming the musical movie Wicked, she seriously thought about giving up on music.
她最近坦承，在 2022 年拍攝音樂劇電影《魔法壞女巫》（Wicked）時，她曾認真考慮過放棄音樂。

[Day 16 - Paragraph 5]
Ironically, her movie role may have ended up saving her music career.
諷刺的是，她的電影角色最終可能拯救了她的音樂事業。
The character of the good witch Glinda, whom Grande plays in the movie, was so confident that it began to rub off on Grande herself.
亞莉安娜在片中飾演的好女巫葛琳達一角是如此充滿自信，以致於這份自信開始感染了亞莉安娜本人。
She decided to continue her music career, but on her own terms.
她決定繼續她的音樂事業，但要按照她自己的方式進行。
She would leave behind the days of long tours and tiring press events.
她將拋下漫長巡演和累人媒體宣傳活動的日子。
From now on, the creative process would be her focus.
從今以後，創作過程將成為她的重心。

[Day 16 - Paragraph 6]
The result of this bold decision was the album eternal sunshine, which dropped in March 2024 and went straight to number one.
這項大膽決定的成果就是《永恆陽光》（eternal sunshine）這張專輯，於 2024 年 3 月推出並直接空降冠軍。
More than two years later, Grande has returned with petal, a daring new work which she has described as being “full of life.”
兩年多後，亞莉安娜帶著《petal》回歸，這是一部被她形容為「充滿生命力」的大膽創新之作。
As she steps into her new musical era, Grande seems excited once again to delight and surprise us all.
隨著她邁入全新的音樂時代，亞莉安娜看來再次興奮不已，準備好讓我們所有人感到驚喜與歡愉。

【Vocabulary 單字表（Day 15 & Day 16）】

--- Day 15 核心單字 ---
*1. award [əˈwɔrd] n. 獎項（文中複合形容詞 award-winning 指「獲獎的」）
    例句：Julie received an award for her science project.
    （茱莉因為她的科學專題而獲得一個獎項。）

*2. quit [kwɪt] v. 放棄、退出；辭職（三態：quit - quit - quit，進行式 quitting）
    例句：The team quit the game after the first half.
    （該隊伍在上半場結束後退出了比賽。）

*3. bomb [bɑm] n. 炸彈（注意字尾 b 不發音）
    例句：The police and the army safely removed the bomb.
    （警方與軍方安全地移除了那顆炸彈。）

*4. stadium [ˈstediəm] n. （露天）體育館；體育場
    例句：We watched the football match in the big stadium.
    （我們在大體育場裡觀看了那場足球賽。）

*5. engagement [ɪnˈɡedʒmənt] n. 婚約；訂婚
    例句：The couple celebrated their engagement with family.
    （這對情侶與家人一起慶祝他們訂婚。）

--- Day 15 字彙小筆記 ---
* unstoppable [ʌnˈstɑpəbəl] adj. 勢不可擋的；無法阻止的（由表否定的字首 un- 加上 stop 及 -able「可……的」組合而成）
* witch [wɪtʃ] n. 女巫
* popularity [ˌpɑpjəˈlærəti] n. 人氣、受歡迎程度；流行
* tragedy [ˈtrædʒədi] n. 悲劇；不幸事件

--- Day 16 核心單字 ---
*1. admit [ədˈmɪt] v. 承認；坦承（三態：admit - admitted - admitted）
    例句：I must admit that I was wrong about the answer.
    （我必須承認我把答案弄錯了。）

*2. creative [kriˈetɪv] adj. 創作的；有創意的；與創意相關的
    例句：Alexander is very creative and loves designing clothes.
    （亞歷山大非常有創意，而且熱愛設計衣服。）

*3. focus [ˈfokəs] n. 重點；焦點
    例句：Fixing the rest of my essay is my main focus this week.
    （修改我報告的其餘部分是我這週的主要重點。）

*4. bold [bold] adj. 大膽的；勇敢的（強調有魄力、不畏風險）
    例句：It was a bold move to open our own coffee shop.
    （開設我們自己的咖啡店是個大膽的舉動。）

*5. daring [ˈdɛrɪŋ] adj. 大膽創新的；勇於冒險的（強調具冒險精神、突破常規）
    例句：After a daring climb, Sam reached the top of the mountain.
    （在一次大膽的攀登之後，山姆抵達了山頂。）

--- Day 16 字彙小筆記 ---
* bounce [baʊns] v. 反彈、迅速恢復（常與 back 搭配使用，bounce back 指「恢復；振作起來」）；（使）彈起
* ironically [aɪˈrɑnɪkəli] adv. 諷刺的是、具有諷刺意味地、出人意料地
* era [ˈɛrə] n. 時代、年代；紀元
* delight [dɪˈlaɪt] v. 使高興、使喜悅
