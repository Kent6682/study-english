## 一、課文原文 (Reading Text)

Many people now **turn to** AI chatbots\* for **advice**¹ on relationships, school, or personal problems. However, a new study from Stanford University warns *(5)* that these tools may not be giving users the honest answers they need.

The study tested 11 popular AI systems, including ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, and found that these models *(10)* agreed with users far more often than humans would. People were also more likely to prefer AI systems that supported their point of view. This may make them more **confident**² that they are right and *(15)* less willing to **apologize**³ when they make mistakes. Lead researcher\* Myra Cheng worries that over time, this could cause people—especially teenagers and young people—to lose important social skills.

*(20)* After all, in real life, the ability to handle uncomfortable **situations**⁴ comes from real experience. Personal growth sometimes requires hearing difficult truths, and AI systems **seem** *(25)* designed not to deliver them. Experts suggest that while AI can be a useful tool, it should not **replace**⁵ the honest feedback\* that friends, family, or counselors\* can provide.

## 二、關鍵解析 (Language Notes)

### 1. `turn to` 的不同意思

- **(a) 在文中表示「求助於⋯⋯；向⋯⋯尋求幫助或支持」。**
  - You can always **turn to** me when you need support.
    當你需要支持時隨時都可以向我求助。
- **(b) 表示「變成、成為」，與 `turn into` 意思相近，表示事物狀態的改變。不過 `turn to` 後面常接顏色、某種狀態或特定結果。**
  - The rain **turned to** snow when the weather got colder.
    天氣變得更冷時，雨就變成了雪。

### 2. `seem` 的用法

`seem` 在文中作連綴動詞，表示「似乎、好像、看起來⋯⋯」，常見句型如下：

$$
\text{seem} + \begin{cases} \text{to V.} \\ \text{(to be) Adj.} \end{cases}
$$

- Jess **seems to know** the reason for the delay.
  傑斯似乎知道延誤的原因。
- The train station **seems (to be) very crowded** every weekend.
  火車站每逢週末似乎都非常擁擠。

> [!NOTE]
> 文中 `seem designed not to . . .` 中的 `designed` 為過去分詞作形容詞用，前面省略了 `to be`。`be designed to V.` 指「被設計用來⋯⋯」，而否定的 `be designed not to V.` 則指「被設計成不⋯⋯」。

## 三、字彙 (Vocabulary)

### 核心字彙

1. **`*` advice** `[ədˈvaɪs]` **n. 建議、忠告**（不可數名詞，「一項建議」用 `a piece of advice` 表示）
   - Daniela asked her dad for **advice** about making big decisions.
2. **`*` confident** `[ˈkɑnfədənt]` **adj. 有信心的、自信的**
   - The runner looked **confident** while getting ready.
3. **`*` apologize** `[əˈpɑləˌdʒaɪz]` **v. 道歉、認錯**（作不及物動詞用，可接 `to sb` 或 `for sth`）
   - I **apologized** to my friend for arriving quite late.
4. **`*` situation** `[ˌsɪtʃəˈweʃən]` **n. 情況、形勢；處境**
   - It's important to understand a **situation** before taking action.
5. **`*` replace** `[rɪˈples]` **v. 取代、代替**（`replace A with B` 表示「用 B 取代 A」）
   - We need to **replace** the computer with a new one.

## 四、Info Box 補充註解

- **標題**：`take sb's side` 指「站在某人那一邊」，即「支持某人」的意思。
- **第 4 行**：`Stanford University` 指美國「史丹佛大學」，為全球最頂尖的私立研究型大學之一。
- **第 8 行**：`including` 作介系詞，表示「包含、包括」，用來引出所列舉的例子。
- **第 10 行**：`agree with sb` 指「同意某人的看法」。
- **第 11 行**：`be more likely to V.` 表示「比較有可能做⋯⋯」；**第 13 行** `point of view` 指「觀點、看法」，複數形為 `points of view`。
- **第 20 行**：`after all` 指「畢竟；終究」。

## 五、閱讀測驗 (Reading Comprehension)

**1. What is one possible negative result of turning to AI for advice?**
- (A) People may not be confident enough to ask real people for advice.
- (B) AI chatbots may encourage people to make bad decisions.
- (C) The chatbot may not be able to provide an answer.
- (D) People may become less willing to apologize for mistakes.

<details>
<summary>💡 點擊查看解答與解析</summary>

- **正確答案：(D)**
- **解析**：對應課文第 13–16 行 *"This may make them more confident that they are right and less willing to apologize when they make mistakes."*（這可能會讓他們更加確信自己是對的，並在犯錯時較不願意道歉），故選 (D)。
</details>
