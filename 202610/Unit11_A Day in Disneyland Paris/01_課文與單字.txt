【Part 1：讀門秘笈 Reading Skill Builder — Scanning 尋讀法 (p. 45)】

When we read, we are often trying to find specific information on a particular topic. In such cases, we don't want to spend our time reading every word of a text. Instead, we want to pick out key facts quickly. Scanning allows us to do this, so learning how to scan is an important skill.

1. Make sure the text you are looking at is likely to have the information you need. For example, check titles or the first lines of paragraphs.
2. Search for key words that are related to what you are looking for, such as names or dates. Do this by quickly moving your eyes over the page.
3. If you find a key word, read the text around it to check for important information.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
【Practice Time (p. 45)】
1. Imagine you are looking for information about different types of cars. Which of the words below should stand out to you as a key word?
   (A) Technology  (B) Supply  (C) Transport  (D) System
   ► 正解：(C) Transport（交通工具／運輸）

2. In the following paragraph, the sentences are numbered [1] to [5]. Which sentence tells us where Ed Sheeran spent his childhood?
   [1] Ed Sheeran is a musician known around the world, but his success did not come quickly.
   [2] He was born on February 17, 1991, to his father, John, and his mother, Imogen.
   [3] At a young age, he moved to Suffolk in eastern England, and that is where he grew up.
   [4] He began recording music in 2004 and burned copies of his first album himself when he was only 13.
   [5] However, his career did not really take off until he moved to London in 2008 and played in small clubs for years.
   ► 正解：[3]（關鍵字：At a young age、Suffolk in eastern England、where he grew up）

【Part 2：Day 23 主課文 — A Day in Disneyland Paris 巴黎迪士尼夢幻一日遊 (p. 46)】
[尋讀任務] Imagine you are searching for information on what to eat in Disneyland Paris. Scan the following text to find what options are available.

[Paragraph 1] (Lines 1–14)
Disneyland Paris is a magical place with rides, shows, and our favorite characters. When you enter, you’ll walk along Main Street toward¹ the incredible* castle at the end. But look out—below it sleeps a fearsome* dragon! Do you have the courage² to visit it? Other popular attractions include classics³ like Big Thunder Mountain, Pirates of the Caribbean, and Peter Pan’s Flight. Each area of the park has its own theme,* so you can explore many different exciting worlds in one day.

[Paragraph 2] (Lines 15–24)
If you get hungry, there is plenty of great food to enjoy. Visit Casa de Coco for Mexican dishes or Colonel Hathi’s Outpost for Indian and Italian food. Of course, there are also lots of snacks shaped like the famous Mickey Mouse, including ice cream bars⁴ and pizzas.

[Paragraph 3] (Lines 25–32)
At night, the park shines with lights, music, and fireworks.⁵ Since 2025, visitors have been able to experience Disney Tales of Magic, which takes you through scenes from movies like Cinderella and Encanto. This is a truly wonderful way to cap off a long day at one of the world’s best theme parks.

【Part 3：Vocabulary 必考字彙與參考字彙 (p. 47)】

*1. toward [tɔrd] prep. 朝、向（也可拼成 towards）
    例句：Let’s walk toward the beach soon.
    翻譯：我們待會兒朝海灘走去吧。

*2. courage [ˈkɝɪdʒ] n. [U] 勇氣、膽量（不可數）
    例句：Ella needed courage to try out a new haircut.
    翻譯：艾拉需要勇氣來嘗試新髮型。

*3. classic [ˈklæsɪk] n. [C] 經典作品、名著（亦可指經典遊樂設施）
    例句：This novel is a classic that everyone should read.
    翻譯：這部小說是人人都該閱讀的經典名著。

*4. bar [bɑr] n. [C] 條狀物；塊（文中 ice cream bar 指「雪糕；（含乳製品的）冰棒」）
    例句：There are dried fruits and nuts in the chocolate bar.
    翻譯：這塊巧克力棒裡有果乾和堅果。

*5. firework [ˈfaɪrˌwɝk] n. [C] 煙火、煙火表演（作此義時常用複數形 fireworks）
    例句：A fireworks show will be held by the river next month.
    翻譯：下個月河畔將舉辦一場煙火秀。

【字彙小筆記（僅供參考）】
- incredible [ɪnˈkrɛdəbəl] adj. 驚人的、極好的；令人難以置信的
- fearsome [ˈfɪrsəm] adj. 可怕的、令人生畏的
- theme [θim] n. [C] 主題（第 32 行 theme park 指「主題樂園」）

【地名與專有名詞小辭典】
- Paris [ˈpærɪs] n. 巴黎（法國首都）
- Mexican [ˈmɛksɪkən] adj. 墨西哥的
- Indian [ˈɪndiən] adj. 印度的
- Italian [ɪˈtæljən] adj. 義大利的
