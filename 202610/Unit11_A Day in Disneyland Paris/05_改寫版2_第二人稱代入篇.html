📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（第二人稱導覽代入版・約 175 字）
💬 文體特色：全程以祈使句與 you 引導讀者身歷其境，並把 Scanning 尋讀技巧融入旅遊情境
Imagine you are planning a trip to Disneyland Paris and scanning a guidebook to pick out key facts quickly. As soon as you step inside this magical park filled with rides, shows, and favorite characters, walk along Main Street toward the incredible castle at the end. However, look out—below it sleeps a fearsome dragon! If you have the courage to face it, step into its dark cave before trying popular classics like Big Thunder Mountain, Pirates of the Caribbean, and Peter Pan’s Flight. Since every zone has its own theme, you can easily explore several exciting worlds in one day.
想像你正在規劃一趟巴黎迪士尼之旅，並運用尋讀法瀏覽導覽手冊以快速挑出關鍵事實。一旦你踏入這座充滿遊樂設施、表演與喜愛角色的奇幻樂園，請沿著美國小鎮大街朝向盡頭那座令人驚嘆的城堡走去。然而，當心了——在城堡底下沉睡著一隻令人生畏的惡龍！如果你有勇氣面對牠，不妨在體驗巨雷山、加勒比海盜與小飛俠等熱門經典設施前先踏入牠的幽暗洞穴。因為每個區域都有自己的主題，你能在一天內輕鬆探索好幾個令人興奮的世界。
Whenever you feel hungry, remember that there is plenty of great food waiting for you. Head to Casa de Coco for Mexican dishes or Colonel Hathi’s Outpost for Indian and Italian meals. Better yet, there are adorable snacks shaped like Mickey Mouse, including ice cream bars and pizzas. When night falls and the park shines with fireworks, don’t leave early! Since 2025, you have been able to experience Disney Tales of Magic, which guides you through scenes from Cinderella and Encanto to cap off your long day at one of the world’s greatest theme parks.
每當你覺得餓時，別忘了這裡有許多美味的食物正等著你。前往可可餐廳品嚐墨西哥料理，或到哈athi上校前哨站享用印度與義大利餐點。更棒的是，還有許多做成米奇形狀的可愛點心，包括雪糕與披薩。當夜幕低垂、園區因煙火而閃耀時，可別太早離開！自從 2025 年以來，你便能體驗《Disney Tales of Magic》，它會引領你穿越《仙履奇緣》與《魔法滿屋》的電影場景，為你在世界最棒主題樂園之一的漫長一日畫下圓滿句點。
🧩 三、課文「積木拆句」＋ 逐行中翻英秘訣
🛠️ 學習模式切換：
句 1
Imagine you are planning a trip to Disneyland Paris and scanning a guidebook to pick out key facts quickly.
想像你正在規劃一趟巴黎迪士尼之旅，並運用尋讀法瀏覽導覽手冊以快速挑出關鍵事實。
[V] 祈使破題Imagine (that)想像一下
[S+V] 對稱進行式you are planning a trip to Disneyland Paris and scanning a guidebook你正規劃巴黎迪士尼之旅並尋讀手冊
[M] 目的不定詞to pick out key facts quickly.以快速挑出關鍵事實
💡 Imagine + (that) S. + V 情境導入句型
這是課本 p.45 練習題與 p.46 尋讀任務最愛的開場公式，寫英文演講稿或導覽文超吸睛！
🎯 結合讀門秘笈
把 scanning 與 pick out key facts quickly 放進旅遊情境，一舉兩得！
句 2
As soon as you step inside this magical park, walk along Main Street toward the incredible castle, but look out—below it sleeps a fearsome dragon!
一旦你踏入這座奇幻樂園，請沿著美國小鎮大街朝驚人的城堡走去，但當心——在它底下沉睡著一隻令人生畏的惡龍！
[Conj] 一……就……As soon as you step inside this magical park,一旦你踏入這座奇幻樂園
[V+M] 祈使動作walk along Main Street toward the incredible castle,沿著小鎮大街朝驚人城堡走去
[倒裝核心] 地方副詞句首but look out—below it sleeps a fearsome dragon!但當心——在它底下睡著一隻可怕惡龍！
💡 As soon as ＋ 祈使句 ＋ 地方副詞倒裝
將時間子句 When you enter 替換為 As soon as you step inside，語氣更緊湊。
🎯 倒裝句必背
below it sleeps a fearsome dragon 務必滾瓜爛熟！
句 3
If you have the courage to face it, step into its dark cave before trying popular classics like Big Thunder Mountain, Pirates of the Caribbean, and Peter Pan’s Flight.
如果你有勇氣面對牠，不妨在體驗巨雷山、加勒比海盜與小飛俠等熱門經典設施前先踏入牠的幽暗洞穴。
[Conj] 條件子句If you have the courage to face it,如果你有勇氣面對牠
[V+M] 祈使建議step into its dark cave踏入牠的幽暗洞穴
[M] 介系詞+V-ingbefore trying popular classics like Big Thunder Mountain, Pirates of the Caribbean, and Peter Pan’s Flight.在體驗熱門經典設施之前
💡 have the courage to face it ＋ before + V-ing
把原課文的疑問句 Do you have the courage to visit it? 轉為條件句 If you have the courage to face it...。
🎯 動詞搭配
「面對恐懼／惡龍」除了 visit，用 face 或 confront 更道地！
句 4
Whenever you feel hungry, remember that there is plenty of great food waiting for you, and there are adorable snacks shaped like Mickey Mouse, including ice cream bars and pizzas.
每當你覺得餓時，別忘了這裡有許多美味的食物正等著你，還有做成米奇形狀的可愛點心，包括雪糕與披薩。
[Conj] 複合關係副詞Whenever you feel hungry,每當你覺得餓時
[There be + V-ing] 主動修飾remember that there is plenty of great food waiting for you,別忘了有許多美食正等著你
[There be + p.p.] 被動修飾and there are adorable snacks shaped like Mickey Mouse, including ice cream bars and pizzas.還有做成米奇形狀的點心，包含雪糕與披薩
💡 一句同時練齊 There be + N + V-ing 與 There be + N + p.p.！
前半句 food waiting for you 用現在分詞表主動；後半句 snacks shaped like Mickey Mouse 用過去分詞表被動！
🎯 連接詞升級
If you get hungry → Whenever you feel hungry（無論何時你覺得餓）。
句 5
When night falls and the park shines with fireworks, since 2025, you have been able to experience Disney Tales of Magic to cap off your long day at one of the world’s greatest theme parks.
當夜幕低垂、園區因煙火而閃耀時，自從 2025 年以來，你便能體驗《Disney Tales of Magic》，為你在世界最棒主題樂園之一的漫長一日畫下圓滿句點。
[Conj] 時間副詞子句When night falls and the park shines with fireworks,當夜幕低垂且園區因煙火閃耀時
[Since + 現在完成式]since 2025, you have been able to experience Disney Tales of Magic自 2025 年起你已能體驗魔法大秀
[M] 目的不定詞to cap off your long day at one of the world’s greatest theme parks.為你在世界最棒主題樂園之一的漫長一天畫下完美句點
💡 寫景慣用語：When night falls（當夜幕低垂時）
取代單調的 At night，讓文章畫面感更強烈！
🎯 完美收尾
cap off your long day 為你的漫長一日畫下圓滿句點。
