📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（客觀旅遊報導改寫版・約 170 字）
📰 文體特色：以客觀第三人稱（travelers / visitors / guests）改寫，訓練學測閱讀測驗同義字替換能力
Disneyland Paris stands out as a magical destination featuring thrilling rides, live shows, and beloved characters. Upon entering the gates, travelers stroll along Main Street toward the incredible castle at the end. Yet visitors must look out—below it sleeps a fearsome dragon, waiting for those who possess the courage to approach its cave. Across the resort, popular attractions include timeless classics such as Big Thunder Mountain, Pirates of the Caribbean, and Peter Pan’s Flight. Because each zone features its own distinct theme, guests can explore multiple exciting worlds within a single day.
巴黎迪士尼樂園作為一處奇幻旅遊勝地脫穎而出，擁有刺激的遊樂設施、現場表演與深受喜愛的角色。一踏入大門，旅客便沿著美國小鎮大街朝向盡頭那座令人驚嘆的城堡漫步而去。然而遊客必須當心——在城堡底下沉睡著一隻令人生畏的惡龍，等待著擁有勇氣靠近其洞穴的人。在整個園區中，熱門景點包括巨雷山、加勒比海盜與小飛俠天空之旅等不朽經典。由於每個區域都具備獨特主題，賓客能在單日內探索多個令人興奮的世界。
For hungry travelers, there is plenty of great food available throughout the park. Guests can dine at Casa de Coco for Mexican dishes or Colonel Hathi’s Outpost for Indian and Italian cuisine. Furthermore, there are numerous snacks shaped like Mickey Mouse, including ice cream bars and pizzas. After dark, the sky glows with music, lights, and fireworks. Since 2025, visitors have been able to witness Disney Tales of Magic, an impressive spectacle presenting scenes from Cinderella and Encanto to cap off a memorable trip at one of the world’s premier theme parks.
對於飢餓的旅客而言，園區各處都有充足的美味食物可供享用。賓客可以在可可餐廳品嚐墨西哥菜餚，或在哈athi上校前哨站享用印度與義大利料理。此外，還有許多做成米奇形狀的點心，包括雪糕與披薩。天黑之後，夜空因音樂、燈光與煙火而閃耀。自從 2025 年以來，遊客便能目睹《Disney Tales of Magic》，這場令人印象深刻的視覺盛宴呈現了《仙履奇緣》與《魔法滿屋》的場景，為世界頂尖主題樂園之一的難忘旅程畫下圓滿句點。
🧩 三、課文「積木拆句」＋ 逐行中翻英秘訣
🛠️ 學習模式切換：
句 1
Disneyland Paris stands out as a magical destination featuring thrilling rides, live shows, and beloved characters.
巴黎迪士尼樂園作為一處奇幻旅遊勝地脫穎而出，擁有刺激的遊樂設施、現場表演與深受喜愛的角色。
[S] 主詞Disneyland Paris巴黎迪士尼
[V+M] 片語動詞stands out as a magical destination作為奇幻勝地脫穎而出
[M] 現在分詞片語featuring thrilling rides, live shows, and beloved characters.以刺激設施、表演與受喜愛角色為特色
💡 同義升級：with... → featuring...（以……為特色）
原課文的介系詞 with rides, shows... 在報導文體中常升級為現在分詞 featuring...！
🎯 尋讀秘笈呼應
stand out（顯眼、脫穎而出）正是 p.45 Practice Time 第 1 題出現的核心片語！
句 2
Upon entering the gates, travelers stroll along Main Street toward the incredible castle at the end.
一踏入大門，旅客便沿著美國小鎮大街朝向盡頭那座令人驚嘆的城堡漫步而去。
[M] 一……就……Upon entering the gates,一踏入大門
[S+V] 主詞+動詞travelers stroll along Main Street旅客沿著小鎮大街漫步
[M] 方向介系詞toward the incredible castle at the end.朝向盡頭驚人的城堡
💡 高中必背句型：Upon / On + V-ing（一……就……）
將原句的 When you enter 升級為 Upon entering the gates，是學測作文最受青睞的精簡結構。
🎯 動詞替換
stroll（悠閒漫步）比一般 walk 更能表現逛主題樂園的愜意感。
句 3
Yet visitors must look out—below it sleeps a fearsome dragon, waiting for those who possess the courage to approach its cave.
然而遊客必須當心——在城堡底下沉睡著一隻令人生畏的惡龍，等待著擁有勇氣靠近其洞穴的人。
[Conj+S+V] 轉折提醒Yet visitors must look out—然而遊客必須當心——
[M+V+S] 完全倒裝句below it sleeps a fearsome dragon,在它底下沉睡著一隻可怕惡龍
[M] 分詞構句+關代waiting for those who possess the courage to approach its cave.等待擁有勇氣靠近洞穴的人
💡 地方副詞完全倒裝 ＋ 分詞構句
結合 below it sleeps a fearsome dragon 與 those who possess the courage to V（凡是擁有勇氣做……的人）。
🎯 同義升級
have the courage → possess the courage（擁有勇氣）。
句 4
Across the resort, popular attractions include timeless classics such as Big Thunder Mountain, Pirates of the Caribbean, and Peter Pan’s Flight, and each zone features its own distinct theme.
在整個園區中，熱門景點包括巨雷山、加勒比海盜與小飛俠等不朽經典，且每個區域都具備獨特主題。
[S+V+O] 核心主句popular attractions include timeless classics熱門景點包括不朽經典
[M] 舉例片語such as Big Thunder Mountain, Pirates of the Caribbean, and Peter Pan’s Flight,例如三大經典設施
[Conj+S+V+O] 對等子句and each zone features its own distinct theme.且每區具備自己獨特的主題
💡 常見搭配詞：timeless classics（不朽經典）
形容歷久彌新的經典作品或遊樂設施，加上形容詞 timeless（不受時間影響的、永恆的）立刻加分！
🎯 同義替換
has its own theme → features its own distinct theme（具備其獨特主題）。
句 5
For hungry travelers, there is plenty of great food available, and there are numerous snacks shaped like Mickey Mouse, including ice cream bars and pizzas.
對於飢餓的旅客而言，這裡有充足的美味食物，還有許多做成米奇形狀的點心，包括雪糕與披薩。
[There is] 不可數存在句there is plenty of great food available,有充足的美味食物可享用
[There are] 複數存在句and there are numerous snacks且有許多點心
[M] 過去分詞片語shaped like Mickey Mouse, including ice cream bars and pizzas.形狀像米奇，包括雪糕與披薩
💡 單複數與後位修飾雙重對照
前半句 food 不可數用 there is；後半句 snacks 複數用 there are 並接過去分詞 shaped like...。
🎯 形容詞後位修飾
food available（可獲得的／供應中的食物）＝ food to enjoy。
句 6
After dark, the sky glows with fireworks, and since 2025, visitors have been able to witness Disney Tales of Magic to cap off a memorable trip.
天黑之後，夜空因煙火而閃耀，且自從 2025 年以來，遊客便能目睹《Disney Tales of Magic》，為難忘旅程畫下圓滿句點。
[M+S+V] 夜景描寫After dark, the sky glows with fireworks,天黑後夜空因煙火閃耀
[Since + 現在完成式]and since 2025, visitors have been able to witness Disney Tales of Magic且自 2025 年起遊客得以目睹魔法大秀
[M] 目的不定詞to cap off a memorable trip.為難忘旅程畫下圓滿句點
💡 Since 2025, S. + have been able to V
