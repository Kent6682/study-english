📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（客觀科普報導改寫版・約 175 字）
📰 文體特色：學測科普閱讀測驗最常見的「科學研究＋文化史」客觀報導結構
Few culinary herbs spark as much dispute as coriander, one of the world’s most hotly debated flavors. While fans admire its fresh taste in global cuisines, critics claim it tastes like soap. Scientific research reveals that this divide goes beyond personal preference: a specific gene makes certain individuals sensitive to soap-like chemicals called aldehydes, causing their brains to process the herb as unpleasant. However, this negative reaction is far less common among those raised eating it regularly.
極少有烹飪香草能像香菜一樣引發如此多的爭論，它是世界上爭論最激烈的風味之一。雖然愛好者在各國料理中讚賞它的清新滋味，批評者卻聲稱它嚐起來像肥皂。科學研究揭示，這種分歧超越了個人偏好：一種特定基因使某些人對稱為醛類的肥皂般化學物質敏感，導致他們的大腦將這種香草解讀為令人不快的味道。然而，這種負面反應在從小經常吃香菜長大的人當中少見得多。
Linguistically, Americans call the leaves cilantro, while British speakers call both leaves and seeds coriander—a term derived from a Greek word for “stink bug.” Historically, coriander’s story stretches back to 6000 BCE in the Levant. Romans used its seeds to flavor bread and preserve meat, and by the Middle Ages, the plant had spread across Europe before explorers introduced it to Asia. Today, Taiwan leads the way in unusual coriander products like coriander-flavored chips, and a coriander pizza released by a Japanese restaurant chain became such a hit that it returned with twice the amount of the herb, alongside special festivals and perfumes for devoted fans.
從語言學來看，美國人稱葉子為 cilantro，而英國人則將葉子與種子都稱為 coriander——這個詞源自希臘文的「臭蟲」。從歷史來看，香菜的故事可追溯至西元前 6000 年的黎凡特。羅馬人用它的種子調味麵包並保存肉類，到了中世紀，這種植物已傳遍歐洲，隨後探險家將它引介至亞洲。如今，台灣在香菜口味洋芋片等新奇香菜產品上引領潮流，而日本一家連鎖餐廳推出的香菜披薩更成為如此熱賣的商品，以致於後來以雙倍分量的香菜回歸，此外還有專為忠實粉絲舉辦的特別節慶與香水。

句 1

用「極少有香草能像香菜一樣引發這麼多爭論」加上同位語，是學測作文首段破題的滿分句型！

「引發爭論」動詞可用 spark / trigger / cause dispute。
句 2

前半句 fresh taste 是名詞；後半句 tastes like soap 是連綴動詞 + like + N.！
🎯 同義替換秘訣

[M] 結果分詞構句causing their brains to process the herb as unpleasant導致大腦將其解讀為不快
💡 過去分詞片語簡化 + 結果分詞構句
1. chemicals (that are) called aldehydes 省略關代與 be 動詞。
2. 句尾 , causing their brains to process... 表達自然而然產生的結果。
🎯 同義替換秘訣
課文 is about more than personal preference 換成 goes beyond personal preference（超越個人偏好）。
句 4

然而，這種負面反應在從小經常吃香菜長大的人當中少見得多。
[S] 主詞this negative reaction這種負面反應
[V+C] 比較級補語is far less common少見得多
[M] 分詞後位修飾among those (who were) raised eating it regularly在從小常吃它被撫養長大的人之中

those raised eating it 等於課文的 people who grew up eating it！
🎯 同義替換秘訣
在正式科普文中，常用 those who... 或 individuals who... 取代口語的 `people who...`。

Part A — 克漏字綜合測驗 (Cloze Test, 第 1–5 題)
Few herbs spark as much dispute as coriander. (__1__) fans admire its fresh taste in global cuisines, critics claim it tastes like soap. Research shows that a specific gene makes certain individuals sensitive to aldehydes, (__2__) their brains to process the herb as unpleasant. Linguistically, Americans call the leaves cilantro, whereas British speakers call both parts coriander—a term (__3__) from a Greek word for “stink bug.” Historically, by the Middle Ages, the plant (__4__) across Europe. Today, a coriander pizza released by a Japanese restaurant chain became (__5__) popular hit that it returned with twice the amount of the herb.

1. 請選出最適合填入 (__1__) 的連接詞：

✅ 正解 (A) While！置於句首引導讓步子句，表「雖然、儘管（= Although）」。

2. 請選出最適合填入 (__2__) 的分詞構句形式：

✅ 正解 (B) causing！逗號後無連接詞，用現在分詞 causing 表達前面子句所造成的自然結果（= which causes）。

3. 請選出最適合填入 (__3__) 表示「源自於」的分詞：

✅ 正解 (B) derived！a term (which is) derived from... 表「源自於……的詞彙」。

4. 請選出最適合填入 (__4__) 的動詞時態：

✅ 正解 (B) had spread！搭配 by the Middle Ages（到了過去某時間點為止），用過去完成式 had spread。

5. 請選出最適合填入 (__5__) 的字詞：

✅ 正解 (B) such a！後方有單數可數名詞 popular hit 與結果子句 that...，公式為 such + a + Adj. + 單數可數名詞 + that。
Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (第 6–9 題)

6. Heated ________ over whether coriander belongs on pizza has divided food lovers across Asia.

✅ 正解 (A) dispute（爭論、爭議）！關於香菜是否該放在披薩上的激烈「爭論（dispute）」讓亞洲饕客分成了兩派。

7. Human brains ________ visual and chemical signals from food in less than a second to determine if it is safe to eat.

✅ 正解 (B) process（處理、解讀）！人類大腦在不到一秒內「處理、解讀（process）」來自食物的視覺與化學訊號。

8. Thousands of ________ fans gathered at the annual festival wearing green outfits to show their love for cilantro.

✅ 正解 (A) devoted（忠實的、摯愛的）！數千名「忠實粉絲（devoted fans）」齊聚年度節慶。

9. Taiwanese night markets are famous for offering a wide variety of local ________ and creative snacks.

✅ 正解 (C) cuisines（料理、菜餚）！台灣夜市以提供各式各樣的在地「料理（cuisines）」與創意小吃聞名。
Part C — 重要文法與中翻英除錯 (第 10–12 題)

10. Which of the following sentences is grammatically CORRECT for expressing “two times the amount”?

✅ 正解 (B)！twice + as + 原級 (much) + N. + as 等於 twice the amount of coriander。(A) twice 避免搭配 比較級 + than。

11. The coriander ice cream my sister bought at the night market ________ surprisingly refreshing.

✅ 正解 (B) tastes！後方接形容詞 refreshing（清爽的），連綴動詞直接用 tastes + Adj.，不加 like！

12. 【中翻英除錯】「這場關於香菜的爭論可以追溯到數千年前。」哪一句完全正確？

✅ 正解 (B)！「關於……的爭論」用 the debate over...，「追溯數千年」用主動語態 stretches back thousands of years。
Part D — 大考閱讀測驗 (第 13–15 題)

13. Why does the author mention “aldehydes” in the first paragraph of the rewritten report?

✅ 正解 (B)！提及醛類（aldehydes）是為了解釋為何特定基因攜帶者會覺得香菜有肥皂味。

14. How does British English differ from American English in naming the plant?

✅ 正解 (B)！美式英文將葉子稱為 cilantro、種子稱為 coriander；英式英文則兩者皆稱 coriander。

15. What can be inferred about the coriander trend in East Asia today?

✅ 正解 (C)！從台灣引領獵奇香菜商品潮流與日本必勝客雙倍香菜披薩熱賣可知，大膽創新的香菜商品在台日消費者間極受歡迎。
