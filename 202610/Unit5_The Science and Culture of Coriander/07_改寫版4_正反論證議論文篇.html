📖 二、完整課文沉浸閱讀（正反論證議論文篇・約 180 字）
⚖️ 文體特色：採用 On the one hand... On the other hand... In conclusion... 學測論說文標準架構
Whether coriander is a culinary treasure or a soapy disaster remains one of the world’s most hotly debated flavors. On the one hand, critics in this dispute argue that it tastes like soap, a claim supported by biology rather than mere personal preference. Individuals carrying a specific gene are sensitive to soap chemicals called aldehydes, causing their brains to process the herb as unpleasant. Even its name—called cilantro for leaves in the US while called coriander for both leaves and seeds in the UK—comes from a Greek word for “stink bug.”
香菜究竟是烹飪瑰寶還是肥皂味災難，至今仍是世界上爭論最激烈的風味之一。一方面，這場爭論中的批評者主張它嚐起來像肥皂，這項說法有生物學根據，而非單純的個人偏好。帶有特定基因的人對稱為醛類的肥皂化學物敏感，導致大腦將這種香草解讀為令人不快的味道。就連它的名字——在美國葉子稱為 cilantro，而在英國葉子與種子皆稱為 coriander——也源自希臘文的「臭蟲」。
On the other hand, cultural exposure can overcome genetics, as this negative reaction is far less common among people who grew up enjoying its fresh taste in traditional cuisines. Historically, its value stretches back to 6000 BCE; Romans used it to flavor bread and preserve meat, and by the Middle Ages, it had spread across Europe before explorers brought it to Asia. In conclusion, controversy fuels innovation: Taiwan leads the way in unusual products like coriander-flavored chips, and a coriander pizza released by a Japanese chain became such a hit that it returned with twice the amount of the herb, alongside festivals and perfumes for devoted fans.
另一方面，後天的文化接觸能克服先天基因，因為這種負面反應在從小於傳統料理中享用其清新滋味長大的人當中少見得多。從歷史來看，它的價值可追溯至西元前 6000 年；羅馬人用它調味麵包並保存肉類，到了中世紀它已傳遍歐洲，隨後探險家將它帶到亞洲。總結而言，爭議反而點燃了創新：台灣在香菜口味洋芋片等新奇產品上引領潮流，而日本連鎖店推出的香菜披薩更成為如此熱賣的商品，以致於帶著雙倍分量的香菜回歸，此外還有為忠實粉絲舉辦的節慶與香水。

第一段先講先天基因（`On the one hand`），第二段立刻用 On the other hand 切入後天飲食文化，層次分明！

Part A — 克漏字綜合測驗 (Cloze Test, 第 1–5 題)
Whether coriander is a culinary treasure or a soapy disaster (__1__) one of the world’s most hotly debated topics. On the one hand, critics argue that it tastes like soap, a claim (__2__) by biology rather than mere personal preference. Even its name—called cilantro in the US (__3__) called coriander in the UK—comes from a Greek word for “stink bug.” (__4__), cultural exposure can overcome genetics, as this negative reaction is far less common among people raised eating it regularly. In conclusion, a coriander pizza released by a Japanese chain became (__5__) that it returned with twice the amount of the herb!

1. 請選出最適合填入 (__1__) 的動詞：

✅ 正解 (B) remains！以名詞子句 Whether coriander is A or B 當主詞時視為單數，故配單數動詞 remains。

2. 請選出最適合填入 (__2__) 的分詞：

✅ 正解 (B) supported！這項主張是「被生物學所支持」，用過去分詞 a claim (which is) supported by biology。

3. 請選出最適合填入 (__3__) 的對比連接詞：

✅ 正解 (A) while！對照美國與英國的不同稱呼，用對比連接詞 while。

4. 請選出最適合填入 (__4__) 與前段 On the one hand 呼應的轉折詞：

✅ 正解 (B) On the other hand（另一方面）！與第一段的 On the one hand（一方面） 構成正反並陳對比。

5. 請選出最適合填入 (__5__) 的結構：

✅ 正解 (A) such a hit！such + a + 單數可數名詞 (hit) + that。
Part B — 文意字彙與片語搭配 (第 6–9 題)

6. After years of hard work and practice, the young violinist finally ________ her place in the national orchestra.

✅ 正解 (B) earned！片語 earn one's place 表示「贏得／取得一席之地」。

7. The fast-food ________ has opened more than three hundred branches across Taiwan over the past decade.

✅ 正解 (A) chain（連鎖企業、連鎖店）！the fast-food chain 指「這家速食連鎖企業」。

8. Dairy ________ such as milk, cheese, and yogurt should be kept in the refrigerator to stay fresh.

✅ 正解 (C) products（產品）！dairy products 指「乳製品」。

9. The town council held a public meeting to ________ whether to build a new shopping mall near the park.

✅ 正解 (A) debate（討論、辯論）！舉辦公聽會以「辯論、討論（debate）」是否興建新購物中心。
Part C — 重要文法與中翻英除錯 (第 10–12 題)

10. Cats are often independent and quiet, ________ dogs tend to be more social and energetic.

✅ 正解 (C) while！課本 Language Notes 經典例句：用 , while ... 對照貓與狗的相反習性。

11. Which of the following sentences is grammatically INCORRECT?

✅ 正解 (D) 為錯誤句子！課本 p. 27 明確指出：以 twice 進行比較時，避免使用 twice + 比較級 + than，應改用 two times larger than。

12. 【中翻英除錯】「不管你喜歡還是討厭它，香菜顯然已經在餐桌上取得一席之地。」哪一句完全正確？

✅ 正解 (A)！Love it or hate it 為 Whether you love it or hate it 的慣用簡化句，後接主動完成式 has clearly earned its place at the table。
Part D — 大考閱讀測驗 (第 13–15 題)

13. In this argumentative essay, what does “Nature vs. Nurture” in the title refer to?

✅ 正解 (B)！Nature 指先天生物基因（DNA 與醛類敏感度），Nurture 指後天飲食文化養成（從小常吃香菜長大可減少排斥反應）。

14. How does the author view the intense controversy surrounding coriander in the conclusion?

✅ 正解 (B)！作者在結論指出 controversy fuels innovation（爭議反而點燃了產品創新與熱銷話題）。

15. Which of the following is NOT mentioned as a historical or modern use of coriander?

✅ 正解 (D)！文中僅提到在黎凡特洞穴發現西元前 6000 年最古老的香菜種子，並未提及用來治療基因疾病。
