一、逐句中英對照翻譯
============================================================

【第一段：現代精靈形象的由來】
S01 They are tall, elegant, immortal artists who speak beautiful languages and are warriors with better aim than any human archer.
    他們身材高挑、優雅、長生不死，是說著優美語言的藝術家，也是箭術比任何人類弓箭手都精準的戰士。
S02 This is the image of elves that most of us recognize from modern films and video games.
    這是我們大多數人從現代電影和電玩中所認識的精靈形象。
S03 But that familiar picture is surprisingly recent, and largely the creation of J. R. R. Tolkien, whose mid-1950s novel The Lord of the Rings changed fantasy forever.
    但這幅熟悉的畫面出現的時間竟出奇地晚，而且大體上是 J. R. R. 托爾金的創作——他在 1950 年代中期出版的小說《魔戒》永遠改變了奇幻文學。

【第二段：北歐神話中的光明與黑暗精靈】
S04 After Tolkien's books became global classics, films, games, and other writers adopted his vision, but long before this, elves appeared in different forms across cultures.
    托爾金的作品成為全球經典之後，電影、遊戲和其他作家都採用了他的構想；但早在此之前，精靈就已經以不同的樣貌出現在各個文化中。
S05 For example, in Norse mythology, elves were mysterious spirits tied to light, darkness, and the forces of nature.
    例如在北歐神話中，精靈是與光明、黑暗和自然力量緊密相連的神祕靈體。
S06 The light elves were beings associated with beauty and fertility, while dark elves lived underground and were sometimes mixed up with dwarves.
    光明精靈是與美麗和豐饒相關的生物，而黑暗精靈則住在地底下，有時還會被人和矮人搞混。

【第三段：亦善亦惡，至今仍存在於北歐信仰】
S07 In those same traditions, these elves were powerful figures who could be both kind and dangerous.
    在這些相同的傳說中，這些精靈是既能仁慈、也能危險的強大存在。
S08 Some might bless humans with fortune or success, while others could also bring illness, suffering, or death.
    有些精靈可能會賜予人類好運或成功，有些則也可能帶來疾病、苦難甚至死亡。
S09 Even today, elves have not completely disappeared from beliefs in Nordic countries.
    即使到了今天，精靈也還沒有完全從北歐國家的信仰中消失。
S10 In Iceland, many people speak of huldufólk, the “hidden people,” who live quietly beside humans.
    在冰島，許多人會談到 huldufólk，也就是靜靜住在人類身旁的「隱藏的人們」。
S11 Some roads there have even been redirected to avoid disturbing rocks where elves might live.
    當地有些道路甚至為了避免打擾到精靈可能居住的岩石而改道。

【第四段：中世紀英德——製造病痛的淘氣鬼】
S12 During the Middle Ages, in England and Germany, elves were blamed for unexplained pains and illnesses.
    在中世紀的英格蘭和德國，人們把原因不明的疼痛和疾病歸咎於精靈。
S13 If someone felt a sharp pain in their side, people might say they had been struck by an “elf-shot”—an invisible arrow fired by an elf playing tricks.
    如果有人感到側腹一陣刺痛，人們可能會說他被「精靈箭」射中了——那是精靈在惡作劇時射出的一支隱形箭。
S14 Farmers also blamed elves when animals grew sick, crops failed, or tools went missing.
    當牲畜生病、農作物歉收或工具不見時，農民也會怪罪精靈。
S15 In these societies, elves were not heroes but rather hidden, naughty creatures looking to cause trouble.
    在這些社會裡，精靈不是英雄，而是一心想惹麻煩、躲在暗處的淘氣生物。

【第五段：凱爾特傳說中的精靈一族】
S16 Meanwhile, in the Celtic traditions of Ireland and Scotland, similar stories exist of fairy folk.
    同時，在愛爾蘭和蘇格蘭的凱爾特傳統中，也流傳著關於精靈一族的類似故事。
S17 These creatures were often described as smaller than humans, with behavior that could be chaotic or even cruel.
    這些生物常被描述為比人類矮小，行為可能混亂無序，甚至殘忍。
S18 Tales described them living in hills or near special trees and dancing in fairy rings to lure humans into their world.
    傳說描述他們住在山丘裡或特殊的樹木附近，並在精靈圈裡跳舞，引誘人類進入他們的世界。
S19 They might take a human baby and leave a fairy child, called a changeling, in its place.
    他們可能會抱走人類的嬰兒，留下一個被稱為「換生靈」的精靈孩子代替。
S20 Other tales say they sometimes punished rude travelers by drowning them in rivers.
    其他傳說則說，他們有時會把無禮的旅人淹死在河裡作為懲罰。

【第六段：結語——千年傳統的最新版本】
S21 Over the centuries, elves had many different images.
    幾個世紀以來，精靈有過許多不同的形象。
S22 The tall, elegant archer of modern fantasy works is only one of the latest versions.
    現代奇幻作品中那位高挑優雅的弓箭手，只是最新的版本之一。
S23 Beneath that familiar figure lies a much older and richer series of traditions that stretches back hundreds of years and across cultures.
    在那個熟悉的形象底下，是一連串更古老、更豐富的傳統，可以追溯到數百年前，並橫跨多種文化。

二、關鍵解析詳細拆解
============================================================

1. mix up A with B（把 A 和 B 搞混）
   • 課文：dark elves ... were sometimes mixed up with dwarves.（被動：A be mixed up with B）
   • 主動：People sometimes mixed up dark elves with dwarves.
   • 近義：confuse A with B；分不清：can't tell A from B。
   • 注意：mix up 受詞為代名詞時要放中間：mix them up（✗ mix up them）。

2. some ... others ...（有些……有些……）
   • 課文：Some might bless humans with fortune or success, while others could also bring illness ...
   • 範圍不限定 ➔ some ... others ...；只分兩類、其餘全部 ➔ some ... the others ...。
   • 只有兩個時：one ... the other ...。

3. if 引導子句
   • (a) 直說法「一旦／每當」：描述過去慣常情況用過去式，主句 would/might 表過去習慣或推測。
     課文：If someone felt a sharp pain in their side, people might say they had been struck by an “elf-shot.”
     ➔ had been struck：過去完成被動，表示「中箭」發生在「感到疼痛／人們說」之前。
   • (b) 與現在事實相反：If + S + 過去式（were）, S + would/could/might + V。

4. 地方副詞（片語）置首倒裝
   • 課文：Beneath that familiar figure lies a much older and richer series of traditions ...
     ＝ A much older and richer series of traditions lies beneath that familiar figure.
   • 主詞是名詞 ➔ 倒裝（地方副詞 + V + S）；主詞是代名詞 ➔ 不倒裝（Here it is.）。
   • 動詞單複數依倒裝後的主詞：a ... series（單數）➔ lies。

5. Info Box 補充
   • beings associated with ...：= beings that were associated with ...（過去分詞片語修飾 beings）
   • redirect：re-（重新）+ direct（指向）＝改道
   • looking to cause trouble：= that were looking to cause trouble；be looking to V＝打算做……
   • similar stories exist of fairy folk：of＝about；原本 similar stories of fairy folk exist，因主詞太長、動詞太短，把 of 片語移到句尾。
   • describe A as + N./Adj.：把 A 描述為……；describe A + V-ing：描述 A 正在……
   • in its place：代替它（its＝the human baby）

6. 其他重要句型
   • be blamed for N.／blame A for B／blame B on A：把 B 怪到 A 頭上
   • not A but (rather) B：不是 A 而是 B
   • punish sb by V-ing：以……的方式懲罰某人
   • bless A with B：賜予 A 某物
   • tied to／associated with：與……有關聯
   • stretch back + 時間：可追溯到……之前

Reading Comprehension
1. What does vision on line 13 refer to?
   (A) Tolkien's plan to write more novels
   (B) Tolkien's success in fantasy writing
   (C) Tolkien's idea of what elves were like
   (D) Tolkien's knowledge of ancient mythology
   ➔ 答案 (C)。vision 指托爾金對精靈的「構想」，即上一段所描述的高挑優雅精靈形象。

2. According to the first part, which statement is NOT true?
   (A) In Iceland, stories about elves are still told today.
   (B) Tolkien had a strong influence on modern fantasy.
   (C) Old stories sometimes connected elves with nature.
   (D) In Norse mythology, elves were always friendly to people.
   ➔ 答案 (D)。課文說北歐精靈 could be both kind and dangerous，有些還會帶來疾病和死亡。
