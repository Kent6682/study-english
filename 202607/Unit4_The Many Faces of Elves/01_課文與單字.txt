【第一部分】

[P1]
(01) They are tall, elegant, immortal artists who speak beautiful languages
(02) and are warriors with better aim than any human archer. This is the image
(05) of elves that most of us recognize from modern films and video games. But that
(07) familiar picture is surprisingly recent, and largely the creation of J. R. R. Tolkien,
(09) whose mid-1950s novel The Lord of the Rings changed fantasy forever.

[P2]
(11) After Tolkien's books became global classics, films, games, and other writers
(13) adopted his vision, but long before this, elves appeared in different forms across
(15) cultures. For example, in Norse mythology, elves were mysterious spirits tied to light,
(17) darkness, and the forces of nature. The light elves were beings associated with
(19) beauty and fertility, while dark elves lived underground and were sometimes mixed
(21) up with dwarves.

[P3]
(22) In those same traditions, these elves were powerful figures who could be both
(24) kind and dangerous. Some might bless humans with fortune or success, while
(26) others could also bring illness, suffering, or death. Even today, elves have not
(28) completely disappeared from beliefs in Nordic countries. In Iceland, many
(30) people speak of huldufólk, the “hidden people,” who live quietly beside humans.
(32) Some roads there have even been redirected to avoid disturbing rocks
(34) where elves might live.

【第二部分】

[P4]
(01) During the Middle Ages, in England and Germany, elves were blamed for
(03) unexplained pains and illnesses. If someone felt a sharp pain in their side,
(05) people might say they had been struck by an “elf-shot”—an invisible arrow
(07) fired by an elf playing tricks. Farmers also blamed elves when animals grew
(09) sick, crops failed, or tools went missing. In these societies, elves were not heroes
(11) but rather hidden, naughty creatures looking to cause trouble.

[P5]
(13) Meanwhile, in the Celtic traditions of Ireland and Scotland, similar stories
(15) exist of fairy folk. These creatures were often described as smaller than humans,
(17) with behavior that could be chaotic or even cruel. Tales described them living
(19) in hills or near special trees and dancing in fairy rings to lure humans into
(21) their world. They might take a human baby and leave a fairy child, called a
(23) changeling, in its place. Other tales say they sometimes punished rude travelers
(25) by drowning them in rivers.

[P6]
(26) Over the centuries, elves had many different images. The tall, elegant archer
(28) of modern fantasy works is only one of the latest versions. Beneath that
(30) familiar figure lies a much older and richer series of traditions that stretches
(32) back hundreds of years and across cultures.

三、關鍵解析 Language Notes
============================================================

【第一部分】
1. mix up 的用法
   片語動詞 mix up 作及物用，指「把……搞混；把……放錯位置」，常見句型為：
     mix up + 複數名詞
     mix up A with B（文中使用被動語態）
   • Nolan mixed up the folders and filed the report in the wrong one.
     諾蘭把資料夾弄混，把報告歸錯了資料夾。
   • Quinn mixed up salt with sugar when she made coffee.
     奎茵煮咖啡時把鹽和糖搞混了。

2. some ... others ... 的用法
   some ... others ... 用來列舉沒有限定範圍的人或事物。some 和 other 亦可作形容詞，
   用法為：some N. ... other N. ...。為避免重複名詞，other N. 常用代名詞 others 代替。
   • When the students visited the night market, some bought fried chicken, and others chose bubble tea.
     學生們逛夜市時，有些學生買了炸雞，有些則選了珍奶。
   • At the book sale, some books were new, and others were old.
     在書籍特賣會上，有些書是新的，有些則是舊的。
   【比較用法】若只限於兩類，則需加上定冠詞，寫作：some ... the others ...（有些……其餘的……）。
   • At the party, some guests sat inside, and the others stayed in the yard.
     派對上，有些客人坐在室內，其餘的則待在院子裡。

【第二部分】
1. if 引導子句的用法
   (a) 用來說明在特定情況下可能發生的情況及其結果，表示「一旦……；假使……」，意同 when 或 in the event that。
       子句須用直說法，時式依所描述的時間而定，文中 If someone felt a sharp pain ..., people might say ...
       描述過去傳說，故用過去式。
       • When I was a baby, if I cried, my grandma would sing a soft song to calm me.
         我還是嬰兒時，如果哭了，奶奶會唱輕柔的歌來哄我。
   (b) 用來描述假設情況，子句中的動詞須用過去式（be 動詞在正式用法中一律用 were）；
       主要子句則用 would/could/should/might + V. 來表示假設的結果，
       這種句型常用來表達不太可能發生的情況，或與現在事實相反的假設。
       • If Amy were more careful, she could make fewer mistakes on the exam.
         如果艾咪更細心一點，她在考試中就能少犯一些錯。

2. 地方副詞（片語）置於句首的倒裝句
   地方副詞（片語）置於句首時，主詞和動詞須倒裝，主詞為代名詞時則無須倒裝。句型如下：
     地方副詞（片語）+ be 動詞/V. + S.（名詞）
     地方副詞（片語）+ S.（代名詞）+ be 動詞/V.
   • At the corner of the street stood a tall boy with a blue backpack.
     街角站著一個背著藍色背包的高個子男孩。
   • Behind the door she waited quietly for the interview to begin.
     她在門後靜靜地等待面試開始。

四、Info Box
============================================================
• 第 8 行 J. R. R. Tolkien（J. R. R. 托爾金）是英國著名作家，被譽為「現代奇幻文學之父」，
  以經典奇幻巨作《魔戒》（The Lord of the Rings）聞名於世。
• 第 18 行，being 在此作名詞，指「生物；生命體」。分詞片語 associated with 前面省略了 that were，
  A be associated with B 指「A 與 B 聯繫在一起；A 與 B 有關」。
• 第 33 行 redirect 為動詞，意指「改變……的方向；使……改道、轉向別處」，
  由字首 re-（再次、重新）與動詞 direct（朝向、指向）組合而成。
• 第 12 行分詞片語 looking to V. 前面省略了 that were，be looking to V. 表示「打算、計畫做……」。
• 第 15 行的 of 表示「關於」，用來引出故事的主題，fairy folk 則指「精靈、仙子、妖精一族」。
• 第 16 行 describe sb/sth as N./Adj. 表示「描述某人或某事物為……」；
  第 18 行 describe (sb/sth) + V-ing 則表示「描述（……）正……」。
• 第 23 行 in one's place 表示「代替某人；處於某人的處境或位置」。

圖說
• 拜《魔戒》系列作品所賜，人們一提到「精靈」，往往會聯想到高冷優雅、身手敏捷且箭術高超的形象。
• 在冰島各地常可看到只有幾十公分高的迷你小屋。根據當地民間傳說，這些小屋是用來庇護傳說中的「隱藏的人們」。
• 所謂的 fairy ring（精靈圈）是自然形成的蘑菇圈。在凱爾特民間傳說中，精靈會在其中起舞，並引誘人類進入他們的世界。
