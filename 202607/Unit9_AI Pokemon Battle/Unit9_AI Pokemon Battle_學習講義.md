## 二、課文（中英對照）

**¶1** Forget boring math tests and essay writing! Tech giants are testing the world's smartest generative AI models by making them play classic Pokémon games.
> 別再提無聊的數學考試和作文了！科技巨擘正透過讓全球最聰明的生成式 AI 模型玩經典寶可夢遊戲，來測試它們。

**¶2** For researchers, this is more than just a fun experiment. Pokémon games are turn-based and **involve** solving puzzles. They are an excellent way to test **whether** AI can think ahead and stay focused on long, complex missions. While these models can write complex code in seconds, they often struggle with the tasks in a video game. AI players can **end up** going around in circles for hours because they forget a key item or panic when a Pokémon is close to losing energy.
> 對研究人員來說，這不只是一場有趣的實驗。寶可夢遊戲採回合制，而且需要解謎。它們是測試 AI 能否提前規劃、並在漫長複雜的任務中保持專注的絕佳方式。雖然這些模型幾秒內就能寫出複雜的程式碼，但面對電玩遊戲中的任務卻常常很吃力。AI 玩家可能因為忘記一項關鍵道具，或在寶可夢快要沒有體力時驚慌失措，結果原地打轉好幾個小時。

**¶3** Nonetheless, there **has been** progress: Newer versions of these models can now beat entire games like *Pokémon Red* or *Blue* in far fewer steps than before. This shows that they can learn to play and perform better. If you want to see this for yourself, Twitch live streams **such as** *Claude Plays Pokémon* and *Gemini Plays Pokémon* let fans watch AI attempt to master Pokémon and become champions.
> 儘管如此，AI 仍有進步：這些模型的較新版本現在能以比過去少得多的步驟，破完《寶可夢 紅》或《藍》這類完整的遊戲。這顯示它們能夠學會玩遊戲，並表現得更好。如果你想親眼看看，《Claude 玩寶可夢》和《Gemini 玩寶可夢》等 Twitch 直播頻道，能讓粉絲看著 AI 試圖精通寶可夢、成為冠軍。

## 三、克漏字五題考點

| 題 | 答案 | 考點 | 易混選項 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | involve | involve + N./V-ing 涉及、需要 | allow、practice、encourage |
| 2 | whether | test 後接 whether 子句：是否 | why、what、whenever |
| 3 | end up | end up + V-ing 結果落得 | stick to、keep up、start out |
| 4 | has been | 現在完成式：進展延續至今 | used to be、must be、will be |
| 5 | such as | 列舉例子 | instead of、except for、as well as |
