## 二、課文（中英對照）

**¶1** Walking is one of the easiest ways to stay active. You don't need special equipment, a gym membership, or a lot of time. Still, a large number of people believe walking is "not enough" for real health benefits. Let's look at four common myths about walking and what makes them false.
> 走路是保持活躍最簡單的方法之一。你不需要特殊的器材、健身房會員或大量的時間。然而，仍有許多人認為，要獲得真正的健康益處，走路「還不夠」。讓我們來看看關於走路的四個常見迷思，以及它們錯在哪裡。

**Myth 1: "Walking isn't real exercise."**（迷思一：「走路不算真正的運動。」）

**¶2** Many people think only activities such as running, cycling, or going to the gym count as exercise. But this is not true. Brisk walking is a form of moderate-intensity exercise. Organizations like the World Health Organization recommend at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity each week, and walking fits this recommendation. Walking can improve heart health, lower blood pressure, and reduce the risk of type 2 diabetes. It can also improve your mood. So yes, walking is definitely real exercise—and it has the health benefits to prove it.
> 很多人認為只有跑步、騎自行車或上健身房這類活動才算運動。但事實並非如此。快走是一種中等強度的運動。世界衛生組織等機構建議每週至少從事 150 分鐘的中等強度有氧活動，而走路正符合這項建議。走路能改善心臟健康、降低血壓，並減少罹患第二型糖尿病的風險。它還能改善你的心情。所以沒錯，走路絕對是真正的運動——而且它的健康益處就是最好的證明。

**Myth 2: "If you're not sweating, it's not effective."**（迷思二：「如果你沒流汗，就沒有效果。」）

**¶3** Some people believe exercise must make you sweat a lot to be useful. In reality, sweat is not a reliable measure of how effective your workout is. Even light walking can help improve blood flow, control blood sugar, and help keep your joints flexible. Don't worry about sweating—the healthy approach is to focus on moving regularly and consistently.
> 有些人認為運動一定要讓你大量流汗才有用。實際上，汗水並不是衡量你的運動有多有效的可靠標準。即使是輕度的走路，也能幫助改善血液循環、控制血糖，並幫助你的關節保持靈活。別擔心流不流汗——健康的做法是專注於規律且持續地活動。

**Myth 3: "You have to walk fast to see results."**（迷思三：「你必須走得很快才看得到成效。」）

**¶4** It is often thought that if you aren't power walking like you're late for a flight, it doesn't count. Although it's true that higher speeds do burn more calories per minute, the real key to making the most of walking is consistency. Of course, if you prefer to add more intensity, you can try walking up hills or at a slightly faster pace. However, a comfortable walk will also strengthen your body. Focus on regular movement, and your health will thank you.
> 一般認為，如果你不是像趕飛機一樣地健走，就不算數。雖然速度越快確實每分鐘燃燒的卡路里越多，但要充分發揮走路效益，真正的關鍵在於持之以恆。當然，如果你比較喜歡增加強度，可以試著走上坡，或用稍微快一點的步調行走。不過，舒適地散步同樣能強健你的身體。專注於規律的活動，你的健康會感謝你的。

**Myth 4: "You must walk 10,000 steps a day."**（迷思四：「你一天必須走一萬步。」）

**¶5** This notion started as a marketing idea in Japan many years ago. However, it has never been an official health recommendation. Research shows that health benefits can begin at around 4,000 steps per day for many adults. More movement is usually better, but some activity is much better than doing nothing. If 10,000 steps feels too hard, start with a smaller goal and build up slowly.
> 這個觀念是多年前在日本以一個行銷點子起家的。然而，它從來都不是官方的健康建議。研究顯示，對許多成年人來說，每天大約 4,000 步就能開始帶來健康益處。活動量越多通常越好，但做一些活動遠比什麼都不做好得多。如果一萬步感覺太難，就從比較小的目標開始，再慢慢增加。

**¶6** Walking may seem simple, but it offers real health benefits for your heart, body, and mind. You don't need to run, sweat heavily, or hit a 10,000-step goal, because even daily walks add up to big improvements in your well-being.
> 走路看似簡單，卻能為你的心臟、身體和心靈帶來真正的健康益處。你不需要跑步、大量流汗，或達成一萬步的目標，因為即使是每天的散步，累積起來也能大幅改善你的身心健康。

## 四、Language Notes

1. **a large number of + 複數 N**：許多的……（the number of + N + 單數 V＝……的數量）
2. **how + Adj./Adv. + S + V**：名詞子句「……有多……」，直述句語序；課文作介系詞 of 的受詞
3. **keep + O + O.C.**：使保持……（O.C.＝Adj.／V-ing／p.p.／介片）
4. **It is thought that S + V ＝ S is thought to V**：一般認為……（believe／say／report／expect 同型）
5. **build up**：逐漸增加；累積
6. **add up to**：總計達到；累積導致

Info Box：count as（算作）／it doesn't count（不算數）／in reality（實際上）／focus on（專注於）／the key to N/V-ing（……的關鍵）／make the most of（充分利用）／prefer to V（較喜歡）
