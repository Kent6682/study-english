# Unit 3｜Going on a City Tour 參加城市導覽

> 主題式會話・旅遊觀光｜難度 ★☆☆
> 四個場景：預訂導覽 → 行程介紹 → 與團隊走散 → 討論用餐

## 一、四格大意（起承轉合）

| 階段 | 場景 | 重點句 |
|---|---|---|
| 起 · 預訂導覽 | 山姆到旅行社詢問方案，選了每天早上 9 點出發、每人 45 美元的半日導覽 | `I'm interested in booking a city tour.` |
| 承 · 行程介紹 | 導遊介紹三大景點，提醒大家跟緊隊伍、看好隨身物品、尊重古蹟，並建議帶水 | `stay with the group and watch your belongings` |
| 轉 · 與團走散 | 山姆在上一站後走散，向當地人問路；對方說走約 15 分鐘就能跟上 | `I got separated from my group.` |
| 合 · 用餐選擇 | 導遊推薦開業 60 多年的麵店：價格實惠、口味道地，當地人與觀光客都愛 | `That sounds affordable. Is it authentic?` |

## 二、課文與翻譯

### 1 Booking a City Tour（山姆走進一間旅行社。）
| | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| S | Hi, I'm interested in booking a city tour. Could you tell me what options you have? | 你好，我想預訂城市導覽。請問有哪些方案可以選擇？ |
| T | Of course! We offer a half-day city tour and a full-day city tour. | 當然！我們有半日城市導覽和全日城市導覽。 |
| S | What's included in the half-day tour? | 半日導覽包含哪些行程？ |
| T | It includes a walking tour of the historic old city area, the Independence **Monument**, and lunch at a local market. | 包括步行導覽歷史悠久的舊城區、參觀獨立紀念碑，以及在當地市場吃午餐。 |
| S | What time does it start? | 幾點出發？ |
| T | The **departure** time is every morning at 9 a.m. | 每天早上九點出發。 |
| S | That's great, and how much does it cost? | 太好了，那費用是多少？ |
| T | The tour costs $45 per person. | 導覽費用每人 45 美元。 |
| S | Great. Then I'd like to book it for tomorrow. | 好，那我想預訂明天的行程。 |

### 2 Introducing an Itinerary（導遊說明行程。）
| | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| G | Good morning, everyone! Welcome to the city tour. Today we'll visit three main attractions: the Old Town, the Independence Monument, and the Central Market. | 大家早安！歡迎參加城市導覽。今天我們會參觀三個主要景點：舊城區、獨立紀念碑和中央市場。 |
| S | Is there anything we should be careful about? | 有什麼需要特別注意的嗎？ |
| G | Yes. Please remember to stay with the group and watch your belongings, especially in crowded areas like the market. Also, be respectful at historical **sites**—don't touch artifacts or monuments. | 有。請記得跟緊隊伍，並留意隨身物品，尤其是在市場等人潮擁擠的地方。另外，在歷史古蹟要保持尊重——不要觸碰文物或紀念碑。 |
| S | Before we leave, is there anything we should bring? | 出發前，有什麼東西是我們該帶的嗎？ |
| G | The weather's looking quite hot this morning, so it would be a good idea to bring some water. | 今天早上天氣看起來滿熱的，所以最好帶點水。 |
| S | Got it. Thanks for the advice! | 了解。謝謝你的建議！ |

### 3 Getting Separated from the Group（山姆向一位當地人問路。）
| | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| S | Excuse me, could you help me? I was on a city tour, but I got separated from my group after our last stop. | 不好意思，可以幫我一下嗎？我在參加城市導覽，但在上一站之後和團隊走散了。 |
| L | Do you know where your group was heading next? | 你知道你的團接下來要去哪裡嗎？ |
| S | The guide said we were going to the Central Market. | 導遊說我們要去中央市場。 |
| L | Don't worry. That's not far from here; you'll easily catch up. | 別擔心。那裡離這裡不遠，你很容易就能跟上。 |
| S | That's a relief. Can I walk there? | 那我就放心了。我可以走過去嗎？ |
| L | Definitely. It will take **roughly** 15 minutes. Follow this road on the left and look out for signs for the market. | 當然可以。大約要走 15 分鐘。沿著左邊這條路走，留意通往市場的指標。 |
| S | Great, I'm very **grateful** for your help. | 太好了，非常感謝你的幫忙。 |
| L | No problem. Good luck finding your group! | 不客氣。祝你順利找到你的團！ |

### 4 Talking About Dining Options（導遊推薦一些美食。）
| | English | 中文 |
|---|---|---|
| G | Before we finish the tour, it's time for lunch. We'll be having lunch at my favorite spot, a famous noodle shop just around the corner. It's been in business for more than 60 years. | 在結束導覽之前，該吃午餐了。我們會在我最愛的地方吃午餐，那是一家就在轉角的知名麵店。它已經營業超過 60 年了。 |
| S | What kind of noodles does the shop serve? | 那家店賣的是哪種麵？ |
| G | Mostly traditional noodles with beef or seafood. You can try either soup noodles or fried noodles. | 大多是加牛肉或海鮮的傳統麵食。你可以選湯麵或炒麵。 |
| S | Is it expensive? | 會很貴嗎？ |
| G | Not really. A bowl of noodles usually costs around $5 to $8. | 不太貴。一碗麵通常大約 5 到 8 美元。 |
| S | That sounds **affordable**. Is it **authentic**? | 聽起來價格很實惠。那是道地的口味嗎？ |
| G | Yes! Their food is very popular with both locals and tourists, so I highly recommend trying it. | 是的！他們的料理很受當地人和觀光客歡迎，所以我非常推薦大家去吃吃看。 |

## 四、必考句型

1. **be interested in + V-ing**：I'm interested in booking a city tour.
2. **間接問句（直述語序）**：Could you tell me what options you have? ／ Do you know where your group was heading next?
3. **be included in**（被動）：What's included in the half-day tour?
4. **remember to V**：Please remember to stay with the group.
5. **it would be a good idea to V**：It would be a good idea to bring some water.
6. **get separated from**：I got separated from my group after our last stop.
7. **catch up (with)**：You'll easily catch up.
8. **It takes + 時間**：It will take roughly 15 minutes.
9. **look out for + N**：look out for signs for the market
10. **be grateful for**：I'm very grateful for your help.
11. **現在完成式 + for**：It's been in business for more than 60 years.
12. **either A or B**：You can try either soup noodles or fried noodles.
13. **sound + adj.**：That sounds affordable.
14. **recommend + V-ing**：I highly recommend trying it.

## 五、問路與旅遊實用句

- Excuse me, how do I get to + 地名？ 不好意思，我要怎麼去……？
- Can you tell me the way to + 地名？ 你可以告訴我去……的路嗎？
- What is the best way to get to + 地名？ 請問去……最好的方式是什麼？
- 導覽類型：walking／bus／food／night／historical／cultural tour

## 六、易錯提醒

- ✗ Could you tell me what options do you have? ➔ ✓ what options you have
- ✗ I'm interested in book ➔ ✓ interested in booking
- ✗ I recommend to try it ➔ ✓ recommend trying it
- ✗ That sounds well ➔ ✓ sounds good／affordable
- ✗ It's in business since 60 years ➔ ✓ It's been in business for 60 years
- ✗ belonging（指財物時）➔ ✓ belongings（複數）
