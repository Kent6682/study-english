UNIT 3｜主題式會話｜Going on a City Tour 參加城市導覽
當你到另一個城市旅行時，你知道如何用英文預訂導覽行程、詢問行程內容、在突發狀況下尋求協助，以及討論用餐選擇嗎？

==================================================
【主題句型】
==================================================
預訂導覽行程：Hi, I'm interested in booking a city tour. Could you tell me what options you have?
  你好，我想預訂城市導覽。請問有哪些方案可以選擇？
  ➔ options [ˋɑpʃənz] 指「選擇、選項、可選擇的事物」。
  常見的城市導覽類型：
    · walking tour 步行導覽　　· bus tour 巴士導覽
    · food tour 美食導覽　　　 · night tour 夜間導覽
    · historical tour 歷史導覽 · cultural tour 文化導覽

行程介紹：Please remember to stay with the group and watch your belongings, especially in crowded areas like the market.
  請記得跟緊隊伍，並留意隨身物品，尤其是在市場等人潮擁擠的地方。
  ➔ belongings [bɪˋlɔŋɪŋz] 指「所有物；財物」，常用複數形。

與團隊走散：I was on a city tour, but I got separated from my group after our last stop.
  我在參加城市導覽，但在上一站與團隊走散了。
  問路時，這些句型很實用：
    · Excuse me, how do I get to + 地名？　　不好意思，我要怎麼去……？
    · Can you tell me the way to + 地名？　　你可以告訴我去……的路嗎？
    · What is the best way to get to + 地名？請問去……最好的方式是什麼？

討論用餐選擇：What kind of noodles does the shop serve?
  那家店賣的是哪種麵？

==================================================
【課文】
==================================================
1 Booking a City Tour
Sam enters a tourist agency office.
S = Sam　T = Tourist agent
L01 S: Hi, I'm interested in booking a city tour. Could you tell me what options you have?
L02 T: Of course! We offer a half-day city tour and a full-day city tour.
L03 S: What's included in the half-day tour?
L04 T: It includes a walking tour of the historic old city area, the Independence Monument, and lunch at a local market.
L05 S: What time does it start?
L06 T: The departure time is every morning at 9 a.m.
L07 S: That's great, and how much does it cost?
L08 T: The tour costs $45 per person.
L09 S: Great. Then I'd like to book it for tomorrow.

2 Introducing an Itinerary
The tour guide explains the itinerary.
G = Guide　S = Sam
L10 G: Good morning, everyone! Welcome to the city tour. Today we'll visit three main attractions: the Old Town, the Independence Monument, and the Central Market.
L11 S: Is there anything we should be careful about?
L12 G: Yes. Please remember to stay with the group and watch your belongings, especially in crowded areas like the market. Also, be respectful at historical sites—don't touch artifacts or monuments.
L13 S: Before we leave, is there anything we should bring?
L14 G: The weather's looking quite hot this morning, so it would be a good idea to bring some water.
L15 S: Got it. Thanks for the advice!

3 Getting Separated from the Group
Sam asks a local for help with directions.
S = Sam　L = Local
L16 S: Excuse me, could you help me? I was on a city tour, but I got separated from my group after our last stop.
L17 L: Do you know where your group was heading next?
L18 S: The guide said we were going to the Central Market.
L19 L: Don't worry. That's not far from here; you'll easily catch up.
L20 S: That's a relief. Can I walk there?
L21 L: Definitely. It will take roughly 15 minutes. Follow this road on the left and look out for signs for the market.
L22 S: Great, I'm very grateful for your help.
L23 L: No problem. Good luck finding your group!

4 Talking About Dining Options
The tour guide gives some food recommendations.
S = Sam　G = Guide
L24 G: Before we finish the tour, it's time for lunch. We'll be having lunch at my favorite spot, a famous noodle shop just around the corner. It's been in business for more than 60 years.
L25 S: What kind of noodles does the shop serve?
L26 G: Mostly traditional noodles with beef or seafood. You can try either soup noodles or fried noodles.
L27 S: Is it expensive?
L28 G: Not really. A bowl of noodles usually costs around $5 to $8.
L29 S: That sounds affordable. Is it authentic?
L30 G: Yes! Their food is very popular with both locals and tourists, so I highly recommend trying it.

【Vocabulary 字彙】（* 為大考出現過的字彙）
==================================================
*1. agency [ˋedʒənsi] n. 代理機構；（政府）行政機關、局、處、署
    補 agent [ˋedʒənt] n. 代辦人、代理人、經紀人（L01 情境）
    例：The job agency helped my brother find work.
*2. monument [ˋmɑnjəmənt] n. 紀念碑；（具歷史或文化價值的）古蹟、歷史遺跡
    例：Scarlett visited a war monument on Sunday.
*3. departure [dɪˋpɑrtʃɚ] n. 出發、啟程；離開、離境
    例：The plane's late departure was because of fog.
*4. site [saɪt] n. 地點；場所；（特殊用途的）場地；網站（同 website）
    例：Many popular sites are closed on Mondays.
*5. roughly [ˋrʌflɪ] adv. 粗略地；大致上；約略地
    例：Gavin's house is roughly three kilometers from here.
*6. grateful [ˋgretfəl] adj. 表示感謝的；感激的
    例：Paige is grateful to the doctor for his care.
*7. affordable [əˋfɔrdəbl̩] adj. （價格）負擔得起的；價格實惠的
    例：Daniel found an affordable hotel near the station.
*8. authentic [ɔˋθɛntɪk] adj. 道地的、正宗的；真正的、真實的
    例：The band played authentic folk music at the concert.

字彙小筆記（僅供參考）
  · itinerary [aɪˋtɪnə͵rɛrɪ] n. 行程安排；旅行計畫（尤指詳細行程表）
  · artifact [ˋɑrtɪ͵fækt] n. 文物；人工製品

【Info Box 關鍵解析】
==================================================
1. it would be a good idea to V（L14）
   常用於口語提出建議，語氣較委婉，表示「最好做……、做……會比較好」。
2. got it（L15）
   常見於口語，表示「我明白了；我收到了」，用於快速回應、確認理解或訊息接收。
3. catch up（L19）
   片語動詞 catch up 在此作不及物用，表示「跟上、趕上（進度、他人等）」，之後可接 with sb/sth。
4. look out for N.（L21）
   在此指「留意並尋找……；密切注意（某人或某事）」。
5. in business（L24）
   指「（公司、商店等）在營業、在經營中」。
