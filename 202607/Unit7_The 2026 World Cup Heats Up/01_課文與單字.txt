【課文】
==================================================

1 Discussing the World Cup
Tim and Vanessa are talking about the 2026 World Cup.
T: Have you been following the World Cup news?
V: Yeah! There's so much tension and excitement. This one is really different from the previous tournaments.
T: Exactly. For starters, it's being hosted by three countries together—it's the first time in World Cup history!
V: Yeah, and aren't there 48 teams competing instead of the usual 32?
T: Right! More teams mean more matches and more chances for different countries to play. It's interesting to see unexpected teams shine and have their moment too.
V: Totally. I'm ready to keep watching and see how the rest of the matches play out.

2 Comparing Favorite Teams
Vanessa and Tim are discussing the teams they support.
V: So, which team are you supporting in the competition?
T: I always root for Argentina. I love their style of play, and my favorite player, Martínez, is amazing. Plus, Argentina has such a strong track record. What about you?
V: I'm cheering for France. The team has had lots of success on the international stage recently, so I want to see them win again.
T: They've done well in previous World Cups; let's see if they keep it up.
V: There's lots of tough competition, though. Brazil and Croatia usually do very well.
T: You're right, but either way I will keep supporting my favorite.
V: Definitely! I don't want to miss a single minute of the games.

【字彙 Vocabulary（＊為大考出現過的字彙）】
==================================================
1. tension [ˋtɛnʃən] n. 緊張；緊張關係、局勢
   The tension before the game was clear.
2. previous [ˋprivɪəs] adj. 之前的；早先的
   This winter is warmer than previous years.
3. competition [͵kɑmpəˋtɪʃən] n. 比賽、競賽；競爭（補 compete [kəmˋpit] v. 競爭；參加比賽）
   Alicia is preparing for a running competition.
4. amazing [əˋmezɪŋ] adj. 極好的、非常出色的；驚人的
   We had an amazing time on our vacation.
5. tournament [ˋtʊrnəmənt] n. 錦標賽（字彙小筆記）

【Info Box 關鍵解析】
==================================================
· heat up：加熱、變暖；在標題中作不及物動詞，引申指情況、競爭、活動等越來越激烈。
· for starters：首先、第一點，用來引出所要論述的第一個重點，常置於句首。
· shine：原指閃耀、發光，在此引申為表現出色、大放異彩。
· root for N.：為……加油打氣、支持；root 在此義同 cheer，見 I'm cheering for France.。
· plus：加上、而且、此外，常用於口語或非正式文章，用來補充說明前述內容。
· track record：過去的表現紀錄或戰績。
