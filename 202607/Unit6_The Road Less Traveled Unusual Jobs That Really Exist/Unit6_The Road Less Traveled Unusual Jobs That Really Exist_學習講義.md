## 二、課文（中英對照）

**¶1** Many people think of a job as sitting in an office, doing research in a laboratory, or working in a store. But have you ever dreamed of a job that doesn't fit such molds? Well, if you're looking for a job that's far from ordinary, you might consider one of these unexpected paths.
> 很多人想到工作，腦中浮現的不外乎是坐辦公室、在實驗室做研究，或是在商店上班。但你是否曾夢想過一份不符合這些既定框架的工作呢？嗯，如果你正在找一份一點也不平凡的工作，或許可以考慮以下這幾條出人意料的路。

**¶2｜Netflix Tagger 標籤員** If you enjoy watching movies all day or finishing a whole season of the latest TV show in one sitting, this might be your perfect career. A Netflix tagger watches hours of content and assigns specific keywords or “tags” to each show. These tags help the site's algorithm recommend new movies and shows to other viewers. However, because such positions are rare, they are highly sought after by those who know about them.
> 如果你喜歡整天看電影，或是一口氣追完最新影集的一整季，這也許就是你的完美職業。Netflix 標籤員要看好幾個小時的內容，並替每部節目指定明確的關鍵字或「標籤」。這些標籤能幫助網站的演算法向其他觀眾推薦新的電影和節目。不過，由於這類職位十分稀少，它們在知情的人之間非常搶手。

**¶3｜Snake Milker 取蛇毒員** This job is definitely not for everyone. A snake milker takes venom from some of the world's most dangerous species of snakes. Using special tools, they carefully handle the snake and encourage it to bite onto a collection container so the venom can be safely gathered. This venom is extremely valuable because scientists use it to create life-saving medicines called antivenom. While it is a risky career, these professionals help save thousands of lives every year.
> 這份工作絕對不是人人都做得來。取蛇毒員要從世界上一些最危險的蛇種身上取得毒液。他們使用特殊工具，小心翼翼地抓住蛇，引導牠咬住收集容器，好讓毒液能安全地被收集起來。這種毒液極為珍貴，因為科學家用它來製造名為抗蛇毒血清的救命藥物。雖然這是一份高風險的職業，這些專業人員每年卻幫助拯救數千條人命。

**¶4｜Bicycle Fisher 自行車打撈員** In Amsterdam, thousands of people ride bikes every day, and many of those bikes end up at the bottom of the city's numerous canals. Bicycle fishers use a special boat with a large claw to pull the rusty bikes out of the water. This keeps the Dutch capital's famous waterways cleaner and safer for boat traffic.
> 在阿姆斯特丹，每天有成千上萬的人騎自行車，而其中許多自行車最後落得沉在這座城市眾多運河的河底。自行車打撈員使用裝有巨大機械爪的特殊船隻，把生鏽的自行車從水裡撈出來。這讓這座荷蘭首都著名的水道更乾淨，船隻通行也更安全。

**¶5｜Pet Food Tester 寵物食品試吃員** Do you like trying new foods? What if that food isn't intended for humans? Pet food testers are often food scientists who help ensure that pet food is both safe and appealing for animals. They usually don't swallow the food, but will still taste small amounts. Their work also involves checking ingredients and comparing flavors to improve recipes.
> 你喜歡嘗試新食物嗎？萬一那種食物不是給人吃的呢？寵物食品試吃員通常是食品科學家，他們協助確保寵物食品既安全又能吸引動物。他們通常不會把食物吞下去，但仍會嚐一點點。他們的工作還包括檢查成分和比較風味，以改良配方。

**¶6｜Professional Cuddler 專業擁抱者** This job helps people dealing with loneliness or stress. Professional cuddlers provide non-romantic physical comfort through hugging sessions. Several studies have shown that physical touch reduces stress and anxiety. Naturally, cuddlers must have strong communication skills and be willing to set clear boundaries to create safe experiences for clients and themselves.
> 這份工作幫助飽受孤獨或壓力所苦的人。專業擁抱者透過擁抱療程，提供非戀愛性質的肢體安慰。多項研究顯示，肢體接觸能減輕壓力和焦慮。當然，專業擁抱者必須具備良好的溝通能力，並願意設定清楚的界限，為客戶和自己創造安全的體驗。

**¶7** As you can see, the world is full of surprising career choices. Whether it's milking snakes or fishing for bikes, these roles prove that there are far more ways to make a living than we might imagine.
> 如你所見，這世界充滿令人驚喜的職業選擇。無論是取蛇毒還是撈自行車，這些職業都證明，謀生的方式遠比我們想像的多得多。

## 四、Language Notes

```
【1】think of ... as ... 認為……是……
  think of＋O＋as＋N./V-ing/Adj. 表示「認為……是……；把……當成……」。
  文中：Many people think of a job as sitting in an office ...
  例：Sandra thinks of learning English as an exciting challenge.（珊卓認為學習英文是一項令人興奮的挑戰。）

【2】far from 一點也不……、遠非……
  be far from 用來強調語氣，後面可接形容詞（文中 far from ordinary）、名詞或動名詞。
  例：The plan is far from perfect, but it is a good start.（這個計畫一點也不完美，但它是一個好的開始。）
  例：I think we're still far from being ready for the competition.（我認為我們離準備好參加比賽還差得很遠。）

【3】be sought after 很搶手；很熱門
  seek–sought–sought 原意「尋找、尋求」，be sought after 以被動形式表示「很搶手、廣受歡迎」；加 highly 表「炙手可熱」。
  例：This university is highly sought after by international students.（這所大學深受國際學生青睞。）

【4】Word Focus 字尾 -er：做……的人；從事……職業者
  tag＋-er＝tagger [ˋtægɚ] 標註員（重複字尾 g）／view＋-er＝viewer [ˋvjuɚ] 觀眾／milk＋-er＝snake milker [snek] [ˋmɪlkɚ] 取蛇毒員／cuddle＋-er＝cuddler [ˋkʌdlɚ] 擁抱者（字尾去 e）

【5】What if＋S.＋V.? 萬一……怎麼辦？
  詢問若發生某情況時該如何應對，可用直說法，或用假設語氣表示與現在事實相反的情況。
  例：What if I decide to stay at home instead?（如果我決定待在家裡會怎麼樣？）
  例：What if James missed the last train home?（要是詹姆士錯過了回家的最後一班火車該怎麼辦？）

【6】Whether A or B, S.＋V. 無論……或……；不管……或……
  引導讓步副詞子句，引出兩種以上的選擇或可能性；連接子句時相同的部分可省略，如本句 or 後省略了 it's。
  例：Whether he tells the truth or he hides it, people will eventually find out.（不管他說實話還是隱瞞，大家終究會發現。）
  例：Whether summer or winter, Julie goes jogging every week.（無論是夏天或冬天，茱莉每週都會去慢跑。）
  ⚠️ milk 作動詞原指「擠乳」，在此指「提取（毒液）」；fish for 原指「捕魚、撈魚」，在此指「搜尋、撈取」。

【7】make a living 謀生、維持生計
  後面可接 as＋職業，表示「以……謀生」。
  例：Samuel hopes to make a living as an artist someday.（山繆希望有一天能以藝術家的身分謀生。）
```
