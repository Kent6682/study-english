================================================================
一、閱讀技巧：Making Connections（建立文本連結）
================================================================
Getting the most out of reading requires more than just an understanding of what has been written down. As readers, we should use our own knowledge, feelings, and experiences to enrich our appreciation of texts. To do this, we need to make connections.

1. A text-to-self connection is when something in a text reminds a reader of something from their own life.
2. A text-to-text connection is when something in a text reminds a reader about another text.
3. A text-to-world connection is when something in a text reminds a reader of a real-world event, such as a news story.

Practice Time
(1) Read the following paragraph. Underline the sentence with a reading connection and say which of the three it is.
The Hobbit is a book by English author J. R. R. Tolkien. It first came out in 1937, but I only read it last year. To me, it was similar to many of the fairy stories I used to read. I enjoyed it, and I'm looking forward to reading other books like it.

(2) Which type of text connection is used in each of the following sentences?
A. Both this story and the book To Kill a Mockingbird have a strong female main character.
B. The recent news story about an earthquake reminds me of when I visited Japan and I felt the floor shaking when I was in my hotel room.
C. The treatment of the main character made me think of the way black people were treated in the US in the 1950s.

二、課文原文
================================================================

Read the following article. Consider how to make the different types of connection.

¶1（L1–11）
Iceland is a land where ice and fire exist side by side. Huge glaciers spread across the mountains, shining white under the pale sky. Beneath them, the earth burns with hidden heat. Volcanoes are all around, and lava pours from them like bright red rivers. Steam drifts into the cold air, and the ground feels alive.

¶2（L12–18）
This is a wild place. Long roads cross empty spaces—driving along them, you may not see another house for miles. Wind moves across dark open fields, and the sea beats against rough cliffs. Nature is powerful here, and it takes a lot to overcome it.

¶3（L19–35）
Not much grows in this harsh landscape, so people have often had to be clever with their food. In the past, they preserved what they could to survive long winters. One famous dish that remains is fermented shark. It has an unusual, sharp smell that surprises many visitors. Most travelers find it unpleasant, but the locals have strong stomachs. Truly, ice and fire have not only shaped the land but also the spirit of the people who call it home.

課文旁註（示範閱讀連結）
‧ 標題：The title may remind readers of the book series A Song of Ice and Fire. It makes us think of the wild, exciting landscapes in the books.
‧ Volcanoes：You may think of volcanic activity that often appears in the news.
‧ fermented shark：Taiwanese readers may be reminded of the strong smell and taste of stinky tofu. Many Taiwanese like it, while many foreigners don't, just like the fermented shark.

三、Vocabulary（＊為大考出現過的字彙）
================================================================
＊1. drift [drɪft] v. 飄、隨風移動；漂流
     Light fog drifts above the lake in the morning.
＊2. cliff [klɪf] n.（常指海邊的）峭壁、懸崖
     Please stay away from the edge of the cliffs.
＊3. overcome [͵ovɚˋkʌm] v. 克服；戰勝（動詞三態為 overcome-overcame-overcome）
     Colin had to overcome strong waves to reach the shore.
＊4. harsh [hɑrʃ] adj. 嚴酷的；惡劣的
     Few animals can live in harsh desert climates.
＊5. preserve [prɪˋzɝv] v. 保存（食物）、保鮮；維護
     Lucy preserved fruit in glass jars.

字彙小筆記：僅供參考
‧ glacier [ˋgleʃɚ] n. 冰河、冰川
‧ volcano [vɑlˋkeno] n. 火山（複數形為 volcanoes 或 volcanos）
‧ lava [ˋlɑvə] n. 熔岩
‧ fermented [fɚˋmɛntɪd] adj. 發酵的

================================================================
四、Word Focus：字首 un-（表示「不；非；無」等否定含義）
================================================================
un- + usual（通常的）= unusual [ʌnˋjuʒʊəl] 不尋常的；獨特的（第 25 行）
un- + pleasant（令人愉快的）= unpleasant [ʌnˋplɛznt] 令人不快、不舒服的（第 30 行）
