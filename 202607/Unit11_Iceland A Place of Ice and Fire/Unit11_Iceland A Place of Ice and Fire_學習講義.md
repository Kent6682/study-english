## 三、課文（中英對照）

**¶1** Iceland is a land where ice and fire exist side by side. Huge glaciers spread across the mountains, shining white under the pale sky. Beneath them, the earth burns with hidden heat. Volcanoes are all around, and lava pours from them like bright red rivers. Steam drifts into the cold air, and the ground feels alive.
> 冰島是一片冰與火並存的土地。巨大的冰河綿延在群山之間，在淡色的天空下閃著白光。在冰河底下，大地正燃燒著潛藏的熱力。四周都是火山，熔岩像一條條鮮紅的河流，從火山傾瀉而出。蒸氣飄進冷冽的空氣中，大地彷彿活了過來。

**¶2** This is a wild place. Long roads cross empty spaces—driving along them, you may not see another house for miles. Wind moves across dark open fields, and the sea beats against rough cliffs. Nature is powerful here, and it takes a lot to overcome it.
> 這是一片荒野之地。漫長的公路穿越空曠的大地：沿著這些路開車，你可能好幾英里都看不到另一棟房子。風吹過幽暗開闊的原野，海浪拍打著嶙峋的懸崖。這裡的大自然力量強大，要克服它並不容易。

**¶3** Not much grows in this harsh landscape, so people have often had to be clever with their food. In the past, they preserved **what they could** to survive long winters. One famous dish that remains is fermented shark. It has an unusual, sharp smell that surprises many visitors. Most travelers **find it unpleasant**, but the locals have strong stomachs. Truly, ice and fire have not only shaped the land but also the spirit of the people who call it home.
> 這片嚴酷的土地上長不出多少東西，所以人們常常得在食物上動腦筋。過去，他們為了熬過漫長的冬天，能保存的食物都保存下來。流傳至今的一道名菜是發酵鯊魚。它有一股不尋常的刺鼻氣味，讓許多遊客大吃一驚。大多數旅人都覺得它令人難以消受，但當地人的胃可是強得很。的確，冰與火不僅塑造了這片土地，也塑造了以此為家的人們的精神。

## 四、Language Notes

1. **複合關代 what**：what ＝ the thing(s) that，引導名詞子句，作主詞時視為單數。
   - *[What Hayden said] was not true.* 海登說的不是真的。
   - 課文 *preserved what they could*：what 子句作受詞，could 後省略 preserve。
2. **find + O. + O.C.**：補語可為分詞、形容詞或介系詞片語。
   - *Tina found her cat sleeping on her pillow.*
   - *Samuel found the dish burned in the kitchen.*
   - *We found the room clean and bright.*
   - *Keira found her grandfather in poor health.*
3. **Info Box**：shine + 顏色形容詞；be clever with sth（巧妙利用）；a strong stomach（胃強；承受力強）。
4. **Word Focus**：字首 un-：unusual（不尋常的）、unpleasant（令人不快的）。
