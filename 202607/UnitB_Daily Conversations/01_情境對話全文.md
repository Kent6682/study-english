## Scene 1｜體育課：健走的學問 PE Class: Walking for Health

📍 體育課上，教練要大家健走三十分鐘，順便說明走路的好處。

本場單字：moderate, research, measure, pace, intensity, calorie, slightly, strengthen, flexible, consistently

**Coach**：Today we're walking for thirty minutes. Keep a **moderate** speed—not too slow, not too fast.  
　　今天我們要走三十分鐘，保持適中的速度：不要太慢，也不要太快。

**Jason**：Walking? That's too easy. Does it really count as exercise?  
　　走路？太簡單了吧。這真的算運動嗎？

**Coach**：It does. **Research** shows that brisk walking is good for your heart.  
　　算。研究顯示快走對心臟很好。

**Lily**：How do we know if we're walking fast enough?  
　　我們要怎麼知道自己走得夠不夠快？

**Coach**：A simple **measure** is the talk test. You should be able to talk, but not sing.  
　　有個簡單的衡量標準叫「說話測試」：你應該能說話，但沒辦法唱歌。

**Jason**：Ha! So if I can sing, I need to pick up the **pace**.  
　　哈！所以如果我還能唱歌，就要加快腳步。

**Coach**：Exactly. That's the right **intensity** for most people.  
　　沒錯，這對大多數人來說是適當的強度。

**Lily**：How many **calories** can we burn in thirty minutes?  
　　三十分鐘可以燃燒多少卡路里？

**Coach**：About one hundred fifty, depending on your weight.  
　　大約一百五十卡，看你的體重而定。

**Jason**：That's only **slightly** more than a bubble tea!  
　　那只比一杯珍奶多一點點耶！

**Coach**：True, but walking also helps **strengthen** your legs and bones.  
　　沒錯，但走路也能幫助強化你的腿和骨頭。

**Lily**：And it's **flexible**. You can do it anytime, anywhere.  
　　而且很有彈性，隨時隨地都能做。

**Coach**：Right. The key is to walk **consistently**—a little every day is better than a lot once a week.  
　　對。關鍵是要規律地走：每天走一點，比一週走一次走很多更好。

**Jason**：OK, Coach. Starting tomorrow, I'll walk to school.  
　　好的，教練。從明天開始，我要走路上學。

### 實用口語句型

- **count as ...**　算是……
- **pick up the pace**　加快腳步
- **depending on ...**　看……而定
- **Starting tomorrow, I'll ...**　從明天開始，我要……

## Scene 2｜報名城市導覽 Booking a City Tour

📍 莉莉和傑森要跟學校出國交流，自由活動那天想報名一日城市導覽，先到旅行社詢問。

本場單字：agency, monument, departure, site, roughly, authentic, affordable, exchange, grateful

**Clerk**：Welcome to Sunny Travel **Agency**. How can I help you?  
　　歡迎光臨陽光旅行社，有什麼可以幫您的嗎？

**Lily**：We're going abroad with our school, and we have one free day. Do you have any city tours?  
　　我們要跟學校出國，有一天自由活動。你們有城市導覽行程嗎？

**Clerk**：Yes. This one-day tour visits the old town and a famous war **monument**.  
　　有。這個一日遊會去老城區和一座著名的戰爭紀念碑。

**Jason**：What time is the **departure**?  
　　幾點出發？

**Clerk**：The bus leaves the hotel at eight and stops at five historic **sites**.  
　　巴士八點從飯店出發，會停五個歷史景點。

**Lily**：How long does the whole tour take?  
　　整個行程要多久？

**Clerk**：**Roughly** seven hours, including lunch.  
　　大約七小時，含午餐。

**Jason**：Is lunch at a tourist restaurant? I want to try **authentic** local food.  
　　午餐是在觀光餐廳嗎？我想吃道地的當地美食。

**Clerk**：Don't worry. It's a small family restaurant that locals love.  
　　別擔心，是一間當地人很愛的家庭小餐館。

　　聽起來很完美。多少錢？

　　每人四十美元，這是我們最實惠的行程之一。

**Jason**：Great. Should we **exchange** money before we go?  
　　太好了。我們出發前要先換錢嗎？

**Clerk**：Yes, but only a little. Most shops there accept credit cards.  
　　要，不過換一點就好，那裡大部分商店都能刷卡。

**Lily**：Thank you so much. We're really **grateful** for your help.  
　　非常謝謝您，真的很感謝您的幫忙。

## Scene 3｜圖書館聊奇幻小說 A Fantasy Book in the Library

📍 午休時間，莉莉在圖書館讀一本關於精靈的奇幻小說，傑森好奇地湊過來。

本場單字：fantasy, creation, invisible, bless, disturb, crop, cruel, drown, behavior, adopt

**Jason**：What are you reading? You haven't looked up for twenty minutes.  
　　你在看什麼？你已經二十分鐘沒抬頭了。

**Lily**：Sorry, I didn't mean to ignore you. It's a **fantasy** novel about elves.  
　　抱歉，我不是故意不理你。這是一本關於精靈的奇幻小說。

**Jason**：Elves? Like the tall, beautiful archers in movies?  
　　精靈？就像電影裡那些又高又美的弓箭手？

**Lily**：That's a modern **creation**. In old stories, elves were very different.  
　　那是現代的創作。在古老的故事裡，精靈很不一樣。

**Jason**：Different how?  
　　怎麼個不一樣？

**Lily**：Some were tiny and **invisible**. People believed elves could **bless** a village with good luck.  
　　有些很小，而且看不見。人們相信精靈能為村莊帶來好運。

**Jason**：So they were the good guys?  
　　所以他們是好人？

**Lily**：Not always. If you **disturbed** them, your **crops** might fail.  
　　不一定。如果你打擾了他們，你的農作物可能會歉收。

**Jason**：Wow. That sounds a bit **cruel**.  
　　哇，聽起來有點殘忍。

**Lily**：In some stories, they even tried to **drown** travelers in rivers.  
　　在某些故事裡，他們甚至會想把旅人淹死在河裡。

**Jason**：Scary! Why is their **behavior** so different in each story?  
　　好可怕！為什麼每個故事裡他們的行為差這麼多？

**Lily**：Each culture **adopted** the idea of elves and changed it to fit its own beliefs.  
　　每個文化都採用了精靈的概念，再依自己的信仰改編。

**Jason**：Interesting. Can I borrow the book after you finish?  
　　真有趣。你看完可以借我嗎？

**Lily**：Sure, but you'll have to wait. I'm only on chapter three!  
　　可以，不過你得等一下，我才看到第三章！

### 實用口語句型

- **I didn't mean to ...**　我不是故意要……
- **Different how?**　怎麼個不一樣？（口語追問）
- **the good guys**　好人、正派角色
- **I'm only on chapter three.**　我才看到第三章

## Scene 5｜生涯講座：奇特職業 Career Day: Unusual Jobs

📍 學校生涯講座，一位講者分享幾種真實存在的奇特職業。

本場單字：laboratory, assign, specific, collection, numerous, claw, rusty, container, appealing, anxiety

**Speaker**：Good morning! I used to work in a **laboratory** that studies snake venom.  
　　早安！我以前在一間研究蛇毒的實驗室工作。

**Lily**：Snake venom? Wasn't that dangerous?  
　　蛇毒？那不危險嗎？

**Speaker**：Very. Each worker was **assigned** one **specific** type of snake to take care of.  
　　非常危險。每位員工都被分配照顧一種特定的蛇。

**Jason**：What did you do with the venom?  
　　你們拿蛇毒做什麼？

**Speaker**：The **collection** of venom is the first step in making medicine for snake bites.  
　　收集蛇毒是製作蛇咬解毒藥的第一步。

**Jason**：Do you know any other strange jobs?  
　　你還知道其他奇怪的工作嗎？

**Speaker**：**Numerous** ones! In Amsterdam, some people fish bikes out of the canals.  
　　多得是！在阿姆斯特丹，有人專門從運河裡把腳踏車撈上來。

**Lily**：How do they do that?  
　　他們怎麼撈？

**Speaker**：They use a boat with a giant **claw**. Most of the bikes are old and **rusty**.  
　　他們用裝有巨大機械爪的船，撈上來的腳踏車大多又舊又生鏽。

**Jason**：Then what? Do they throw them away?  
　　然後呢？丟掉嗎？

**Speaker**：No, they put them into a big **container** and send them to be recycled.  
　　不，他們把車放進大貨櫃，送去回收。

**Lily**：These jobs are cool, but are they **appealing** to young people?  
　　這些工作很酷，但對年輕人有吸引力嗎？

**Speaker**：More than you think! Some jobs even help people with stress or **anxiety**, like professional huggers.  
　　比你想的更有吸引力！有些工作甚至能幫助壓力大或焦慮的人，例如專業擁抱師。

**Jason**：A professional hugger? I'd be great at that job!  
　　專業擁抱師？我一定很適合這份工作！

### 實用口語句型

- **You've probably never heard of ...**　你可能從沒聽過……
- **fish ... out of ...**　從……撈出……
- **More than you think!**　比你想的還多！
- **I'd be great at that job!**　我一定很適合這份工作！

## Scene 10｜動物園看鴕鳥 Ostriches at the Zoo

📍 生物課校外教學，莉莉和傑森在動物園聽導覽員介紹鴕鳥。

　　好大！不過牠們不會飛，對吧？

　　沒錯。不過牠們的腿非常有力。

　　牠們的腳看起來好奇怪，只有兩根腳趾。

　　對。牠們腳的構造讓牠們能以時速七十公里奔跑。

　　太驚人了！牠們的腿幾乎跟恐龍的很像。

　　鴕鳥害怕時真的會把頭埋進沙裡嗎？

## Scene 11｜照英國食譜烤司康 Baking from a British Recipe

📍 媽媽的英國朋友寄來一份司康食譜，莉莉和媽媽在廚房研究上面奇怪的單位。

本場單字：volume, differ, logic, trace, pub, scale, pour

**Lily**：Mom, this recipe says eight ounces of flour. How much is that?  
　　媽，這份食譜寫八盎司麵粉，那是多少？

**Mom**：About two hundred twenty-five grams. Britain still uses a mix of old and new units.  
　　大約兩百二十五克。英國到現在還混用新舊兩種單位。

**Lily**：And it says one cup of milk. Is a cup a weight?  
　　還寫了一杯牛奶。杯是重量單位嗎？

**Mom**：No, a cup measures **volume**, not weight.  
　　不是，杯是用來量容量的，不是重量。

**Lily**：Wait, do British and American cups **differ**?  
　　等等，英式和美式的杯子不一樣嗎？

**Mom**：Slightly. That's why I prefer grams.  
　　有一點點不同，所以我比較喜歡用克。

**Lily**：Why do these old units still exist? What's the **logic**?  
　　這些舊單位為什麼還存在？有什麼道理？

**Mom**：Habit, mostly. You can **trace** many of them back to ancient times.  
　　主要是習慣。很多單位都可以追溯到古代。

**Lily**：My English teacher said people in a British **pub** still order drinks by the pint.  
　　我英文老師說，在英國酒館大家還是用品脫點飲料。

**Mom**：That's true. OK, this recipe makes twenty scones. Let's **scale** it down by half.  
　　沒錯。好了，這份食譜可以做二十個司康，我們把份量減半吧。

**Lily**：So four ounces of flour and half a cup of milk.  
　　所以是四盎司麵粉和半杯牛奶。

**Mom**：Perfect. Now **pour** the milk in slowly while I stir.  
　　完美。現在我攪拌的時候，你把牛奶慢慢倒進去。

**Lily**：This is like a math class and a cooking class at the same time!  
　　這簡直同時在上數學課和烹飪課！

**Mom**：And the best part is, we get to eat our homework.  
　　而且最棒的是，我們的作業可以吃。

### 實用口語句型

- **by the pint**　以品脫為單位
- **scale it down by half**　把份量減半
- **at the same time**　同時
- **The best part is ...**　最棒的是……
