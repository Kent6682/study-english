## 二、課文（中英對照）

**¶1** Tom Holland is one of the most recognizable actors of his generation. Over the past decade, he has built a strong career through major film roles that have gained him millions of fans around the world. By 2019, he was included on the *Forbes* 30 Under 30 Europe list, a sign of his rising status and growing influence in the global film industry.
> 湯姆・霍蘭德是同世代最具辨識度的演員之一。過去十年來，他靠著多部重要的電影角色建立起穩固的演藝事業，這些角色為他贏得了全球數百萬名粉絲。到了 2019 年，他已入選《富比士》歐洲 30 位 30 歲以下傑出人物榜，這象徵他在全球電影產業的地位逐漸攀升、影響力日益擴大。

**¶2** The year 2021 marked a major turning point for Holland. He starred in *Spider-Man: No Way Home*, which became his biggest career success. The film's impact confirmed his place as a leading figure in big-budget cinema. That same year, he expanded his range in *Cherry*, playing a troubled man dealing with the emotional pain of war. He also appeared in the dystopian film *Chaos Walking*, taking on a complex and demanding part. These diverse performances called attention to his versatility and professional growth beyond superhero films.
> 2021 年是霍蘭德的一大轉捩點。他主演了《蜘蛛人：無家日》，這部片成為他演藝生涯中最大的成功。這部片的影響力確立了他在大製作電影界的領軍地位。同一年，他在《Cherry》中拓展戲路，飾演一名承受戰爭情緒創傷、內心困頓的男子。他也演出了反烏托邦電影《混沌漫步》，接下一個複雜又吃重的角色。這些多元的演出讓人注意到他的多才多藝，以及他在超級英雄電影之外的專業成長。

**¶3** 2026 is proving to be another significant year for Holland, owing to his roles in *The Odyssey* and his fourth Spider-Man film, *Spider-Man: Brand New Day*. Holland's back-to-back projects reflect his hard work and commitment to his craft.
> 2026 年看來又是霍蘭德意義重大的一年，這要歸功於他在《奧德賽》以及他第四部蜘蛛人電影《蜘蛛人：Brand New Day》中的角色。霍蘭德接連不斷的作品，反映出他的努力以及對演藝專業的投入。

**¶4** Tom Holland is known for his energetic, kind, and even goofy personality. He is often described as relatable and grounded. In addition, he has earned strong positive reactions for speaking honestly about his personal struggles. He has openly discussed his experiences with dyslexia and ADHD. As a child, he found spelling and reading difficult and sometimes frustrating, but he never allowed those challenges to define him. He often credits his parents for their help, patience, and constant encouragement during his early years.
> 湯姆・霍蘭德以充滿活力、善良，甚至有點傻氣的個性著稱。他常被形容為讓人有共鳴而且腳踏實地。此外，他因坦率談論自己的個人困境而贏得熱烈的正面迴響。他曾公開談論自己患有讀寫障礙和過動症的經歷。小時候，他覺得拼字和閱讀很困難，有時甚至令人沮喪，但他從不讓這些挑戰定義自己。他常把功勞歸於父母在他童年時期的幫助、耐心與不斷的鼓勵。

**¶5** Rather than seeing ADHD as a weakness, Holland views it as a source of creativity and imagination. He has explained that embracing his "inner child" helps him prepare for complex roles. This view has shaped his highly dynamic screen presence. Beyond acting, Holland is deeply involved in charity work to improve society and help those in need. In 2017, the Holland family founded The Brothers Trust, which supports health and social programs. Through this organization, he has helped raise funds for medical research for various conditions.
> 霍蘭德不把過動症視為弱點，反而把它看作創造力與想像力的來源。他曾解釋，擁抱自己的「內在小孩」有助於他為複雜的角色做準備。這種觀點塑造了他極具活力的銀幕魅力。除了演戲之外，霍蘭德也積極投入慈善工作，改善社會並幫助有需要的人。2017 年，霍蘭德一家創立了「兄弟信託基金會」（The Brothers Trust），支持健康與社會方面的計畫。透過這個組織，他已協助為各種疾病的醫學研究募集資金。

**¶6** Looking ahead, Holland's long-term ambition is to set up a free school in England that can compete with private schools in the country. Through both his career and charity work, he continues to show kindness and perseverance while remaining a lasting inspiration.
> 展望未來，霍蘭德的長期抱負是在英格蘭創辦一所能與國內私立學校競爭的免費學校。透過演藝事業與慈善工作，他持續展現善良與毅力，同時始終是激勵人心的榜樣。

## 四、Language Notes

### 1. N. + V-ing／p.p. 分詞片語
- 省略主格關代，再把動詞改分詞：主動／進行 ➔ V-ing，被動 ➔ p.p.
- *a troubled man (who was) dealing with the emotional pain of war*
- *The boy sitting by the window is my brother.*／*The car parked near the café belongs to Mia.*
- 句中 *playing ...* 是由 *and he played ...* 簡化的分詞構句。

### 2. take on 的不同意思
- (a) 承擔、接下（角色、任務）：*taking on a complex and demanding part*
- (b) 開始具有、呈現：*Andrew's voice suddenly took on a serious tone.*
- (c) 與……競爭：*Zoe trained hard before taking on the champion.*

### 3. set up 的用法
- (a) 創建、成立：*to set up a free school in England*
- (b) 安排、策劃：*Tricia is responsible for setting up the graduation party.*
- (c) 安裝、設置：*Caleb helped his brother set up the new sound system.*

### 4. remain 作連綴動詞
- remain + N.／Adj.／介系詞片語＝保持；仍然是
- *while remaining a lasting inspiration*／*Nora and Grace remain close friends.*／*Please remain quiet.*
