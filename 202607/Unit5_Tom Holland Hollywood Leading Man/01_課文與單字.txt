一、完整課文（中英對照，依段落）
================================================================

Tom Holland: Hollywood's Leading Man
湯姆・霍蘭德：好萊塢一線男星
—Taurean Dick

【第一部分】

¶1（L1–7）
Tom Holland is one of the most recognizable actors of his generation. Over the past decade, he has built a strong career through major film roles that have gained him millions of fans around the world. By 2019, he was included on the Forbes 30 Under 30 Europe list, a sign of his rising status and growing influence in the global film industry.
湯姆・霍蘭德是同世代最具辨識度的演員之一。過去十年來，他靠著多部重要的電影角色建立起穩固的演藝事業，這些角色為他贏得了全球數百萬名粉絲。到了 2019 年，他已入選《富比士》歐洲 30 位 30 歲以下傑出人物榜，這象徵他在全球電影產業的地位逐漸攀升、影響力日益擴大。

¶2（L8–21）
The year 2021 marked a major turning point for Holland. He starred in Spider-Man: No Way Home, which became his biggest career success. The film's impact confirmed his place as a leading figure in big-budget cinema. That same year, he expanded his range in Cherry, playing a troubled man dealing with the emotional pain of war. He also appeared in the dystopian film Chaos Walking, taking on a complex and demanding part. These diverse performances called attention to his versatility and professional growth beyond superhero films.
2021 年是霍蘭德的一大轉捩點。他主演了《蜘蛛人：無家日》，這部片成為他演藝生涯中最大的成功。這部片的影響力確立了他在大製作電影界的領軍地位。同一年，他在《Cherry》中拓展戲路，飾演一名承受戰爭情緒創傷、內心困頓的男子。他也演出了反烏托邦電影《混沌漫步》，接下一個複雜又吃重的角色。這些多元的演出讓人注意到他的多才多藝，以及他在超級英雄電影之外的專業成長。

¶3（L22–27）
2026 is proving to be another significant year for Holland, owing to his roles in The Odyssey and his fourth Spider-Man film, Spider-Man: Brand New Day. Holland's back-to-back projects reflect his hard work and commitment to his craft.
2026 年看來又是霍蘭德意義重大的一年，這要歸功於他在《奧德賽》以及他第四部蜘蛛人電影《蜘蛛人：Brand New Day》中的角色。霍蘭德接連不斷的作品，反映出他的努力以及對演藝專業的投入。

【第二部分】

¶4（L1–15）
Tom Holland is known for his energetic, kind, and even goofy personality. He is often described as relatable and grounded. In addition, he has earned strong positive reactions for speaking honestly about his personal struggles. He has openly discussed his experiences with dyslexia and ADHD. As a child, he found spelling and reading difficult and sometimes frustrating, but he never allowed those challenges to define him. He often credits his parents for their help, patience, and constant encouragement during his early years.
湯姆・霍蘭德以充滿活力、善良，甚至有點傻氣的個性著稱。他常被形容為讓人有共鳴而且腳踏實地。此外，他因坦率談論自己的個人困境而贏得熱烈的正面迴響。他曾公開談論自己患有讀寫障礙和過動症的經歷。小時候，他覺得拼字和閱讀很困難，有時甚至令人沮喪，但他從不讓這些挑戰定義自己。他常把功勞歸於父母在他童年時期的幫助、耐心與不斷的鼓勵。

¶5（L16–30）
Rather than seeing ADHD as a weakness, Holland views it as a source of creativity and imagination. He has explained that embracing his "inner child" helps him prepare for complex roles. This view has shaped his highly dynamic screen presence. Beyond acting, Holland is deeply involved in charity work to improve society and help those in need. In 2017, the Holland family founded The Brothers Trust, which supports health and social programs. Through this organization, he has helped raise funds for medical research for various conditions.
霍蘭德不把過動症視為弱點，反而把它看作創造力與想像力的來源。他曾解釋，擁抱自己的「內在小孩」有助於他為複雜的角色做準備。這種觀點塑造了他極具活力的銀幕魅力。除了演戲之外，霍蘭德也積極投入慈善工作，改善社會並幫助有需要的人。2017 年，霍蘭德一家創立了「兄弟信託基金會」（The Brothers Trust），支持健康與社會方面的計畫。透過這個組織，他已協助為各種疾病的醫學研究募集資金。

¶6（L31–37）
Looking ahead, Holland's long-term ambition is to set up a free school in England that can compete with private schools in the country. Through both his career and charity work, he continues to show kindness and perseverance while remaining a lasting inspiration.
展望未來，霍蘭德的長期抱負是在英格蘭創辦一所能與國內私立學校競爭的免費學校。透過演藝事業與慈善工作，他持續展現善良與毅力，同時始終是激勵人心的榜樣。

三、Language Notes 關鍵解析
================================================================

【第一部分】
1. N. + V-ing／p.p. 分詞片語
   形容詞子句可簡化成分詞片語來修飾先行詞：省略主格關係代名詞，再把動詞改為分詞。
   表主動或進行時用現在分詞（V-ing）；表被動時用過去分詞（p.p.）。
   ... playing a troubled man who was dealing with the emotional pain of war
   ➔ ... playing a troubled man dealing with the emotional pain of war
   • The boy sitting by the window is my brother.（坐在窗戶旁邊的男孩是我弟弟。）
   • The car parked near the café belongs to Mia.（停在咖啡館附近的那輛車是米雅的。）
   ※ 句中的 playing ... 是由 and he played ... 簡化而來的分詞構句。

2. take on 的不同意思
   (a) 承擔、接下（角色或任務）（文中用法）
       • Our team agreed to take on the project.（我們團隊同意承接這項專案。）
   (b) 開始具有、呈現（某種面貌或特質）
       • Andrew's voice suddenly took on a serious tone.（安德魯的聲音突然變得嚴肅起來。）
   (c) 與……競爭、較量
       • Zoe trained hard before taking on the champion.（柔伊在與冠軍較量之前努力訓練。）

【第二部分】
1. set up 的用法
   (a) 創建、成立（組織、公司等），作及物用，如文中用法
       • The artist set up a small studio in her garage.（那名藝術家在她的車庫裡設立了一間小工作室。）
   (b) 安排（會議）、策劃（活動）
       • Tricia is responsible for setting up the graduation party.（崔莎負責籌辦畢業派對。）
   (c) 安裝、裝配、設置（設備或機器）
       • Caleb helped his brother set up the new sound system.（迦勒幫弟弟安裝了新的音響系統。）

2. remain 作連綴動詞的用法
   remain 在文中作連綴動詞，表示「保持（某種狀態）；仍然是」，之後可接名詞（文中用法）、形容詞或介系詞片語。
   • In spite of the argument, Nora and Grace remain close friends.（儘管發生爭執，諾拉跟葛蕾絲依然是親密的朋友。）
   • Please remain quiet while the teacher is speaking.（老師說話時請保持安靜。）

四、Info Box 補充
================================================================

【第一部分】
• 標題 leading man：原指「男主角」，在此有雙關含義——leading 也有「最出色的、當紅的」之意，形容湯姆・霍蘭德是好萊塢炙手可熱的一線男星。
• Forbes：美國知名財經雜誌《富比士》；30 Under 30 為榜單名，表揚三十位年齡不滿三十歲的傑出領袖。
• big-budget（adj.）：高成本的；大製作的。
• range：在此指「戲路；演技範圍」。
• back-to-back（adj.）：連續進行的。

【第二部分】
• grounded（adj.）：踏實的、不浮誇的；心理與情緒穩定的。
• credit A for B：把 B 歸功於 A。
• inner child（內在小孩）：心理學家榮格（Carl Jung，1875–1961）提出的概念，指人們潛意識中保留的童年經驗、情緒與渴望。即使在成年後，內在小孩仍會影響一個人的行為、情緒反應與人際關係。
• screen presence：一個人在銀幕上的魅力或表現力。
• be involved in sth：積極參與、全心投入……。
• in need：需要幫助的狀態；貧困的、困難中的。
• looking ahead：可視為獨立分詞片語，表示「展望未來、放眼未來」。

五、課後練習（Reading Comprehension／Integrated Questions）

【第一部分 Reading Comprehension】
1. Which of the following is NOT true according to the first part?
   (A) Holland was already well known before 2021.
   (B) Spider-Man: No Way Home was a huge success for him.
   (C) His 2026 film projects may point to another strong year.
   (D) He entered the Forbes 30 Under 30 Europe list after Chaos Walking.
   Ans: (D)（2019 年就已入榜，Chaos Walking 是 2021 年的作品）
2. What does "turning point" on line 8 mean?
   (A) A steady rise  (B) A short break  (C) An early success  (D) An important change
   Ans: (D)

【第二部分 Integrated Questions】
1. In what order are the following ideas presented in the second part?
   A. Tom's future educational goal
   B. Tom's work to support people in need
   C. The challenges Tom faced while growing up
   D. How Tom's conditions have influenced his acting
   E. Tom Holland's public image and personal qualities
   Ans: E → C → D → B → A
2. Some of the sentences in the second part are numbered ① to ⑤. One of them expresses the writer's opinion rather than a fact. Write the number of the sentence.
   ① He has openly discussed his experiences with dyslexia and ADHD.
   ② He often credits his parents for their help, patience, and constant encouragement during his early years.
   ③ He has explained that embracing his "inner child" helps him prepare for complex roles.
   ④ In 2017, the Holland family founded The Brothers Trust, which supports health and social programs.
   ⑤ Through both his career and charity work, he continues to show kindness and perseverance while remaining a lasting inspiration.
   Ans: ⑤（kindness、perseverance、lasting inspiration 都是作者的主觀評價）
