【讀門秘笈 Reading Skill Builder】
Identifying Key Words —Harry Jenkins

In texts, there are certain words that provide important information. If we can spot these key words, we can read and understand a text more quickly. Doing this can also help us answer questions about a text. But what are some examples of key words?

1. Proper nouns are the names of places, people, companies, and more. They are always capitalized, which makes them stand out on the page.
2. Numbers also stand out. These could indicate dates or data and can help us understand which period of time is being discussed or an important fact.
3. Important verbs describe actions, so they can tell us a lot about events. They often carry the key information in a sentence, especially when we want to know who did what.
4. Repeated words are also worth noticing. When a writer uses the same word or phrase again and again, it is usually because the idea is important.
5. Signal words show how ideas are connected. Words like because, but, and however tell us whether a reason or an opposite idea is coming next.

Practice Time
If we want to find out when the scientist Karl von Frisch was rewarded for his work, which key words or phrases help us most? Underline them in the following paragraph.
In the 1940s, an Austrian scientist named Karl von Frisch published his study of the honeybee waggle dance. He noticed that when a bee finds food, it returns to the hive and moves in a figure-eight pattern. The angle of the dance shows the direction of the food, while the length of the waggle shows how far away it is. Because of this discovery, von Frisch shared the Nobel Prize in 1973.

【課文】Read the following article. Pay attention to the key words and consider how they provide us with important information.

第 1 段（第 01–10 行）
Many people use cotton swabs to clean their ears. Since the 1970s, however, doctors have warned against this habit. Cotton swabs can push wax deeper into the ear and even damage the eardrum. One study found that about 12,500 American children a year needed emergency care for these injuries.
很多人用棉花棒來清耳朵。然而，自 1970 年代起，醫生就一直告誡大家別這麼做。棉花棒可能會把耳垢推到耳朵更深處，甚至傷害耳膜。有一項研究發現，每年約有 12,500 名美國兒童因這類傷害而需要急診。

第 2 段（第 11–19 行）
Actually, earwax is not dirty at all. According to Dr. Marcella Bothwell, it “prevents harmful things like bugs, sand, and dirt from getting into our ears.” It also contains substances that fight infection. For most people, then, there is no health reason to remove it.
其實，耳垢一點也不髒。根據馬賽拉・博斯維爾醫師的說法，耳垢能「防止有害的東西，例如蟲子、沙子和灰塵，跑進耳朵裡」。耳垢還含有能對抗感染的物質。因此，對大多數人來說，沒有任何健康上的理由需要清除耳垢。

第 3 段（第 20–31 行）
Still, nobody wants dirty ears. If you want to wash them, the safest way is to use a warm, damp cloth on the outside only. When there is too much wax, ear drops can soften it. Never put sharp or small objects inside your ear. If your hearing gets worse or your ear hurts, see a doctor instead of removing the wax yourself. Your ears already clean themselves, so the best thing you can do is leave them alone.
不過，沒有人喜歡耳朵髒髒的。如果你想清洗耳朵，最安全的做法是用溫熱的濕布只擦外側。耳垢太多的時候，點耳劑可以把耳垢軟化。千萬不要把尖銳或細小的物品放進耳朵裡。如果你的聽力變差或耳朵痛，就去看醫生，而不是自己清耳垢。耳朵本身就會自我清潔，所以你能做的最好的事，就是別去動它。

【Language Notes 關鍵解析】
1. since 的用法
since 表示「自從」時，可作介系詞或連接詞使用。作介系詞時，後面接表示過去某時間點的名詞或名詞片語；作連接詞時，後面接子句。主要子句常使用現在完成式或現在完成進行式，表示從過去某個時間點一直持續到現在的狀態或動作。
句型：Since + N. ／ S + V-ed, S + have／has + p.p.／been V-ing
‧Since Monday, I have been feeling much better.（從星期一開始，我就一直感覺好多了。）
‧Since we arrived in Egypt, we have visited many interesting places.（自從抵達埃及後，我們參觀了許多有趣的地方。）
‧課文：Since the 1970s, however, doctors have warned against this habit.

2. if 引導條件副詞子句
if 可引導條件副詞子句，用來表示現在或未來可能發生的情況。表示未來情況時，if 子句通常使用現在式，而不用未來式。
句型：If S + V., 祈使句（文中用法）／ If S + V., S + will／may／can + V.
‧If you don't understand the question, ask for help.（如果你不明白這個問題，就尋求協助。）
‧If Evan finishes his homework early, he can play video games.（如果埃文早點完成作業，他就可以玩電動遊戲。）
‧課文：If your hearing gets worse or your ear hurts, see a doctor instead of removing the wax yourself.
