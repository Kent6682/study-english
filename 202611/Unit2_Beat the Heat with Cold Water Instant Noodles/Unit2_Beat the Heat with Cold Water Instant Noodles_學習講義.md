## 二、課文（中英對照，克漏字已填入正解）

**¶1** What if you could enjoy delicious instant noodles without using hot water? It may sound impossible, but one Japanese company has turned this concept into reality. Nissin has introduced the Hiyashi Cup Noodle—*hiyashi* means "cold" in Japanese. **Rather than** boiling water, all you need is cold water straight from the refrigerator.
> 如果不用熱水也能享用美味的泡麵，那會怎麼樣？這聽起來或許不可能，但有一家日本公司已經把這個概念化為現實。日清推出了「冷水杯麵」——hiyashi 在日文裡是「冰涼」的意思。不用滾燙的開水，你只需要從冰箱直接拿出來的冷水。

**¶2** Making it couldn't be easier. Simply pour the cold water into the cup, wait five minutes, and the noodles are ready to eat. To make this possible, Nissin spent five years developing a method that allows the noodles to soften without heat. Even the small pieces of meat and vegetables turn out **tender**. The specially made noodles have a chewy and smooth texture, which **stays** firm even after soaking in cold water.
> 做法再簡單不過了。只要把冷水倒進杯裡，等五分鐘，麵就可以吃了。為了讓這件事成真，日清花了五年研發出一種方法，讓麵條不必加熱就能變軟。就連小塊的肉和蔬菜也會變得軟嫩。這種特製麵條口感有嚼勁又滑順，即使泡在冷水裡也能保持紮實。

**¶3** The noodles **come in** two flavors: Spicy Kimchi and Chicken Salt Lemon. Reporters who tried them said the five-minute wait was well **worth it**, since the noodles tasted just like the original—proof that sometimes unusual ideas turn out to be the tastiest.
> 這款麵有兩種口味：香辣泡菜和雞肉鹽味檸檬。試吃過的記者表示，等那五分鐘非常值得，因為麵條吃起來就和原本的杯麵一樣——這證明了不尋常的點子有時反而最美味。

| 題 | 答案 | 考點 | 秒殺關鍵 |

## 五、關鍵句型

1. **What if ...?** 如果……會怎樣？
2. **turn A into B** 把 A 變成 B：turn this concept into reality
3. **Rather than + N./V-ing** 不……而是……（≒ instead of）
4. **All you need is + N.** 你只需要……（you need 前省略 that）
5. **couldn't be + 比較級** 再……不過了
6. **spend + 時間 + V-ing／allow + O + to V**
7. **turn out + adj.／turn out to be** 結果是……
8. **, which + 單數動詞**（先行詞 texture 單數）
9. **come in + 口味／尺寸**
10. **be (well) worth it** 非常值得
11. **N., proof that + 完整子句** 同位語
