Unit 2｜Beat the Heat with Cold Water Instant Noodles
冷水泡麵，消暑新吃法
飲食科技 ★★★（克漏字）｜—Kathy Chapman

================================================================
一、完整課文（中英對照，依段落；克漏字空格已填入正解並以【】標示）
================================================================

¶1（L1–6）
What if you could enjoy delicious instant noodles without using hot water? It may sound impossible, but one Japanese company has turned this concept into reality. Nissin has introduced the Hiyashi Cup Noodle—hiyashi means "cold" in Japanese. 【Rather than】(1) boiling water, all you need is cold water straight from the refrigerator.
如果不用熱水也能享用美味的泡麵，那會怎麼樣？這聽起來或許不可能，但有一家日本公司已經把這個概念化為現實。日清推出了「冷水杯麵」——hiyashi 在日文裡是「冰涼」的意思。不用滾燙的開水，你只需要從冰箱直接拿出來的冷水。

¶2（L7–14）
Making it couldn't be easier. Simply pour the cold water into the cup, wait five minutes, and the noodles are ready to eat. To make this possible, Nissin spent five years developing a method that allows the noodles to soften without heat. Even the small pieces of meat and vegetables turn out 【tender】(2). The specially made noodles have a chewy and smooth texture, which 【stays】(3) firm even after soaking in cold water.
做法再簡單不過了。只要把冷水倒進杯裡，等五分鐘，麵就可以吃了。為了讓這件事成真，日清花了五年研發出一種方法，讓麵條不必加熱就能變軟。就連小塊的肉和蔬菜也會變得軟嫩。這種特製麵條口感有嚼勁又滑順，即使泡在冷水裡也能保持紮實。

¶3（L15–19）
The noodles 【come in】(4) two flavors: Spicy Kimchi and Chicken Salt Lemon. Reporters who tried them said the five-minute wait was well 【worth it】(5), since the noodles tasted just like the original—proof that sometimes unusual ideas turn out to be the tastiest.
這款麵有兩種口味：香辣泡菜和雞肉鹽味檸檬。試吃過的記者表示，等那五分鐘非常值得，因為麵條吃起來就和原本的杯麵一樣——這證明了不尋常的點子有時反而最美味。

二、原題：克漏字（Cloze）
================================================================
1. (A) In spite of  (B) Apart from  (C) Rather than  (D) With regard to
2. (A) nutritious  (B) tender  (C) crispy  (D) sweet
3. (A) stay  (B) stays  (C) staying  (D) has stayed
4. (A) come in  (B) give off  (C) bring out  (D) serve up
5. (A) over it  (B) above it  (C) into it  (D) worth it

答案：1. C　2. B　3. B　4. A　5. D

三、Vocabulary 字彙（* 為大考出現過的字彙）
================================================================
*1. concept [ˋkɑnsɛpt] n. 概念、想法
    The concept of a car without a driver once seemed strange.
*2. pour [pɔr] v. 倒、傾倒（液體）；大量湧出
    Be careful when you pour the hot tea.
*3. original [əˋrɪdʒənl] n. 原作、真品（在此指「原本的杯麵」）
    The cover song sounds very different from the original.
*4. proof [pruf] n. 證明；證據
    Emily's good grades are proof of her hard work.

五、關鍵句型速查（本講義整理）
================================================================
1. What if S + could/過去式 ...?　如果……會怎樣？（假設情境提問）
2. turn A into B　把 A 變成 B（turn a concept into reality 化概念為現實）
3. Rather than + N./V-ing, S + V ...　不……而是……（克漏字第 1 題）
4. All you need is + N.　你只需要……（you need 前省略關代 that）
5. S + couldn't be + 比較級　再……不過了（否定＋比較級＝最高級）
6. spend + 時間 + V-ing；allow + O + to V
7. turn out + adj.／turn out to be + N./adj.　結果是……
8. 非限定 which 指單數名詞 → 動詞用單數（克漏字第 3 題）
9. come in + 口味／尺寸／顏色　有……可供選擇（克漏字第 4 題）
10. be (well) worth it　（非常）值得（克漏字第 5 題）
11. N., proof that + 子句　同位語：證明……
