> 大師名作選｜希臘神話｜Synopsis by Ami Barnes

## 課文

**¶1** Before Perseus was born, his grandfather, King Acrisius, was already terrified of him. An oracle had **warned** that one day, his grandson would kill him. To *stop the prophecy from coming true*, Acrisius locked his daughter, Danaë, in a tower. But the god Zeus found a way in, and Danaë became **pregnant** with his child. She later gave birth to a half-god, half-human boy named Perseus. **Furious**, Acrisius shut mother and son inside a wooden box and threw it into the sea. Waves carried them to *the island of Seriphos, where* a fisherman found them and took them in.

**¶2** Years later, the island's king wanted to marry Danaë, but Perseus refused to allow it. So, the king sent Perseus to a distant cave, home to three terrible sisters known as the Gorgons. One of them, Medusa, had snakes *instead of* hair. The king challenged Perseus to bring back Medusa's head, but he knew the task was impossible—Medusa's **gaze** alone was enough to turn a person into stone. Luckily for Perseus, the goddess Athena stepped in to help, giving him a **shiny** shield, a sword, and a pair of winged shoes.

**¶3** Perseus flew across the sea to find Medusa. He **crept** into her cave while she slept and used the shield as a mirror to avoid looking at her directly. With one **swift** movement, he raised the sword and cut off her head. Then he stuffed her head—snakes and all—into a magic bag. The winged shoes *carried him away* before Medusa's sisters woke.

**¶4** On his journey home, Perseus spotted Andromeda, a beautiful young woman chained to a rock and offered up as a **sacrifice** to a sea monster. He swooped down, pulled Medusa's head from the bag, and instantly **transformed** the monster to stone. Perseus then returned to Seriphos, bringing Andromeda home as his wife.

**¶5** Back on the island, Perseus turned the **wicked** king to stone and set his mother free. He gave Medusa's head to *Athena, who* placed it on her shield to frighten off enemies. But fate wasn't finished with Perseus yet. Sometime later, while attending an athletics event in Larissa, he threw a discus that veered off course, striking and killing an old man in the crowd.

**¶6** Who do you think that man *might have been*?

## 核心單字

補充：terrify 使害怕｜oracle 神諭｜prophecy 預言｜shield 盾牌｜swoop 俯衝｜athletics 田徑運動｜discus 鐵餅｜veer 轉向

## 必考句型

1. **stop/prevent/keep sb/sth from V-ing**：To stop the prophecy from coming true, ...
2. **, where＋完整句**（非限定，補充地點）：the island of Seriphos, where a fisherman found them
3. **instead of＋N./V-ing**：Medusa had snakes instead of hair.
4. **carry sb away**（帶走；get carried away 忘我）：The winged shoes carried him away.
5. **may/might have p.p.**（推測過去）：Who do you think that man might have been?

## 閱讀測驗

1. Why did Acrisius cast Danae and Perseus away? → **(D)** 他害怕外孫會殺了他
2. How did Perseus rescue Andromeda? → **(C)** 用梅杜莎的頭把海怪變成石頭

> 結局謎底：那位老人就是阿克里西俄斯——想盡辦法躲避預言，預言還是成真。
