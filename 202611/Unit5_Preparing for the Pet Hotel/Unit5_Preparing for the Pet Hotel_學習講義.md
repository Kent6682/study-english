## 二、課文（中英對照）

### 場景 1：The Pet Is the Boss 寵物才是老大
*Ellen asks George to join a weekend trip to Tainan.*

**E:** Are you going to come to Tainan with us for the **long weekend**? We're leaving early on Saturday morning.
> 你要跟我們一起去台南過連假嗎？我們星期六一大早就出發。

**G:** I don't think so. Max hates being alone, even for one night.
> 應該不去了。麥斯很討厭獨處，就算只有一個晚上也不行。

**E:** Can't a friend **look after** him and take him for a walk?
> 不能請朋友照顧他、帶他去散步嗎？

**G:** He probably wouldn't let them. He's **used to** a walk at six and having dinner at seven, or he gets anxious and barks **throughout** the night.
> 他大概不會讓他們這麼做。他習慣六點散步、七點吃晚餐，不然他會焦躁不安，整晚吠個不停。

**E:** That's not a dog's **routine**! He sounds like a manager!
> 那根本不是狗的作息！他聽起來像個經理！

**G:** Exactly, and I follow it every day!
> 一點也沒錯，而且我每天都照著做！

**E:** How about booking a pet hotel? Max can also have his own vacation.
> 要不要訂寵物旅館？麥斯也可以有他自己的假期。

**G:** That sounds like a good idea.
> 聽起來是個好主意。

### 場景 2：Packing for a Pet 幫寵物打包行李
*George is preparing everything Max may need at the pet hotel.*

**E:** Did you book a pet hotel for Max?
> 你幫麥斯訂好寵物旅館了嗎？

**G:** Yes, but I still need to pack a few things to help him feel at home.
> 訂了，不過我還得打包幾樣東西，讓他住得像在家一樣自在。

**E:** What does he need for only a couple of nights away?
> 只是在外面住幾晚，他需要什麼？

**G:** His **vaccination** and **deworming** record, **along with** his blanket, favorite toy, special food, water bowl, and leash for his evening walk.
> 他的疫苗接種和驅蟲紀錄，還有他的毯子、最喜歡的玩具、特製飼料、水碗，以及傍晚散步用的牽繩。

**E:** It sounds like he'll have more things packed for the weekend than I do.
> 聽起來他這個週末要打包的東西比我還多。

**G:** Max likes **familiar** things. Without them, I'm worried he'd refuse to eat or sleep.
> 麥斯喜歡熟悉的東西。沒有那些東西，我擔心他會不吃不睡。

**E:** You take such good care of him! He's a very **fortunate** dog.
> 你把他照顧得這麼好！他真是一隻很幸運的狗。

**G:** I **do my best**. I hope he'll be all right.
> 我盡力而為。希望他一切都好。

## 四、Info Box 與必考句型

| 句型 | 意思 | 課文 |
|---|---|---|
| long weekend | 連假 | for the long weekend |
| look after N | 照顧、看管 | Can't a friend look after him? |
| be used to + N／V-ing | 習慣於 | He's used to a walk at six and having dinner at seven |
| along with + N | 以及、連同 | along with his blanket, favorite toy ... |
| do one's best | 盡力而為 | I do my best. |
| How about + V-ing? | ……如何？ | How about booking a pet hotel? |
| sound like + N／子句 | 聽起來像 | He sounds like a manager! |
| have + O + p.p. | 把某物處理好 | he'll have more things packed |

**防錯三連：** be used to + V-ing（習慣）／used to + V（過去常常）／be used to + V（被用來）
