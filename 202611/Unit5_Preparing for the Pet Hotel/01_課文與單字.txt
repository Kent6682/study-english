Unit 5｜Preparing for the Pet Hotel
寵物旅館入住準備
生活情境對話 ★☆☆｜主題圖解：Our Pets and Pet Supplies 寵物與寵物用品

================================================================
一、完整課文（中英對照，依場景；行號依原文）
================================================================
角色：E = Ellen（艾倫）　G = George（喬治）

【場景 1】The Pet Is the Boss 寵物才是老大
Ellen asks George to join a weekend trip to Tainan.（艾倫邀喬治參加週末的台南之旅。）

L4  E: Are you going to come to Tainan with us for the long weekend? We're leaving early on Saturday morning.
       你要跟我們一起去台南過連假嗎？我們星期六一大早就出發。
L7  G: I don't think so. Max hates being alone, even for one night.
       應該不去了。麥斯很討厭獨處，就算只有一個晚上也不行。
L9  E: Can't a friend look after him and take him for a walk?
       不能請朋友照顧他、帶他去散步嗎？
L11 G: He probably wouldn't let them. He's used to a walk at six and having dinner at seven, or he gets anxious and barks throughout the night.
       他大概不會讓他們這麼做。他習慣六點散步、七點吃晚餐，不然他會焦躁不安，整晚吠個不停。
L15 E: That's not a dog's routine! He sounds like a manager!
       那根本不是狗的作息！他聽起來像個經理！
L17 G: Exactly, and I follow it every day!
       一點也沒錯，而且我每天都照著做！
L18 E: How about booking a pet hotel? Max can also have his own vacation.
       要不要訂寵物旅館？麥斯也可以有他自己的假期。
L20 G: That sounds like a good idea.
       聽起來是個好主意。

【場景 2】Packing for a Pet 幫寵物打包行李
George is preparing everything Max may need at the pet hotel.（喬治正在準備麥斯在寵物旅館可能需要的所有東西。）

L25 E: Did you book a pet hotel for Max?
       你幫麥斯訂好寵物旅館了嗎？
L26 G: Yes, but I still need to pack a few things to help him feel at home.
       訂了，不過我還得打包幾樣東西，讓他住得像在家一樣自在。
L28 E: What does he need for only a couple of nights away?
       只是在外面住幾晚，他需要什麼？
L30 G: His vaccination and deworming record, along with his blanket, favorite toy, special food, water bowl, and leash for his evening walk.
       他的疫苗接種和驅蟲紀錄，還有他的毯子、最喜歡的玩具、特製飼料、水碗，以及傍晚散步用的牽繩。
L34 E: It sounds like he'll have more things packed for the weekend than I do.
       聽起來他這個週末要打包的東西比我還多。
L36 G: Max likes familiar things. Without them, I'm worried he'd refuse to eat or sleep.
       麥斯喜歡熟悉的東西。沒有那些東西，我擔心他會不吃不睡。
L38 E: You take such good care of him! He's a very fortunate dog.
       你把他照顧得這麼好！他真是一隻很幸運的狗。
L40 G: I do my best. I hope he'll be all right.
       我盡力而為。希望他一切都好。

================================================================
二、Vocabulary 字彙（* 為大考出現過的字彙）
================================================================
*1. throughout [θruˋaʊt] prep. 自始至終；遍及、遍布
    The baby slept throughout the afternoon.（寶寶整個下午都在睡。）
*2. routine [ruˋtin] n. 作息、例行事項；常規、慣例
    William's morning routine starts with a shower.（威廉的晨間例行公事從沖澡開始。）
*3. familiar [fəˋmɪljɚ] adj. 熟悉的
    Elizabeth saw a familiar face in the crowd.（伊莉莎白在人群中看到一張熟悉的面孔。）
*4. fortunate [ˋfɔrtʃənət] adj. 幸運的
    We were fortunate to have good weather for the trip.（我們很幸運，旅途中天氣都很好。）

字彙小筆記（僅供參考）：
•* vaccination [͵væksəˋneʃən] n. 疫苗接種
•  deworm [diˋwɝm] v. 驅除寄生蟲（對話中 deworming 為動名詞，表示「驅蟲、除蟲」，和前面的 vaccination 並列，修飾後面的 record）

================================================================
三、Info Box 關鍵解析
================================================================
1. 第 5 行 long weekend：字面意思是「長週末」，通常指「包含週末並延長一天或數天的連假」。
2. 第 9 行 look after N.：指「照顧、看管……」。
3. 第 31 行 片語介系詞 along with：指「以及、連同……一起」。
4. 第 40 行 do one's best：指「竭盡所能、盡力而為」。

補充句型（課文出現）：
• be used to + N／V-ing：習慣於……（He's used to a walk at six and having dinner at seven）
• take sb for a walk：帶某人／某動物去散步
• sound like + N：聽起來像……（He sounds like a manager!／That sounds like a good idea.）
• How about + V-ing?：……如何？（How about booking a pet hotel?）
• help sb (to) V／feel at home：幫助某人……／感到自在
• have sth + p.p.：把某物（被）處理好（he'll have more things packed）
• take good care of sb：好好照顧某人（You take such good care of him!）
